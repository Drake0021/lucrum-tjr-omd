## Summary
TJR defines "order flow" as simply the trend (HH/HL = bullish, LL/LH = bearish, plus consolidation), identified from the most recent break of structure / most recent highs and lows. He teaches three ways order flow is **disrespected** (BOS, inverse FVG, push past the 79% Fib extension) and that order flow is **respected** when price retraces into FVG / equilibrium / order block / breaker block and continues. His execution: on the 5m, wait for the old order flow to be disrespected (5m BOS or IFVG), then for the new order flow to be respected (retrace into a 5m FVG/EQ/OB/breaker), then drop to the 1m for a BOS/IFVG in the trade direction and enter, targeting prior draws on liquidity. For daily bias he reads where price sits in the higher-timeframe trend (extension to external liquidity vs. retracement into internal liquidity / imbalance) — he equates "internal/external liquidity" with order flow. Early-2025 Trading Transformation era video.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Order flow** — "literally just trends": are we in an uptrend or a downtrend. Identified by "the most recent break of structure" or the most recent highs and lows. [01:07–01:44]
- **Bullish order flow** — higher highs and higher lows; **bearish order flow** — lower lows and lower highs. [02:19–02:54]
- **Three types of order flow** — uptrend, downtrend, consolidation. [19:48]
- **Disrespecting bullish order flow** (three confluences) [03:28]:
  1. **BOS** — in an uptrend, a candle **closes below the most recent low**. [04:39]
  2. **Inverse FVG** — a bullish FVG gets **closed below** by a candle, "causing an inverse fair value gap". Logic: if order flow were respected price would enter the FVG and move up off it. [05:12–05:47]
  3. **79% Fib extension** — draw the Fib like equilibrium over the up-leg; if price "moves past the 79% extension" bullish order flow is disrespected and price will "eventually" break structure or inverse an FVG below. He "rarely" uses it; "you guys can very well do without this". [05:47–06:21]
- **Disrespecting bearish order flow** — mirror: close above the most recent high in the downtrend; a bearish FVG closed above by a candle (IFVG); price pushing past the 79% of the high→low leg. [06:54–07:32]
- **Respecting order flow** — in an uptrend, price retraces and "hits equilibrium, fills fair value gaps, fills order blocks and breaker blocks" and then continues. Confluence list: FVG, order block, breaker block, equilibrium ("OTE" mentioned as unnecessary: "we don't need to do that"). [09:36–10:08]
- **High-timeframe order flow / internal vs external liquidity** — HTF trends move in HH/HL or LL/LH; daily-bias job = identify "what point in time are we in these high time frame trends": sweeping hourly/LTF liquidity to go fill a 4H imbalance and then move higher, or in a 4H imbalance about to sweep LTF liquidity and move higher. "Internal and external liquidity ... is literally just order flow." [18:05–19:48; 23:46]
- **Change of order flow requires a liquidity sweep** — "in order to change order flow — liquidity sweep". [27:15]
- **5-minute retrace** — a 5m retrace "typically makes the one minute order flow turn into the other direction" (1m goes bearish during a bullish 5m pullback). [15:17]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Execution TF = 5m order flow "dictates like everything" for where he trades; scale down to 1m for the entry. [12:25–13:00]
- Core rule: "I look for the previous order flow to get disrespected, then I look for new order flow to get respected." [14:09–14:42]
- Step 1 (5m): previous order flow disrespected via 5m BOS (or IFVG) in the new direction. [14:09]
- Step 2 (5m): price retraces into the new 5m confluences (FVG, EQ, breaker, order block). [14:42]
- Step 3 (1m): typically the 1m has turned against (pullback); wait for a 1m BOS or 1m IFVG back in the 5m direction → take the trade. If the 1m didn't flip against first, "it doesn't really matter and we can take the trade regardless". [15:17–15:52]
- He does **not** require 1m order flow to be respected (no waiting for a 1m FVG/EQ retest after the 1m BOS) — 1m BOS/IFVG off a 5m confluence in 5m bullish order flow is sufficient. [15:52–16:59]
- Targets: "previous draws on liquidity", fair value gaps; in a trend, the prior highs are the take-profits. [16:59; 26:03]
- Time filter: don't act on the first 5m BOS ~5 min after the open; "wait for market to move into the macro, move into an optimal time to trade ... at around 9:50". [13:00]
- Don't short into a HTF imbalance being filled (HTF bullish order flow context) even if 5m broke down early. [13:34]
- Daily bias: on daily/4H, determine if the next move is the extension (to external liquidity/prior high) or the retracement (higher low into FVG/EQ/OB/breaker, internal liquidity). After the extension comes the higher low; after the higher low comes the extension. [19:14–21:32; 23:46; 25:30–26:03]
- After a HTF trend change (e.g., 4H BOS down), look for retrace into FVG and target the low. [28:22]

## Process / order of operations
1. HTF (daily/4H): label order flow (uptrend/downtrend/consolidation) by most recent BOS / swings. [19:48–20:22]
2. Determine the phase: extension to take external liquidity vs. retrace to fill HTF FVG/EQ/OB/breaker (internal). This is the daily bias. [23:46–24:21]
3. Wait until ~9:50 ET ("the macro"). [13:00]
4. 5m: previous order flow disrespected (BOS/IFVG, rarely 79% Fib). [14:09]
5. 5m: new order flow respected — retrace into 5m FVG/EQ/breaker/OB. [14:42]
6. 1m: BOS or IFVG in trade direction → enter. [15:17–16:26]
7. TP at previous draws on liquidity / FVGs. [16:59]

## Confluences mentioned
- BOS (candle close beyond most recent swing) — primary disrespect signal. [04:39]
- Inverse FVG — disrespect signal, equal standing with BOS; also 1m trigger. [05:12; 15:52]
- 79% Fib extension — rarely used, optional ("can very well do without"). [06:21]
- FVG, equilibrium, order block, breaker block — "respect" (retrace) zones; "this is all you need". [09:36–10:08]
- OTE — dismissed ("we don't need to do that"). [09:36] (inferred: "OT" = OTE)
- 4H order block at equilibrium + breaker in the same area — HTF retrace confluence for daily bias. [20:57]
- Liquidity sweep — required to change order flow. [26:03; 27:15]
