## Summary
A pre-market bias for Thu 2024-01-25, a heavy news day. Pre-market: Advance GDP, GDP price index, unemployment claims, core/durable goods. New home sales comes 30 minutes into the open. He calls it "a high-risk day to be trading" and says he should not risk "a lot of bread". ES: the daily is bullish toward ATH, the hourly swept lows, and a 4H sweep + BOS up has formed. He wants a dip into the hourly FVG/order block and a bullish reaction. If the open rips up without an entry, or sweeps the highs and turns bearish, he takes **no trade** (no counter-bias short). NQ: the 4H filled an imbalance / the "leg down that caused this move up" (OB) and is reacting up. Important rules: he does not trade a level just because it is high-confluence, and he will not wait into the PM session; he trades only within his allocated hours.
