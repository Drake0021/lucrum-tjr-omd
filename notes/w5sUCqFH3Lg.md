## Summary
Recorded on a CPI day, this video covers TJR's news filter. He uses ForexFactory with yellow (low-impact) folders hidden and only the currencies he trades shown (USD for S&P 500/gold/GU, GBP, JPY). He never trades CPI, PPI, FOMC or NFP days. For other red or orange folder events, he waits until the release and then lets price develop for 15–30 minutes. If news causes large wicks or big moves, he doesn't trade. If not, he trades defensively at reduced risk. He then explains how to read actual vs forecast and the "usual effect" to get a news bias, including the rule that good for USD means bad for SPX, gold, GU and BTC, and the fraction (top/bottom of the pair) rule. News bias is never the day's bias, because price action is superior.

## Concepts & definitions
- **ForexFactory setup** — Hide yellow folders (low-impact, "random stupid news"). Keep gray folders (bank holidays and non-trading days). Filter to the currencies you trade. S&P 500 news falls under USD. His set: GBP (GBPUSD, GBPJPY), JPY (GBPJPY), USD (GBPUSD, gold, S&P 500). Times display in your local time zone. [01:42], [02:20], [02:55], [03:28], [04:02]
- **Red / orange folders** — High- and medium-impact news. Always wait for the release. [03:28], [05:41]
- **Forecast vs actual** — Forecast is the consensus prediction, and actual is the released number (blank until release). Open the folder for details. The only field he cares about is "Usual Effect", for example "Actual > Forecast = good for currency". [10:14], [10:47], [11:21], [11:54]
- **"Good for currency" ≠ good for S&P 500** — Good for USD means DXY up, which means anything priced against the dollar falls: SPX/ES, gold, GBPUSD, EURUSD, AUDUSD, BTC/USD. Bad for USD means the reverse. [12:29], [13:01], [13:37], [14:11]
- **Fraction rule for pairs** — A pair is a fraction: the top (base) currency has positive correlation and the bottom (quote) is inverse. Good news for the top means up. Bad news for the bottom means up. Good news for the bottom means down. Bad news for the top means down. For example, USD good means GBPUSD down but USDJPY up. [16:57]–[21:58]
- **News bias** — The direction implied by actual vs forecast. It is not the day's bias. It "just helps us understand news data". [11:21], [22:30]
- **Price action > news bias** — On the CPI day (actual below forecast, so bad for USD), DXY dumped on the release candle and S&P rallied, but the dollar then rallied and "ate up" the news drop. So wait for news and follow price. [14:44], [15:17], [15:50]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Never trade CPI, PPI, FOMC or NFP (anything "non-farm") days: "just call it quits, sleep in". [06:49], [07:22], [22:30]
- On any red-folder day: don't trade before the release. Wait for it, then let price develop about 15–20 minutes (he also says 15–30). For example, an 8:30 release means re-evaluate around 8:45–8:50 ET. [04:02], [05:08], [05:41], [10:14]
- If news drastically moves the market (large wicks, large moves), don't trade that day. [06:16], [08:31]
- If news didn't move the market much: you may look for a trade, but play defensively. Treat it as a lower-probability day, reduce risk, and only take a great opportunity. [06:16]
- Days with many red/orange USD folders: treat as low probability and de-risk. Consider trading only a non-affected pair (for example GBPJPY when all the news is USD). [07:57], [08:31]
- News 30 minutes after the open (open 9:30 ET, news at 10:00 ET): don't trade before 10:00, and still don't trade until about 10:30 ET to let price develop. [08:31], [09:07]
- On a news-heavy week, don't try to make a lot of money. "Look for a reason not to trade, not a reason to trade." [09:07], [09:41]
- Check ForexFactory every morning before trading so a release minutes later doesn't stop you out. [24:09]
- (From the following video's example, qhuFg_OXdmE: GBPJPY moved only 34 pips since the US open before PPI/FOMC, so "you should not be trading today".)

## Process / order of operations
1. Each morning open ForexFactory, filtered to your currencies with yellow folders hidden.
2. If CPI/PPI/FOMC/NFP is on: no trading.
3. If other red or orange folders: no trades before the release. After it, wait 15–30 minutes.
4. Assess the reaction: big wicks or moves mean done for the day. Muted reaction means you may trade defensively with reduced risk.
5. Optionally read the news bias (actual vs forecast, usual effect, fraction rule), but defer to price action.
