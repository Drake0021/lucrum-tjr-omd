## Summary
A short ES recap with two trades: a −$5,000 long and a profitable short taken right after it, netting +$7,370 per the title. The HTF bias was long, toward an unfilled new-week opening gap (also a new-day opening gap) on ES and NQ. He longed after a sweep of pre-market lows into a 5m order block/breaker with what he thought was an IFVG. The gap had not actually been inverted (the candle closed exactly at the gap edge), and he missed a 5m SMT pointing down. After the stop-out he shorted on the 5m SMT + 1m BOS + 1m FVG fill. He took partials and then the full exit at the previous day's low, which he says was the "much easier draw on liquidity". The lesson: don't marry your bias; let the market show you its draw.

## Concepts & definitions
- **New week opening gap (NWOG) / new day opening gap** — "super high time frame draws on liquidity"; the gap "most definitely needs to be filled completely". This was the reason for his long bias [00:32].
- **Easier draw on liquidity** — the previous-day low was closer and "much easier" to hit than the NWOG above, so it made "so much more sense for price to want to go down and get there" [01:07–01:41].
- **Inverse FVG (IFVG) validity** — a gap counts as inverted only when a candle *closes through* it. A close "perfectly equal with the gap" is not an inversion [02:14–02:47].
- **5m SMT divergence** — on the short: one index made a high, the other a lower high ("high right here, lower high right here, SMT divergence"). He wrongly assumed "NASDAQ is lagging and will come up above these highs" [03:55–04:29].
- **Breaker / order block** — he lists a 1m OB, a 5m OB and a 5m breaker as confluences for the losing long [02:14].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Don't enter on an IFVG unless the candle body actually closes beyond the gap; an equal close is no inversion [02:47].
- Check the 5m SMT between ES and NQ before entering; an opposing SMT is a confluence *against* the trade [02:47–03:22].
- When a nearer HTF draw (previous-day low) sits opposite the bias draw (NWOG), expect price to take the easier one first (inferred from [01:07–01:41]).
- Short sequence used: 5m SMT (lower high on one index) → 1m BOS down → retrace into 1m FVG → enter on the confirming candle [05:05].
- TPs: partials at an intermediate level (inferred), full exit at the previous-day low [05:05].
