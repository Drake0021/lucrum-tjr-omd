## Summary
TJR's first YouTube video (Sept 2022): S&P 500 weekly/daily/monthly analysis plus a recap of two same-day trades (one −1% loss, one large win). HTF: weekly bearish (lower lows/lower highs), next draws at the weekly low and a weekly FVG; on the daily/4H he prefers the daily FVG that has lots of unswept highs (liquidity) beneath it over a closer FVG with little liquidity. Entry idea: "sweep of liquidity into a fair value gap, break of structure into another fair value gap" — the "perfect trade entry". The loss came from shorting after a buy-side sweep while ignoring that sell-side had also been swept (setting up a run on the London high). The win came from shorting a 1m FVG after price pushed into a 15m FVG and swept buy-side liquidity. Management: 50% off at TP1, stop to BE.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Draw on liquidity** — on the weekly, the next lower low / weekly low is "our next draw on price… the next draw on liquidity"; FVGs are also price points. [01:41]–[02:15]
- **FVG strength depends on liquidity around it** — an FVG with "not much liquidity to be swept" near it is a weak target; a daily FVG with many unswept highs below it ("liquidity, liquidity, liquidity…") is a strong draw. [03:24]–[06:14]
- **Wick-filled FVG** — FVGs already "wick filled" are no longer targets; "this fair value gap was already filled in with this high, so this is the next one that we're going to target". [03:24]; [07:24]
- **Support-turned-resistance** — visible as extra confluence, but "that's not how I trade". [07:24]
- **Monthly structure** — S&P monthly is bearish (first since 2008); "this low wasn't even broken, it was just swept" (Covid low). Notes a 23% drop so far vs 57% in 2008; "gaps get filled". [09:36]–[11:48]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- **Preferred entry:** "I like to see a sweep of liquidity along with it going into a fair value gap and then I'll enter off a break of structure into another fair value gap." [04:32]
- **Perfect trade entry:** "broke market structure after the sweep into a fair value gap — sweep of liquidity, fair value gap, break of structure into a fair value gap — that is the perfect trade entry." [16:16]
- **HTF zone choice:** choose the HTF FVG that has unswept liquidity (stacked highs) leading into it; skip FVGs with no liquidity to sweep. [04:32]–[06:14]
- **Not pre-placed:** he won't set a limit far in advance: "I obviously take trades on current market conditions… not… set a limit this far in advance"; wait to see what London and the NY open do. [08:30]–[09:03]
- **Targets:** lows printed on the push-up into the zone (liquidity areas); TP1 at the next lows, final at a lower level. [09:03]; [15:43]
- **Stop:** above the FVG used for entry. [15:43]
- **Management:** TP1 hit → 50% off, stop to BE → "it's a risk-free trade". [16:50]
- **Bias lesson:** after buy-side was swept in London, don't assume the day is down if sell-side was also swept — the London high may be the next target. [12:21]–[12:53]; [17:25]

## Process / order of operations
1. Weekly: structure + next draws (weekly low, weekly FVG). [01:41]
2. Daily/4H: list FVGs; rank by unswept liquidity leading into them. [02:15]–[06:14]
3. 1H/15m: find a 15m FVG inside the daily FVG and the liquidity above which must be swept. [06:49]–[07:24]
4. Wait for London and the NY open; price pushes into the FVG and sweeps liquidity. [08:30]
5. 5m/1m: rejection → 1m BOS → enter in a 1m FVG (or on the BOS). [13:27]–[16:16]
6. Stop above the FVG; TP1 = nearest lows → 50% off, BE; final TP at lower liquidity. [15:43]–[17:25]

## Risk & psychology rules
- Risk 1% (the loss was "a one percent loss"). [14:36]
- After a loss: "maybe I'm done trading for the day", but he took a second trade (2 trades that day). [14:36]
- Clear the chart after the session for a fresh head. [17:58]
- "Every single trader loses; if anybody says they have 100% win rate they're lying." [19:08]–[19:40]

## Changes vs. earlier teaching / contradictions
- **Timeline placement:** Sept 2022, the earliest video in the set (pre-Boot Camp). Sweep → FVG → BOS → FVG entry already resembles the 2026 canonical "sweep → BOS → retrace into FVG → 1m trigger".
- Stop above the entry FVG (tight), not beyond the sweep — contradicts the later "invalidation = sweep extreme" rule (§11.13).
- Two trades in a day after a loss, against the later "done after a loss" rule; consistent with BC2 re-entry.
- No news, SMT, OB, time window or contract sizing mentioned.
- Answers OPEN #14 partly: choose among HTF zones by how much unswept liquidity leads into them. [04:32]–[06:14]
