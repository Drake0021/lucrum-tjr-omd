## Summary
Dec 2022 day-in-the-life vlog (first ~3.5 min) + order block lesson. Definition: an order block is the candle prior to the expansionary move that follows a liquidity sweep — "where orders were accumulated" — and price returns to it to fill more orders. A pullback without a preceding sweep is "a retracement, not an order block". He walks through that morning's S&P trade (sweep → expansion down → 5m OB → tap → 1m BOS → short; 3 TPs: 15m liquidity, 15m OB 50%, highs; +2.3%), then multiple 4H/1H/daily OB examples, and a one-slide "template" of how he trades: liquidity sweeps cause reversals; BOS shifts bias/structure; FVGs and EQ are for retracements only; OB = first candle before the expansion after a sweep. Ends with a long risk/discipline rant (Mark Douglas, no revenge trading, no strategy hopping).

## Concepts & definitions
- **Order block** — "the candle prior to the expansionary move" after a liquidity sweep; "this is the candle that caused our liquidity sweep that accumulated orders"; banks push price back into it to accumulate more orders and continue. [07:56] [12:31] [13:05] [26:49]
  - (Caption slip at [06:13]: he says "a fair value Gap is the price range where orders are accumulated prior to the expansionary move" — context shows he means the order block; (inferred).)
- **What is NOT an OB** — a retracement without a sweep: "an order block comes off of a sweep of liquidity and then an expansionary move". [13:37]
- **Drawing** — box the candle; "sometimes I do the bodies sometimes I do the Wicks it really doesn't matter because it's about the same size". [09:05] [19:57] [21:07]
- **Multi-timeframe** — usable on every timeframe; may need to drop to 1m to find the candle before expansion; can nest (daily OB → 4H OB inside). [09:38] [17:37] [22:16]
- **Liquidity sweep** — "we wick above the high and break structure to the downside" = sweep; purpose: induce opposite-direction entries/exits so banks fill orders. [05:07] [08:30]
- **FVG / liquidity void** — nobody trading against the banks there, so price returns to fill orders; FVGs are for **retracements**, not reversals; partially filled then left = "done", don't reuse; an opposite-direction FVG after structure shifted = don't use; weeks-old FVGs probably invalid. [06:13] [24:01] [24:34] [25:07]
- **Equilibrium** — for retracements, on the next higher timeframe: daily BOS up → likely weekly retracement → plot weekly EQ; 4H BOS down → daily retracement → daily EQ, etc. [26:16]
- **His template** — liquidity sweep → reversal; BOS → shift in bias/structure; FVG → retracement; EQ → retracement (next HTF); OB → first candle before the expansion after a sweep. "All five ... can be used to understand where the market is going at all times." [22:52]–[26:49] [29:05]
- **BOS check** — a wick above a high is not a BOS; need a close above. Swing high = "a move up then a move down". [19:57] [21:40]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Sequence (S&P, Dec 2022): sweep of liquidity → expansionary move → mark OB (candle before expansion) → price taps OB → LTF BOS → entry; stop beyond the OB/sweep; TPs at liquidity / OBs / 50% of an OB. [10:13] [10:46]
- A 5m BOS against a sweep-driven expansion is just a 15m retrace, not a reversal ("I don't care because we swept liquidity and made an expansionary move"). [09:05] [09:38]
- Short entry at OB after 1H BOS: stop above; TPs at the swing low / previous liquidity, final TP further. [15:51]
- After the trade, done for the day ("I took my profits and I exited ... that's all I need"). [11:20]
- Management (vlog): TP1 → 50% off; on Wednesday he deviated (held instead of following plan) and lost after TP1 → "follow your plan". Implied plan = BE after TP1. [02:21] [02:53]

## Process / order of operations
1. Identify a liquidity sweep (wick beyond a high/low). [08:30]
2. Confirm expansion / BOS the other way. [08:30]
3. Box the candle before the expansion (OB), on that TF or a lower TF. [09:05]
4. Wait for price to return to the OB. [10:13]
5. LTF BOS → enter; stop beyond; TPs at liquidity/OBs. [10:13]
