## Summary
TJR gives a trading-plan template (shown on screen, partly unreadable in captions) with five sections: (1) pre-designated risk (lot-size method, daily $ risk, number of trades), (2) when you trade (session/time window), (3) news policy, (4) what you trade (one instrument), (5) confluences/entry model. He states his own answers: NYSE open (AM session), trade only within the first 1.5 hours, one trade a day (sometimes two at reduced risk summing to one day's risk), set lot sizes for high/regular/low risk days, S&P 500 with daily bias bullish → only buys via liquidity sweep + BOS + FVG/OB entry, no trading on bank holidays. The goal is robotic, emotionless execution: if a setup doesn't follow the plan, leave. (~4:40 of banter at the start.)

## Concepts & definitions
- **Trading plan** — a written, "foolproof" checklist answering risk, time, news, instrument and confluences, kept in front of you every trading day so execution is robotic and emotionless. [04:38] [05:13] [13:00] [19:08]
- **Pre-designated risk** — decide before trading how much you will lose per day, the lot-size method, and the number of trades. [05:50] [06:22]
- **Set lot size vs calculated lot size** — set: fixed predetermined lot sizes (TJR uses three — high risk, regular risk, low risk — and picks one for the day); calculated: use a lot-size calculator (account balance, risk %, stop in pips). TJR mostly "guesstimates" from the stop size; most people should use a calculator. [06:54] [07:26]
- **Daily risk budget** — total risk per day is fixed; if he takes two trades, he de-risks each so both together equal one trade's risk. [07:59] [08:32]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Risk: write down the $ amount you risk per day and number of trades; example plan line: "I am risking $100 a day while taking one trade". Beginners: exactly one trade a day (not "1–2"). [09:05] [09:40]
- TJR himself: one trade a day, sometimes two; when two, each is de-risked so total = one day's risk. [07:59] [08:32]
- Never plan five trades a day; at most 1–2 high-probability quality setups. [08:32] [09:05]
- Trading time: TJR trades the New York Stock Exchange open (AM session) and takes a trade within the first hour and a half; if no trade in that window, he doesn't trade. The trade itself can run past 1.5 hours — he just must be in the trade by then "or else I'm done". [10:12] [10:47] [11:18]
- He calls Asian session "terrible session to trade if you ask me". [10:47]
- News: decide whether you trade on/after news at all. High-impact news (CPI, NFP, FOMC, PPI) is in a separate class from regular red/orange-folder news; with regular news you can read the bias and trade if it plays into your bias. Beginners may avoid news altogether for a high win rate. [11:18] [11:53]
- News volatility cutoff: write a rule like "if news causes an X-point spike within a 5-minute candle, I'm done for the day" — he gives 5 points/5 minutes only as a random example and says to determine it yourself. "If news f***s up the market I'm done for the day." [12:26] [13:00]
- Instrument: trade only one instrument until profitable; add another only after. [13:35] [14:09] [14:42] [16:24]
- Entry confluences (his recommendation for beginners): only take trades aligned with the daily bias and the overall market trend — liquidity sweep in line with daily bias + break of structure aligned with daily bias. [17:29] [18:03]
- Entry model (as of Day 29): liquidity sweep → BOS → then FVG or order block entry (retracement), unless the liquidity sweep + BOS alone offers good RR. [17:29] [18:36]
- TJR's plan filters (ES/S&P 500): daily bullish → only buys; if no buy via liquidity sweep + BOS + FVG/OB entry → no trade; if not within the first 1.5 hours of NYSE open → no trade; if not on the S&P 500 → no trade; if pre-designated risk not set when he wakes up → no trade. [18:03] [18:36]
- Leave conditions: setup doesn't follow plan; already took the day's trade; max daily risk used; US bank holiday when your instrument (S&P 500) doesn't trade; no liquidity sweep + BOS + all confluences. Don't look at other forex pairs/crypto/commodities. [19:41] [20:16]
- Test confluences with backtesting/journaling: e.g. test liquidity sweep + BOS for two weeks, record win rate and emotions. [16:56] [17:29]

## Process / order of operations
Trading plan template (answer in writing) [05:50]–[18:36]:
1. Risk: lot-size method (set vs calculated); $ risk per day; trades per day.
2. Time: which session and exact window (his: NYSE open, first 1.5 h).
3. News: trade on/after news or not; volatility cutoff rule.
4. Instrument: one (his: S&P 500).
5. Confluences/entry: bias alignment, liquidity sweep, BOS, FVG/OB entry.
6. Each morning: set pre-designated risk before anything; then execute only if every step matches; otherwise leave. [18:36] [19:08]
