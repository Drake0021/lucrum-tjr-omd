## Summary
An early (Oct 2022, NFP week) lesson on fair value gaps, after a day-in-the-life vlog (first ~8:50 is vlog). TJR defines an FVG as an imbalance left by an impulse candle: a three-candle pattern boxed from the wick of candle 1 to the wick of candle 3. He credits the concept to ICT, but says he uses it differently. He presents his strategy as three building blocks in order: (1) liquidity sweep, (2) break of structure, (3) FVG entry (or a retest of the broken level). He enters at the 50% of the FVG (Gann box 0.5), stops above/below the FVG or the recent swing, and targets previous liquidity at roughly 1:1–1:3. Examples are a NY-open 1m short (instrument unnamed, likely S&P/forex), a 15m forex pair, 4H and daily charts, and GBPJPY daily. He says losses come from mis-identifying liquidity and entering a false BOS.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Fair value gap** — "an imbalance within the market". An impulse candle (one big full-bodied candle that breaks "support or resistance") leaves orders that could be filled; when price returns it tends to "fill these gaps, fill the orders that are resting here and then change direction", or use it as a retracement before the next move. [10:00]–[12:15]
- **Drawing** — "fair value gap works in three candles: the first candle, the gap candle, and the third candle". Box from the bottom wick of candle 1 to the top wick of candle 3 (bearish case) and extend it right. "A fair value gap is anywhere between a wick and a wick with an impulse candle in between." [12:49]–[13:23]; [25:19]–[25:51]
- **50% fill** — "people say that more often than not fair value gaps get filled 50%… almost a hundred percent of the time fair value gaps will get filled 50 or all the way up to the top". He uses a Gann box with a 0.5 level and enters at 0.5 for a better entry. He is unsure ("I don't know how much I trust it"). [19:07]–[20:16]
- **BOS candle = FVG source** — "what does the candle look like that usually breaks structure? It looks like an impulse candle… that's where your fair value gap is." [20:50]
- **Most recent FVG** — after a push, price "usually only fills the most recent one". [22:32]
- **News FVGs** — a large daily FVG from a news candle may not get filled: "sometimes those imbalances… just don't get filled because it was a news event." [16:12]
- **FVGs alone aren't a plan** — "fair value gaps all over the place… it's just when you're supposed to use them"; don't sell just because price reaches a daily FVG; need more confluences. [13:57]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- **Model:** "one, liquidity sweep; two, break of structure; three, fair value gap entry, or just a retest of previous support or previous resistance". [16:45]
- **Session sweep:** at the New York open, look for a sweep of the London session high (or low), then drop to a lower timeframe (1m) for the BOS and FVG. [16:45]–[17:20]
- **Entry:** limit at the 50% of the FVG ("enter off the 50"), or when price enters the FVG. [17:54]; [21:25]; [23:38]; [26:25]
- **Stop:** above the FVG (its far edge / wick end) or above the most recent high (short); below the FVG or the wick for longs. [17:54]; [20:16]; [22:32]; [23:38]; [29:19]
- **Targets:** previous areas of liquidity (prior lows/highs), TP1 roughly 1:1 on the 1m, then 1:2–1:3 levels; multiple TPs (TP1–TP3). [17:54]–[18:29]; [20:16]; [26:25]
- **Sweep makes BOS meaningful:** "breaks of structure aren't too significant off of a non-sweep of liquidity, at least on a new session open." [28:04]
- **Optional pieces:** he concedes you can enter off just a BOS or just a sweep, "but I want to be very precise… we're trying to get high win rates" so use all three. [24:12]–[24:45]

## Process / order of operations
1. Mark session highs/lows (e.g. London high) before the NY open. [16:45]–[17:20]
2. NY open sweeps the liquidity. [17:20]
3. Drop to the 1m; mark the most recent swing low (for a short); wait for a close below = BOS. [17:20]
4. Mark the FVG in the impulse/BOS leg (wick to wick). [17:54]
5. Enter when price retraces into the FVG (at 50%); stop above the FVG/high; TP at previous lows (≈1:1 first). [17:54]

## Changes vs. earlier teaching / contradictions
- **Timeline placement:** Oct 2022, pre-Boot Camp. The sweep → BOS → FVG three-step model is the seed of the later 2026 canonical "sweep → BOS/IFVG → retrace into FVG → trigger".
- **FVG entry as a touch/limit at 50%** with no reaction or LTF trigger required — contradicts BC2/2026 (reaction or 1m BOS/IFVG trigger required). Framework §11.5/§11.18 later resolve this against touch entries.
- FVG definition (wick of C1 to wick of C3 around an impulse candle) matches framework §2.11.
- Mentions "retest of previous support or resistance" as an entry alternative, inconsistent with the later "S/R is guessing" stance.
- Credits ICT for FVGs here ("I learned it from him"); framework §11.24 notes conflicting statements.
- NFP: he doesn't trade NFP Friday — consistent with later news rules.
- News FVGs may not fill (new nuance).
