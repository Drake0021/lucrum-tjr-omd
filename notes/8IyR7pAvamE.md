## Summary
Nov 2022 lesson applying his building blocks to long-term investing (not day trading): enter on a **monthly BOS** to the upside (optionally after a monthly-wick sweep with a weekly BOS, or on a retest of the BOS level / EQ / OB, DCA-ing below), hold until price reaches the prior swing high, then exit on the first **weekly BOS** (candle close below a weekly swing low wick). Don't "buy the dip" in a monthly downtrend. Examples: BTC (2015–17, 2019–21), ETH, silver (yearly structure), Apple. Opens with a non-trading crypto opinion rant. Irrelevant to the ES intraday bot except for confirming the structure/BOS definitions on HTFs.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Investment entry** — "all I want is monthly break of structure". Alternatively: monthly wick above a high = sweep → weekly BOS → enter on the weekly close. [04:36] [06:51]
- **Retracement entry** — after a monthly BOS, "retracements typically retest the break of structure"; EQ (Fib 50%) often aligns with the BOS level; can DCA whenever price is below that line; a bearish OB also noted as hit. [10:16] [10:51] [11:25]
- **Exit** — wait for the prior swing high to be taken, then exit on a weekly BOS down (close below the wick of the weekly swing low); before the swing high is reached, ignore weekly BOS. [07:58] [08:30] [15:30] [16:04]
- **Yearly play (silver)** — yearly BOS up out of a multi-year range; 3-month BOS down = expected yearly retrace; 50% line aligns with BOS area; exit on monthly BOS after the high is hit. [17:09]–[19:25] [21:46]
- **Buying the dip** in a downtrend = long drawdowns (BTC 2013–16 example, Apple 10 months). [05:44] [26:51] [27:25]
- BOS validity: "not closing below the wick" → not a BOS. [15:30]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Only look for investments after a monthly BOS (in downtrend: wait for monthly BOS up). [13:07] [26:16]
- Entry: monthly BOS close, or retest of BOS/EQ (DCA below the line). [10:16] [11:25]
- Exit: after the swing high is reached, first weekly BOS against (monthly BOS for yearly plays). [07:58] [21:46]
- Use only idle capital you won't need for years. [04:36] [05:09]
- No stops discussed.

## Process / order of operations
1. Monthly: wait for BOS up (or sweep + weekly BOS). [06:51]
2. Enter at close or on retest/EQ. [10:51]
3. Hold through drawdown until the prior swing high is taken. [11:57]
4. Weekly: exit on first BOS down after that. [12:31]

## Confluences mentioned
- Monthly/weekly BOS — required. [04:36]
- Liquidity sweep (monthly wick) — optional. [06:51]
- EQ / OB / BOS retest — optional entry refinements. [10:16] [10:51]
