## Summary
A remake of his break-of-structure (BOS) lesson (Nov 2024). BOS = a trend shift in current market structure that can also indicate orders being filled. Bullish BOS = a candle **closes** above the most recent high while in a downtrend; bearish BOS = a candle closes below the most recent low while in an uptrend. Wicks never count, only the most recent swing counts, a swing is a two-candle pattern (move up then move down), and you cannot "break structure" in the direction of the existing trend (that is trend continuation). He stresses that BOS alone is not a trade: it needs context (time of day, full strategy). States he never takes a trade without a BOS on his execution timeframe.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Break of structure** — "identifies a trend shift in current market structure and ... can indicate orders being filled". [00:32]
- **Three market states** — uptrend (HH/HL), downtrend (LL/LH), consolidation (sideways). [01:04–01:37]
- **Bearish BOS** — in an uptrend, "the most recent low ... gets closed underneath", on any timeframe. [02:12–02:46]
- **Bullish BOS** — in a downtrend, a candle closes above the most recent high. [02:46–03:20]
- **Consolidation** — many internal "breaks"; the relevant event is a **breakout** of the range (close above range highs / below range lows, then a trend starts). [03:52–04:26]
- **Candle closure, not a wick** — "we need ... a candle closure not a wick"; the candle must "fully close above this high". [06:05; 07:10]
- **Swing high** = two-candle pattern: a move up then a move down (bullish candle then bearish candle); an indecision doji in between acts as "the middleman". [06:36; 19:44]
- **Swing low** = a move down then a move up. Two consecutive down candles are **not** a low/high pair even if a wick pokes beyond ("literally just two moves down"); it might be a swing on the 1m but not on the 5m. [19:44–20:17; 21:25]
- **Trend continuation ≠ BOS** — "You can't break structure to the upside when we're already in an uptrend ... it can't break the same structure two times in a row." [11:06–12:19]
- **Wick beyond a low in an uptrend** — "maintaining bullish structure" even though technically a lower low by wick; the new wick low becomes the most recent low to monitor. [14:00–15:09]
- **Most recent swing only** — "we're not monitoring a low that happened like seven lows ago"; if a newer low forms, the older one is irrelevant for BOS. [14:35; 18:36]
- **Equal close** — a candle closing equal with the low is not a BOS. [21:59]
- **Mini BOS against trend** — a small 5m BOS down in an uptrend gave "probably like a 15-minute retrace", then continuation. [07:42]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Never take a trade without a BOS on the execution timeframe. [00:00]
- Uptrend → monitor the most recent low; bearish BOS when a candle body closes below it. Downtrend → monitor the most recent high; bullish BOS on a close above it. [12:19; 15:46]
- A wick through the level = no BOS; update the monitored swing to the newest one. [14:00–15:09; 20:52]
- Do not trade a BOS blindly: "these confluences essentially mean nothing without context". [08:48–09:24]
- Time filter: a BOS at 12 p.m. on a Friday before close is not tradeable ("I would have called you an idiot"). [09:24]
- Evaluate swings on the timeframe being read (5m), not by lower-TF wicks. [20:17]

## Process / order of operations
1. Identify the current state (uptrend / downtrend / consolidation) on the timeframe. [01:04]
2. In a downtrend monitor the most recent swing high (two-candle up/down); in an uptrend the most recent swing low. [06:36; 12:19]
3. Update the monitored swing every time a new swing forms (including wick-only extensions). [14:35]
4. BOS = candle closes beyond it; new trend state begins. [07:10]
5. Combine with context and other confluences before trading (full execution video promised). [08:48]
