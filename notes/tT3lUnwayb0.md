## Summary
Guide to prop-firm (funded) accounts, with a sponsor walk-through of Alpha Capital forex (2-step / 1-step) and Alpha Futures rules (50K/100K/150K; drawdown, consistency rule, contract limits, profit split). The trading portion (~22:00–37:20) is a recap of an ES long he took "yesterday" (~11 May 2025, inferred) that he calls an A+ setup: 4H low draw hit on NQ while ES held a higher low (SMT) → ES as leading index → 1m BOS + 1m SMT → 1m FVG fill + up-close → long; stop under the BOS low/FVG; TPs at hourly highs and London session high (1:2.29 / 1:3.48 / 1:5.68). Also states he doesn't trade the first 20 min ("waiting for 9:50"). Risk section: forex prop 1–1.5% per trade; futures prop either de-risk (8–10 trades to pass) or "full-port" ~1% of balance and accept resets.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Prop firm / funded account** — pay a fee, pass an evaluation within drawdown rules, then trade firm capital for a profit split. Sponsor-specific numbers (not part of his method): Alpha forex 2-step: 8% target, 8% max DD, 4% daily DD, step 2 = 5% target, 80% split; 1-step: 10% target, 6% max DD, 4% daily DD. Alpha Futures 50K: $3,000 target, $2,000 max DD, max 5 contracts, 50% consistency rule (evaluation), 40% (funded), news holding allowed, splits 70/80/90%. 150K: 15 contracts, $6,000 DD. [02:43–21:41]
- **Manipulation window** — first ~20 min after the open: "price is going to be manipulating and really setting up for the overall move for the day." [27:18–27:52; 28:26]
- **Higher time frames hold higher power** — even if low-resistance highs are swept at the open, he keeps the HTF draws (hourly highs above, 4H low below) in mind. [28:26]
- **SMT divergence** — "when NASDAQ makes a lower low, but the S&P 500 makes a higher low." At a key level it shows "the S&P is the leading index, meaning the S&P 500 has more buying power because it didn't want to go down and take out these lows" → trade the S&P. Used on HTF (4H low) and LTF (1m lows) simultaneously. [30:39–32:53]
- **Session highs as strong draws** — Asian session high (red), London session high (blue); an hourly high that is also a session high is a stronger draw. [26:45–27:18]
- **Stop logic ("protected")** — stop under the low that created the BOS (should be a higher low if idea is valid) and under the FVG the entry is based on. [36:15–37:21]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- On funded accounts take **only A+ setups**; no "partial risk" B-trades. [24:27–25:04]
- **No trades in the first ~20 min** after 09:30; "We're really waiting for 9:50 for me to be able to start looking for trades." [27:18–28:59]
- Don't take a short-term, LTF, "pretty heavy, risky trade" off the manipulation move (e.g., sweep of 5m low-resistance highs at the open) on a funded account; aim for the HTF move. [28:59]
- Don't trade 5–8 times a day; "the best is less." [28:59–29:32]
- Strategy (as written on screen): (1) HTF key level gets hit, (2) reaction off the key level (1m BOS, SMT), (3) continuation of the new trend (FVG / OB / breaker / EQ fill + closing candle), (4) enter; exit at other HTF key levels; stop where the idea is invalidated. [35:08–36:15]
- Trade the index that held (SMT leader) — here ES held its 4H low while NQ swept it → long ES. [31:12–31:45]
- Stop under the BOS-origin low, also below the FVG; invalidation = that low being taken / FVG closed below. [33:28; 36:15–37:21]
- Targets at hourly highs then session highs (London high). [34:00]
- On futures prop with consistency rule, consider closing a huge winner earlier so one trade doesn't exceed the consistency limit. [34:36]
- Risk: forex prop 1–1.5% per trade (two losses ≈ 2–3%, leaves room under 4% daily DD). Futures prop: smaller DD → risk "way less per trade", or (option) ~1% of balance per trade ($1.5K on 150K, only ~4 losses to max DD) accepting resets; he presents both, notes most people "full-port." [37:21–43:43]

## Process / order of operations
1. Pre-open: mark HTF draws on ES and NQ (4H low), hourly highs, Asia high (red), London high (blue), 5m low-resistance highs. [25:38–27:52]
2. 09:30–09:50: manipulation; observe, don't trade. [27:52–28:59]
3. HTF key level hit (on either index) → check SMT: which index failed to make the new extreme → that is the trade instrument. [30:05–31:45]
4. Scale to 1m: BOS in trade direction (+ LTF SMT if present). [31:45–32:53]
5. Wait for continuation: retrace into 1m FVG (or OB/breaker/EQ) and up-close candle → enter. [33:28]
6. Stop under BOS low / FVG; TPs at hourly highs, then session high. [33:28–34:36]

- SMT divergence ES vs NQ (4H and 1m) — major confluence in the A+ example; picks the instrument. [30:39–32:53]
- 1m BOS — reaction confirmation. [31:45]
- FVG / OB / breaker / EQ — continuation, one needed; here FVG filled by a wick + up close. [33:28]
- Session highs (Asia/London), hourly highs — targets. [26:45; 34:00]
