## Summary
A psychology/mindset video ("top five tips to turn profitable") following five strategy-creation videos. Tips: (1) long-term vision — trading is not get-rich-quick, expect to lose for the first 1–2 years; (2) hard work, consistency, and the right work — 10,000 hours split across psychology, risk management, and edge (strategy); (3) treat trading like a job but enjoy it; (4) treat trading as a skill of predicting price, not making money; (5) focus on one skill ("one cup") before adding other income streams. No chart content.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Trading defined** — "trading isn't about making money it's about predicting where price wants to go every single day." [03:18]
- **Three pillars of profitability** — psychology (perform "robotically", emotions detached), risk management (beyond "risk 1% per trade": win rate, risk-reward analytics), and edge (strategy). [07:44][08:20][09:28][22:42]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Always trade with a stop loss and take profit; never "full port" on news. [12:57]
- Don't risk 100% of account; no strategy has a 100% win rate. [10:02]
- Don't cut winners early or losers early out of fear (psychology failure). [10:36]
- Backtest a strategy before risking live/funded funds. [18:41]
