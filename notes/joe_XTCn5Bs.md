## Summary
Lesson on equilibrium (the 50% level of the most recent swing leg), which TJR classifies as a "continuation confluence" alongside fair value gaps. Equilibrium splits the current leg into premium and discount; in an uptrend you only want to buy below equilibrium (discount), in a downtrend only sell above it (premium). He stresses — angrily — that it must be drawn from the most recent swing low to the most recent swing high (uptrend) or most recent high to most recent low (downtrend), measured in real time as the retrace forms. He shows how it slots into the 3-step model: liquidity sweep (potential to fill orders) → confirmation (IFVG/BOS) → continuation (FVG or equilibrium).

## Concepts & definitions
- **Equilibrium** — the 50% mark from the most recent swing low up to the most recent swing high within a trend (uptrend), or most recent high down to most recent low (downtrend). He uses a Gann box to draw it. [01:10] [02:50] [06:46]
- **Premium / discount** — in an uptrend, above equilibrium of the current leg = premium, below = discount. For shorts in a downtrend, you want price to trade up into premium (above 50%) before selling. [01:44] [02:18] [07:20]
- **Continuation confluence** — equilibrium and fair value gaps are the "continuation confluences": they show the opportunity to continue the (new) trend. [00:34] [04:31]
- **Real-time drawing** — the leg is defined as price forms: in a downtrend, once an up candle prints after the low, that low is the most recent low; draw from most recent high to that low. Wait for an up candle (retrace) before drawing. [12:55] [13:28]
- **3-step strategy** — "We look for potential for orders to be filled [liquidity], confirmation that orders are filled [IFVG/BOS], and then a continuation of the current trend [FVG/equilibrium]." (stated here and in BOS video) [03:22] [03:56] [04:31]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- In an uptrend, do not buy above equilibrium of the current leg; wait for price to push below equilibrium (into discount), then look for bullish confluence/continuation. Mirror for shorts. [02:18] [02:50] [03:22]
- Always draw from the MOST RECENT swing low to MOST RECENT swing high (not an older low connected to the high). [04:31] [05:38] [06:12]
- "Poking its head" below/above equilibrium (even barely, or equal-lows with it) counts as filling it. [08:30] [09:35]
- If equilibrium isn't reached, a fair value gap can serve as the continuation entry instead (either continuation confluence). [09:02] [13:28]
- Equilibrium is "completely and utterly useless" without correct context (prior liquidity sweep + confirmation). [08:30]

## Process / order of operations
1. Liquidity sweep of a draw on liquidity (opportunity to fill orders). [03:56]
2. Confirmation: inverse FVG (or BOS) showing orders filled and trend changing. [03:56] [09:02]
3. Continuation: wait for retrace into an FVG or into equilibrium of the new leg (premium for shorts / discount for longs), then enter in the new trend direction. [04:31]

## Confluences mentioned
- Equilibrium — continuation confluence; works on all time frames; used to spot retrace points for new trends. [11:14]
- Fair value gap — the other continuation confluence; often both work in tandem. [10:09]
- Inverse FVG / BOS — confirmation confluences. [09:02] [11:46]
- Liquidity sweep — the first step. [03:56]
