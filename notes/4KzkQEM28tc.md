## Concepts & definitions
- **Start of a new trend → retrace** — an extension that inverts the key (lowest) gap is "potentially the start of a new trend with a big extension down. What's typically going to follow? A retrace ... looking for some sort of equilibrium. Then we're going to be looking to execute in there" [02:22].
- **Which gap counts as the inverse** — the outermost/lowest gap of the stack must be inverted ("All the way at the lowest one") [01:47]. NQ must invert "the exact same gap" [01:47].
- **Dual-index sequence** — ES completes "every single step" (inversion → retrace into the bearish confluence → bearish confluences), then "All I would be looking for would be NASDAQ breaking structure to the downside because then ES has completed every single step ... and NASDAQ has confirmed" [02:55–03:28].
- **Confirmation inside intact 5m structure** — "The only form of confirmation that we needed was a one minute break or a one minute inverse because we were still within five-minute gaps ... We didn't break five-minute structure at all" [12:45].
- **Don't fade a gap fill inside intact structure** — a skinny bearish gap shouldn't be shorted while bullish structure holds: "we can still come down fill this gap and maintain bullish structure to push price higher on ES" [14:26–14:58].
- **Daily profile** — via an indicator marking Asia/London/NY highs and lows: "London manipulation next is going to be reversal. So, we know that New York is going to be a reversal." It "pre-identifies the daily profile"; "not necessary" [21:14–22:21].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- If ES inverts but NQ is still printing bullish structure far above the lows → not "full-time bearish". Wait for the retrace and NQ's BOS down before shorting [02:55].
- Short entry location after inversion: equilibrium of the new extension leg (retrace) [02:22].
- Long trigger in a bullish 5m context: a 1m BOS or 1m IFVG is sufficient [12:45]. "Price even gave us a chance to be able to enter again" (re-entry on the retest) [13:55].
- Stacked 5m FVGs above/below argue for patience even after an inversion [15:31].
- Targets: the obvious high ("you were right about price wanting to come up and take out these highs") [15:31]. They "should have set a cleaner TP" [16:38].
- Prop-account math (non-method): aim for $6,000 per day across two days to meet a 50% consistency rule (Alpha Futures, $200 tolerance) [17:12–18:22].

## Process / order of operations
1. Check whether the key gap is inverted on ES; check NQ for the same [01:47].
2. If only one index flips, expect a retrace; mark EQ [02:22–02:55].
3. Wait for bearish/bullish confluences at EQ on the leading index, then a BOS on the lagging index [03:28].
4. If the 5m remains intact in the trend direction, trade the 1m BOS/IFVG with the 5m [12:45].
