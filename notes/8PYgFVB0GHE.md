## Concepts & definitions
- **Trading** — "accurately predicting where price wants to go with a high probability on a daily basis" over a long period [04:30–05:02].
- **Purpose of a strategy** — mainly to stop low-probability trades. "Your strategy is less about telling you guys what trade to take and more about telling you guys what trades not to take." [05:38; 56:04–56:41]
- **Draws on liquidity** — "session highs, session lows, 1 hour highs, 1 hour lows, 4 hour highs, 4 hour lows" (HTF highs/lows with resting orders). Used **both** as the entry location (manipulation) and as targets [16:49–17:22; 32:26–34:39].
- **Manipulation** — price pushes above a high or below a low (a draw on liquidity), giving the market "the opportunity" to fill opposite orders and reverse. Opportunity, not certainty [17:55–20:08; 24:37]. Also called a liquidity sweep: "a high or a low that gets pushed above or below" [35:50].
- **Change in trend (step 2 confirmation)** — a **5m BOS** or a **5m IFVG** against the manipulation direction [20:08–21:50].
- **BOS** — uptrend: a candle **closes** below the most recent low = BOS down. Downtrend: a candle closes above the most recent high = BOS up [26:50–28:32]. A "BOS" in the direction of the existing trend is "not even a real thing"; it's just continuation [26:15–26:50].
- **IFVG** — "a fair value gap that gets disrespected": price closes beyond the lowest (for bullish) gap that should have continued the trend [28:32–29:39]. Can coincide with a BOS on the same candle [40:14].
- **5m continuation confluence (step 3)** — a retrace into a **5m equilibrium or 5m FVG** [21:50–22:23]. Simplified version: the 5m retrace = a **1m BOS against** the new 5m trend [26:15; 30:10; 35:50–36:22].
- **Entry trigger (step 4)** — on the 1m, a **BOS or IFVG back in the trade direction** after the 1m counter-BOS [22:56–23:29; 31:17].
- **Index alignment (ES vs NQ)** — "I do not like and I would highly recommend you guys don't take trades when the two indexes are not aligned." Aligned = both in a 5m uptrend or both in a 5m downtrend (by 5m BOS) [48:44–51:33; 53:46–54:21]. Misaligned = "the market is indecisive".

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- **Step 1:** mark draws on liquidity: session (Asia/London/prior), 1H and 4H highs and lows [16:49].
- **Step 2:** require manipulation of one of those levels (sweep), THEN a **5m BOS or 5m IFVG** in the opposite direction [20:08–21:50; 25:09–25:44].
- **Do not enter on the 5m IFVG/BOS itself.** "A lot of people will probably enter here, but that's going to cause you guys to get a horrible entry" [25:44; 30:44].
- **Step 3:** wait for a 5m retrace: price into a 5m FVG/EQ, **or** a 1m BOS against the new trend [21:50; 26:15; 31:17].
- **Step 4:** enter on the **close** of the 1m BOS (or 1m IFVG) back in the trade direction [31:17–31:49; 35:13; 40:14].
- **Stop:** above the "second high" for shorts / below the "second low" for longs, i.e. the swing made by the 1m retrace (the high that the 1m counter-BOS created, inferred) [35:13; 40:14; 45:26]. No point/tick size stated.
- **Targets:** the opposing draws on liquidity: hourly lows/highs, session lows/highs (London, Asia), 4H draws, daily lows as extended targets [32:26; 35:13; 45:59]. Take **partials** along the way [35:13; 42:28].
- Prefer intermediate highs/lows for an approximate 1:1 TP1 rather than waiting "10 freaking years" for a distant high [40:47].
- **Breakeven:** after the first TP, move the stop to BE. In the Wed example the runner was stopped at BE and it was still a winning day [45:59–46:32].
- **No pre-market entries,** even if the manipulation and reversal happen in pre-market: "The answer is always going to be no." Wait for the open for "real volume". If the sweep + reversal already happened in pre-market, after the open only look for the continuation step [44:17–44:51].
- **SMT / index rule for the sweep:** "On ES, we don't take out these lows, but on NASDAQ, we do ... As long as one of the indexes is doing it for us, that's all we need." He **traded NQ, the index that DID sweep**, because ES didn't get the manipulation [37:25–38:00; 41:21–43:04].
- **5m alignment filter:** do not trade unless ES and NQ are in the same 5m trend (both bullish or both bearish by 5m BOS). If misaligned at the open they may align later; keep watching [48:44–52:07; 54:21].
- **Time cutoff:** "By 10:30, I'm done for the freaking day." If the indexes are still misaligned around 10:20 ET, "call it a freaking day" [53:13]. He passes on an 11:05 setup that technically worked ("I'm not willing to sit through all of this chop") [54:21–55:30].
- **Trading window:** "there's an opportunity to trade from 9:30 a.m. ... until market closes", but the strategy filters it down [05:38].
- **Fewer trades** = fewer losses; "the less trades, the better" [03:58–04:30; 56:04].

## Process / order of operations
1. Pre-open: mark session, 1H and 4H highs/lows on ES and NQ [16:49].
2. At or after 9:30 ET (no pre-market entries), watch for manipulation: a sweep of a marked high/low on ES and/or NQ; one index is enough [24:37; 38:00; 44:17].
3. Confirm the reversal with a 5m BOS or 5m IFVG opposite to the sweep [25:09].
4. Check ES/NQ 5m alignment; both must be in the new 5m trend. If not, wait (to ~10:20) or stand down [48:44–53:13].
5. Wait for the 5m retrace: a 1m BOS against the new trend (and/or price into a 5m FVG/EQ) [30:10; 39:05].
6. Enter on the 1m BOS/IFVG close back in the trade direction [31:17; 40:14].
7. Stop beyond the second (retrace) high/low; TPs at the nearest hourly/session draws (≈1:1 first), extended TPs at 4H/daily draws; partials; BE after TP1 [35:13; 40:47; 45:59].
8. Done by 10:30 ET [53:13].
