## Summary
Daily bias part 2, recorded live on a Discord Zoom ~17 minutes before the NY open as real-time pre-market analysis (charts were missing in Part 1). TJR re-explains: weekly direction (line chart OK) for overview, daily market structure for the bias, then find HTF building-block zones (FVG, OB, equilibrium, liquidity) in line with the bias, and scale down for sniper entries (15m sweep/BOS/OB, 5m BOS; on S&P the 5m and 1m). He applies it to ES/S&P (bullish across all timeframes — looking for longs), GBPUSD (daily bearish while retracing — looking for a 15m BOS short) and gold (weekly and daily bearish, retracing — shorts above the 50%). He admits he lost on a short the day before by forcing a fundamentals-driven bias without LTF confirmation.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Weekly = overview only** — use weekly (line chart fine) for overall direction; daily is what the trades are based on, because trades target daily candles. [01:07], [02:53], [05:11]
- **Daily bias = daily market structure** — latest daily BOS; "we just recently closed back above here on the daily ... we broke structure back to the upside on the daily so we're bullish again". Close-based BOS. [04:02]
- **Trade the extension, not the retracement** — in an uptrend extensions (higher highs) are much bigger than retracements, so look for longs in an uptrend (and shorts in a downtrend); the retracement on the daily is "prime time" to set up the with-trend trade. [02:53], [05:43]
- **Daily bias overrides weekly** — GU: weekly bullish (swept weekly OB liquidity and rallied), daily still bearish → bearish until the daily flips bullish. [05:11]
- **High-confluence price zone** — an HTF area (e.g., 4H FVG + OB in bias direction) that is a "high confidence price zone"; inside it, use the same building blocks on lower timeframes for a "sniper entry" while targeting HTF take-profits. [07:29], [08:00]
- **Retracement tools** — fair value gap and equilibrium; premium/discount relative to the 50% of the range. For gold (bearish) he wants shorts in the upper half: "everything above this 50 mark money". (He says "not yet in a discount" / "FVG within a discount" while describing a bearish retrace — likely misspoken for premium; inferred.) [08:33], [09:05], [14:12]
- **Order block definition (15m S&P)** — a down-move that swept liquidity (wicked below lows without closing below) and caused a huge rally = order block. [10:49], [11:57]
- **Execution timeframes by market** — Forex: 15m and 5m; S&P: 5m and 1m. [11:21]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Take trades based on what the market gives (structure), not on what you want or on fundamentals; don't force your bias. [04:02]
- IF daily bias bearish AND daily is retracing (likely 4H BOS up) THEN look for shorts at HTF FVG/OB; require 15m BOS down for confirmation, then 15m OB / 5m BOS for sniper entry. [05:43], [06:51]
- IF the 1H and 4H are bullish while daily bias is short (GU), require at least a 15m BOS before shorting (more development time). [13:39]
- When all timeframes align (weekly, daily, 4H, 1H, 15m uptrend on S&P) → look only for longs; wait for price into the marked building blocks, then LTF BOS up for confirmation, then enter. [13:06]
- Pre-mark all bias-aligned building blocks (liquidity, OB, FVGs at several depths); "it doesn't matter where market goes, we just need to adapt" — trade whichever zone is reached with LTF confirmation (e.g., 5m BOS + OB entry with 1m BOS). [12:31]
- Liquidity sweep can be a wick past lows or a close below them — both acceptable before the reversal (vague). [10:49]
- Don't trade GJ when it's trading sloppily (instrument filter). [09:40]
- Trade after the news comes out (news was 30 min out at the open). [14:45]

## Process / order of operations
1. Weekly: overall direction (line chart OK). [01:07]
2. Daily: market structure → bias; note whether daily is extending or retracing. [02:53]
3. HTF (4H/1H): mark building blocks in line with bias — FVG, OB, liquidity, equilibrium (premium/discount). [05:43], [09:05]
4. When price reaches those zones, scale down: 15m liquidity sweep → 15m BOS → 15m OB; then 5m BOS = sniper entry (S&P: 5m/1m). [06:51], [11:21]
5. Target HTF levels (OBs, liquidity). [06:16], [08:00]
