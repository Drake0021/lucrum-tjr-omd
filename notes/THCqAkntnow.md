## Summary
A pre-market bias for Wed 2024-01-24. Flash manufacturing and services PMI are released 15 minutes after the open (9:45 ET). He recaps 01-23: he went long ES off a sweep of lows, was stopped out, and then every TP was hit. The NQ execution points were never reached ("0 and 1"). Monthly, weekly, daily, 4H and 1H are all bullish at all-time highs, so "I'm going to hold a bullish bias until market proves otherwise."
