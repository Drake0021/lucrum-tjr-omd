## Summary
Explains prop-firm funded accounts as a way for low-capital traders to access $25k-$150k accounts by paying a fee and passing an evaluation (profit target without breaching max drawdown). Largely a sponsored overview of Alpha Futures and Tradeify plans with discount codes. Trading-method content is minimal; the useful parts are the rule parameters (profit target, max drawdown, contract caps, consistency rules) that constrain a bot's risk if trading in a prop account, and his insistence that you need the trading skill first.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Prop firm / evaluation** — pay a fee for a challenge; hit a profit target without exceeding a max drawdown; then trade a funded/qualified account with profit split and payouts. [00:34]
- **Instant funding** — some accounts skip the evaluation but have stricter payout rules (e.g. consistency). [01:09] [13:27]
- **Consistency rule** — cap on how much of total profit can come from one day/trade; e.g. 50% rule ⇒ profits split across at least ~3 trades/days; 20% rule ⇒ ~5-6 winning trades. [10:09] [10:41] [15:39]
- Cheaper accounts typically harder to pass and with smaller payout caps. [02:52] [03:24]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Example Alpha Futures Advanced $100k (as stated in video, Jan 2026): min 2 trading days; profit target 8% ($8,000); max position 10 contracts; max drawdown $3,500 (fail at $96,500); no daily loss limit; reset $279; 50% consistency rule in evaluation; holding through news allowed; qualified account: min 5 trading days, no consistency rule. [07:20] [07:58] [08:30] [09:36] [10:09] [16:12]
- Tradeify Lightning (instant funding): 5-day payout frequency, max 5 accounts, 20% consistency rule. Select accounts: pass eval in ~3 days, payout eligible after 4, daily payouts. [12:54] [15:06]
- "10 contracts is plenty" unless trading seconds timeframes. [07:58]
- He personally prefers Select (daily payouts) over Lightning instant-funding. [17:19]
