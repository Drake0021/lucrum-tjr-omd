## Concepts & definitions
- **4H retrace into 4H equilibrium**: with the 4H bullish, he wants NQ to sweep hourly lows and fill 4H EQ, then look for reversals. "This is close enough for me to consider that 4-hour equilibrium hit." [04:33; 12:31]
- **SMT for index choice**: "if we can make a big bullish SMT on ES, then we can just long … ES off of this SMT cuz NASDAQ is selling off super hard". That means long the index that holds (ES) while the other (NQ) sweeps. Ambiguity: "this also could be a bearish SMT divergence"; he favors the side the HTF supports ("assume NASDAQ is lagging"). [04:33; 10:44–11:24]
- **Conflicting timeframes → decision timeframe**: when the 4H is bullish and the 1H bearish, "the key time frame… the decision maker… is the 15-minute". Either (a) price retraces, fills the 15m gap and continues down (target the hourly lows), or (b) price retraces and disrespects the bearish confluences (IFVG), so target the market-open highs and the gap above. [40:17–42:30]
- **An hourly BOS against the 4H is just a 4H retrace**: "even if we break hourly structure this would just be a 4-hour retrace and we're in 4-hour equilibrium." [27:06; 40:50]
- **Illiquid price range**: a smooth move up then a smooth move down leaves an illiquid range. If price gets back into it, it can travel through it swiftly to the highs. [47:49]
- **Bullish continuation template**: a BOS up, then filling an FVG and getting confirmation; or a BOS / IFVG, then a retrace into EQ or FVG and a push higher. [50:07–50:42]
- **Decision points**: a 15m gap, 15m EQ and 5m gap are repeatedly framed as "make or break". Respect = continuation of that side; inversion = flip. [1:29:17; 1:37:25]
