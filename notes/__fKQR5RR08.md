## Summary
TJR records his pre-market daily bias for S&P, GJ, gold and GU on Fri Jul 14 2023 (prelim consumer sentiment at 10:00 ET, 30 min after the open), then recaps a losing S&P long (~$9.5K loss) and reviews afterward. Key teachings: higher-timeframe (hourly) building blocks work better than 15m ones on S&P; you can't trade against daily bias even if you think the market is overbought; at minimum the daily and 4H must agree (and the 4H can't flip without a 1H BOS first); trade extensions (with-trend legs), not retracements; and the ideal loss is one caused by a HTF reversal, not an execution error. His mistake: he entered off a lower-timeframe, unmarked liquidity sweep instead of waiting for the HTF building blocks he had marked — price later came into his marked 1H order block / FVG and rallied, proving the bias right and the execution wrong.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Hourly vs 15m building blocks on S&P** — "lately I kind of like using hourly building blocks compared to 15 minute building blocks on the S&P; tends to work out a bit better." [02:19]
- **Accumulation zone** — a price range with multiple OBs/FVG/15m low; he treats the whole range as one area ("accumulation central"). [02:52]
- **Best kind of loss** — a trade within daily bias lost because the daily market structure shifted that day (HTF reversal), not an execution error; aim for those to be your only losses. [02:52], [03:25], [14:47]
- **Minimum alignment** — at least the daily and 4H must be in confluence (ideally the 1H too); the 4H BOS confirms daily continuation. To even get a 4H BOS you need a 1H BOS first. [04:03], [04:38], [05:11]
- **Extensions vs retracements** — retracements (the small counter-trend legs) are hard to predict and choppy (doji after doji, low volatility); extensions (with-trend legs) are easy, where volume and volatility go. Map every building block a retrace could reach, then act on the reaction to catch the extension. [15:20]–[17:36]
- **Gold Forex-strategy confluence** — London session low swept / tapped as liquidity aligned with HTF bias → wait for 5m BOS. [06:54]
- **News effect** — over recent months news has affected Forex more than the S&P (he doesn't know why). [08:39]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- No trading until high-impact news (here prelim consumer sentiment, 30 min after open) is out; better to wait and miss the play than gamble on technicals pre-news. [01:07], [09:12], [09:45]
- Do not trade against the daily bias even if you believe the market is overbought and due for a retracement; stick with it until proven wrong. [02:52]
- IF daily bearish but 4H and 1H bullish THEN low odds — bottom of the list; need daily AND 4H in confluence, and a 1H BOS before any 4H BOS. [04:03]–[05:11]
- IF weekly bearish but daily bullish THEN trade the daily (bullish) — need a daily BOS down to show weekly continuation. Look for daily building blocks for longs. [05:47], [06:21]
- IF daily bullish but 4H just broke structure down (and 1H bearish) THEN don't trade — likely a daily retracement. [10:18], [10:52]
- A LTF liquidity sweep against the HTF picture (4H hadn't broken, daily/weekly bullish, 15m bullish off the sweep) is a low-probability sweep — don't take it. [11:25]
- "Don't try and take a trade based off of low time frame confirmation; take a trade off of high time frame bias in line with low time frame confirmation." [11:59]
- Wait for the HTF building blocks you marked pre-market to be hit before looking for execution; don't settle for an unmarked LTF sweep because price is taking forever. [21:32], [22:05], [22:40]
- Entry he used: after sweep, wait for BOS, don't enter on the BOS alone; wait for retrace into equilibrium and strong rejection closing back out of equilibrium → entry candle; stop just below the wick (slightly below the area). [19:18], [19:52]
- An OB tap + 1m BOS with no secondary OB/FVG entry is "not necessarily high confluence". [26:30], [27:03]
- After a loss, don't add risk with another trade (especially after a large loss) — stick to the plan. [20:58], [24:17]

## Process / order of operations
1. Check news & time; set "no trades until news" if needed. [01:07]
2. Per instrument: weekly → daily → 4H → 1H bias. [01:07]
3. Mark building blocks in line with bias (prefer 1H blocks on S&P; note 15m OB/FVG/lows but keep chart uncluttered). [01:41], [02:19]
4. Rank pairs by alignment; drop ones with daily/4H conflict. [05:11], [09:12]
5. Wait for marked HTF building block to be hit → LTF confirmation (BOS) → entry at OB/FVG/EQ with confirmation. [22:05]
6. Post-session: review bias vs actual and execution. [26:30]
