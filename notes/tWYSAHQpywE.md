## Concepts & definitions
- **Pre-market HTF sweep → LTF manipulation at the open** — "when we get the high time frame sweep during pre-market, I'm always looking for some sort of low time frame manipulation" [01:42; 02:15].
- **Bullish SMT (ES swept London lows, NQ did not)** — "We swept it on ES. We didn't sweep on NASDAQ" [01:42]. Later NQ sweeps the 5m lows while ES holds, and he favours ES "because it's the leading index on this lower time frame SMT, as long as this can hold" [03:21–04:28].
- **Gap behaviour** — with a big weekend gap, price usually trades up out of it first and revisits it later by taking new low-resistance liquidity. An immediate full fill is not guaranteed [04:28–05:00].
- **79% extension closure** — "ES we don't have a gap that we can inverse. We can look for a 79% extension closure on the fib from this high down to this low right here. So that's pretty much right above those wicks". Alternative: "see if it closes above this candlestick" [06:01–06:34]. This gives the anchoring: fib from the last swing high to the swing low; a candle close above the 79% level = the long trigger, used when no FVG exists to invert.
- **Gap respected + EQ filled** — "Filled equilibrium ... We stayed respecting the gap ... now I would be willing to take a trade" [10:52–11:32].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Wait at least ~10 minutes after the open: "Last time I took a trade like four minutes into market open. It didn't end that well. Ideally, we wait at least like 10 minutes into market open" [06:01].
- Don't jump in on the first sweep; wait for a retrace into the gap/EQ: "Patience pays" [10:52].
- Trigger options: a 1m IFVG; if no gap exists, a close above the 79% extension or above a candle body; bullish closes on the next candles [06:34; 12:06].
- Index choice: trade the index that held (leading) on the LTF SMT (ES), as long as it holds [03:21].
- A 1m BOS alone at ~9:38 felt "kind of early" → he waited for "a one minute confluence" (later regretted) [08:10–09:23].
- In-trade warning: "two bearish SMTs on the one minute. I don't really like that" [14:45].
- Re-entry after a stop: allowed if price confirms (NQ closes above the highs) [19:09–20:27].
- Management: half at TP1 (London highs/nearby highs), stop to BE (slightly in profit) [30:45–31:29; 44:13].
