## Summary
This is a conceptual intro to **breaker blocks**, a tool not present in the 2023 Boot Camp framework. The first ~5.5 minutes are a Mastermind plug and music. TJR notes that others describe breakers as "failed order blocks," because they count every retracement leg as an OB. He counts only the initial sweeping leg as an OB. His definition: a breaker is a retracement leg within a trend that is then broken through by a BOS against that trend. That range now lacks orders in the old direction, so on a revisit, a reaction in the new direction implies continuation, "super similar to a fair value gap."

## Concepts & definitions
- **His OB vs others' OB** — "I only consider order blocks the initial move up that sweeps liquidity." Others call every retracement leg an OB. [06:41–07:17]
- **Breaker block (his wording)** — "a retracement followed by a break of structure opposite of the current trend, which then makes that price range (the retracement price range) lack buy/sell orders depending on previous trend." [11:16–12:57]
- **Mechanism (bullish breaker)** — In a downtrend, each up-retracement is a range where sell orders were filled. When price pushes all the way through that range and breaks structure up, "this price range no longer has any sell orders to offer." If price revisits it and buying pressure comes out, price continues higher. [08:25–09:36; 14:18–14:52]
- **Mechanism (bearish breaker)** — In an uptrend, each down-retracement is where buy orders were filled. Once price breaks structure down through it, the range lacks buy orders. A revisit with selling power means continuation lower. [10:10–11:16]
- **Why useful** — "if price revisits that area ... if there is selling/buying pressure out of that price range, price will continue into that direction due to the lack of orders from the previous trend that was just broken." [13:44–14:18]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Breaker = continuation confluence. Use it only after the BOS that broke through it, in the new BOS direction, and only with a reaction (buying/selling pressure) on the revisit. [09:36; 14:52]

## Process / order of operations
1. Identify the prior trend and its retracement legs.
2. Wait for a BOS opposite to that trend, through the retracement leg.
3. Box the broken retracement leg = breaker.
4. On a revisit, require a reaction in the new direction.

## Confluences mentioned
- Breaker block — "super similar to a fair value gap" (lack of orders). [09:36]
