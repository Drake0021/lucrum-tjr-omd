## Concepts & definitions
- **Long/buy vs short/sell** — buy if you think price will go higher; sell/short if lower. [03:21] [03:55]
- **Day trading holding period** — "more often than not we are taking trades within a 5 [minute] to an hour long period". [04:29]
- **Timeframe** — how much time each candle covers (1D = 24h of price movement; 15m; 5m). Lower timeframes show the same price action in smaller candles. [12:17] [20:32] [21:41]
- **Candle anatomy** — body = open to close. Up candle (his blue): open at the bottom, close at the top. Down candle (his black): open at the top, close at the bottom. Wicks = where price went but did not open or close; the top of the wick = high, the bottom = low. [13:28]–[18:43]
- **Funded account (prop firm)** — buy a challenge, hit a profit target without breaching loss limits, then trade firm capital (up to $200k); move payouts to a personal live account. [26:40]–[27:47]
