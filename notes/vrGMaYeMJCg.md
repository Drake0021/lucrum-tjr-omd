## Summary
TJR shows how to identify equilibrium with the Gann box at the 50% of the latest swing range. In an uptrend he anchors swing low → swing high, and in a downtrend swing high → swing low. Swings use his two-candle swing rule. He walks drawn and chart examples, including the weekly (instrument unnamed; likely ES, inferred). He stresses that EQ is a continuation confluence that won't always be reached. He also lists his "high probability areas": order blocks, breaker blocks, fair value gaps and equilibrium.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Swing high / swing low for EQ** — "literally just the higher high and the higher low within the uptrend." Swing = move up then move down, with the extreme taken as the highest/lowest of the two candles (the two-candle rule). [01:53; 08:53]
- **Anchoring** — Uptrend: from swing low up to swing high. Downtrend: from swing high down to swing low. [01:09; 05:20–06:02]
- **Tool** — Gann box; EQ = the 50% / halfway point. He shows his settings (not readable in the transcript). [03:02; 05:20]
- **Discount / premium** — Uptrend: below 50% = discount (buy), above = premium. In a downtrend he calls the area **above** 50% the "discounted price range" while also saying "this is the premium" and that we want to short at a higher price. This is the terminology slip already noted in framework §11.23. [04:11; 06:02–06:38]
- **Continuation confluence** — Price does not have to reach EQ. Small legs that never dip below EQ happen; "you just have to accept that." Re-anchor on the next swing low → swing high. [07:13–08:21]
- **High-probability areas** — "order blocks, breaker blocks, fair value gaps and equilibrium." Wait for confluences (buying/selling power) out of them. [11:50–12:24]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Longs: only consider buying after price retraces **below** 50% of the latest swing low → swing high, with buying power out of it. Shorts mirror this, above 50%. [04:11; 06:38]
- Don't enter on tiny pullbacks that reach no FVG, OB or EQ ("we probably don't want to be entering on that"). [11:50]
- The trend is defined by BOS. After a BOS up, switch to the uptrend anchoring. [09:34]

## Process / order of operations
1. Determine the trend from the last BOS.
2. Identify the latest swing pair (two-candle rule).
3. Draw a Gann box; 50% = EQ.
4. Wait for a retrace into discount (longs) or premium (shorts).
5. Require buying/selling power out of it.

## Confluences mentioned
- EQ — continuation confluence; "if it does [go into discount], it's a higher confluence." [07:13]

## Trade examples
- Downtrend chart: BOS down, then swing high → low; price pushes into premium, selling power, lower (x2). Then BOS up, swing low → high, discount, buying, higher. [08:53–10:07]
- Weekly: BOS up, swing low → high, poke into discount, higher [10:07]
- Downtrend: push into EQ, selling power, lower "for a bit" [10:42]

## Risk & psychology rules
- Accept that the market sometimes moves without hitting your confluences. [08:21]
