## Summary
TJR responds to viewers confused by his recent lower-timeframe trades: his strategy is "universal" and fractal — liquidity sweeps, BOS, FVGs, order blocks, breaker blocks exist on every timeframe, so the same model can be shifted up (swing trading: weekly/daily bias, 4H/1H execution) or down (1H/15m bias & sweep, 5m/1m execution). The standard stack remains 4H bias → 1H trend → 15m/5m execution. Key rule: the size of the sweep's timeframe determines the expected move, so targets must match the execution/sweep timeframe (e.g., 15m sweep → 15m high targets, not daily highs). He walks through a May 16 2024 ES/NQ trade using a 15m sweep, SMT, IFVG and 1m entry.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Universal / fractal strategy** — "the confluences that I teach you can apply it to any time frame ... you can scale it down and you can scale it up." Liquidity sweeps, BOS, OBs, FVGs, breaker blocks occur on every TF. [00:39][01:51][08:23]
- **Standard stack** — "we look at the 4 Hour for overall daily bias, then we look at the 1 hour see how that trend correlates and then we scale down to the 15 and the 5 minute and find executions." [01:17]
- **Sweep timeframe ↔ move size** — A 15m sweep implies a smaller move than a 4H or 1H sweep, so use lower TFs for execution and targets. [10:12]
- **SMT as sweep validation** — SMT divergence "kind of signifies a valid sweep." [11:20]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Swing variant: weekly/daily for trend & draws on liquidity; 4H/1H for BOS and FVG entries; stop above the FVG; target draws on liquidity. [03:34][04:09]
- Intraday-lower variant: 1H as bias setter, 15m (or 1H) for liquidity sweep / draws, 5m BOS + 5m FVG, optional 1m BOS coinciding with an order block. [05:55][06:31][07:04]
- Stop: below the 5m FVG the entry was taken off of (in his long example). [07:39]
- Target must match execution timeframe: don't target a daily high from a 5m entry; target 15m highs/previous highs (example ~1:1.94 RR). [07:39][08:23]
- If no HTF (4H/1H) liquidity draws/sweeps are available ("obvious on the chart that there's no draws on liquidity on the super high time frame"), scale down to lower TFs instead of not trading. [10:12][13:04]
- He personally prefers not to swing trade on weekly/daily sweeps — he trades session to session. [05:16]
- Low-TF entry → low-TF take profit. [12:28]

## Process / order of operations
1. Pick timeframe stack (default 4H → 1H → 15m/5m). [01:17]
2. Identify trend/bias on the top TF; find liquidity sweep on the next.
3. Scale down for BOS + FVG/OB entry.
4. Stop at the entry FVG's invalidation; target liquidity on the matching TF. [07:39]

## Confluences mentioned
- Liquidity sweep, BOS, FVG, order block, breaker block — all valid on any TF. [08:23]
- SMT divergence — validates a sweep. [11:20]
- IFVG (invalidating a bearish FVG) + BOS. [11:53]
