## Summary
A week-long vlog and trade diary for FOMC week (Mon Oct 31 – Fri Nov 4, 2022, inferred from the upload date and "FOMC Wednesday / NFP Friday"). Despite the title there is no dedicated FOMC method. He trades Monday, Tuesday and Thursday with the early sweep → BOS → FVG/retest model. He stays out before the Wednesday FOMC release, takes two discretionary trades **after** FOMC, and takes Friday (NFP) off completely, then shows hypothetical NFP-day setups. He also shows weekly bias work: a 4H double top/BOS down, EQ (Fib 0.5) of the daily swing as the "bank buying area", and the daily low whose close decides whether the bias flips. Rules of note: stop 4 pips (his own ~2.5) below the sweep, 50% off at TP1 then BE, 25% off at TP2, risk 0.5% per trade in FOMC week, and he prefers FVGs over order blocks. Instrument is mostly S&P 500 (traded as a CFD with lots), with one trade not specified.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Liquidity labels** — buy-side liquidity = highs above; sell-side liquidity = lows below; mark both before the NYSE open. [08:27]; [16:28]; [47:26]
- **Sweep → hawk the opposite swing** — "once we get our sweep of liquidity I start hawking the highs if we sweep sell side… if we swept buy side I would be hawking the lows." A high = "move up, leg down"; it isn't a high until a bearish (black) candle prints after the up move. [20:53]–[21:29]
- **Real sweep test (speed)** — "if liquidity was swept it would have been quick"; after a real sweep price "will come down, make a high and then break that high almost immediately"; a slow grind after the sweep means "this was not enough liquidity to make a move up". [23:16]–[23:52]
- **Equilibrium** — Fib from the previous daily swing low to swing high; the 0.5 level; "the banks will probably be buying back shares… anywhere under here", i.e. discount buy area. For a bearish leg, "everything from here and above" (above 0.5) is the area for banks to sell (he calls it "a discount", a misnomer for premium). [13:01]; [51:19]
- **FVG 50% entry** — enter at the 50% mark of the FVG, or at its bottom. [10:42]; [48:01]
- **Order block** — the bullish OB tapped; "I don't even really like using order blocks… I'd rather just use fair value gaps, they're easier for me." [42:57]–[43:30]
- **Daily BOS confirmation** — "we can't call it official yet unless this low gets broken… if this daily candle closes above here or literally the body is right on that line… we are not moving down yet." [37:59]–[38:33]
- **Retail trap view** — double tops and obvious BOS attract retail shorts; banks may "fake them out with a false break of structure on the daily". [14:45]; [41:52]–[42:24]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- **Entry model:** sweep of sell-side (or buy-side) liquidity → track the most recent high (low) → candle **close above** it (BOS) → long on the retest of the BOS, or at the 50% of the FVG. [03:25]; [10:07]–[10:42]
- **Stop:** "my stop loss is always four pips below the liquidity sweep"; for himself "like 2.5, it really depends" (covers his broker spread); Discord told to use 4 pips. On news, the stop can be tighter: "under the liquidity sweep or we can keep it a little bit close just because we know it's news". [03:25]–[03:58]; [48:35]
- **Management:** TP1 hit → close 50%, move stop to BE; TP2 → close 25% and trail stop into profit (under a recent low); runner left for big moves (a prior trade reached ~1:27 RR). [04:31]; [06:46]–[07:21]; [31:52]; [34:36]–[35:11]; [49:09]
- **TP1 tight when counter-bias:** "since my bias for today was bearish, I made sure to keep take profits really tight" on a counter-bias long. [10:42]
- **Targets:** previous areas of liquidity / "support and resistance", equal lows, swing highs/lows. [31:52]; [50:14]
- **News timing:** a perfect-looking setup (buy-side sweep, BOS down, FVG) before a 10:00 ET release was skipped: "I would rather lose after news than lose by news stopping me out." Wait until after the release. [08:27]–[09:44]
- **FOMC day:** stays away before the release; "I know I will definitely be trading after it". [11:52]; [26:09]–[26:42]
- **Pre-market news:** news 30 minutes before (pre-market) → "I'm not even looking to trade right now" until the open. [16:28]
- **LTF BOS requirement for a prominent sweep:** for a long off the sell-side sweep he wants a **5m** BOS "because this is the more prominent liquidity sweep". [18:05]–[18:39]; [19:45]
- **Bias preference:** when bearish for the day, prefer a buy-side sweep → short to EQ, rather than longs. [19:45]
- **No sell-side visible:** if no sell-side liquidity sits below the open, use an HTF FVG's 50% as the rejection area, then a LTF BOS. [28:27]–[29:35]
- **NFP:** "I never really like trade on NFP"; took Friday off. [43:30]; [45:08]; [46:51]
- **Weekly-bias flip:** the daily close relative to a specific low decides; if it closes back above, look for liquidity/FVG longs; if below, look for EQ off swing highs and shorts. [38:33]–[41:18]; [53:02]

## Process / order of operations
1. Weekly outlook on the 4H/daily: structure, EQ of the last daily swing, FVGs and liquidity as draws. [12:29]–[15:19]
2. Check news timing; no entries before a scheduled release (10:00 ET data, FOMC) or in pre-market. [08:27]; [16:28]
3. At the NYSE open mark buy-side and sell-side liquidity. [16:28]; [47:26]
4. Wait for a sweep, then mark the most recent high/low and wait for a close beyond it (5m for a prominent sweep, 1m for scalps). [18:39]; [20:53]
5. Enter on the BOS close/retest or the FVG 50%; stop 4 pips beyond the sweep. [03:25]; [10:42]
6. TP1 → 50% off, stop to BE; TP2 → 25% off, stop into profit; runner. [31:52]
7. If the sweep isn't followed by a quick BOS, no trade. [23:52]

## Risk & psychology rules
- FOMC week: risk 0.5% per trade ("we've only been risking 0.5 percent per trade"). [25:03]
- Took one day (NFP) off by design: "I made sure I wasn't going to trade at least one day for this trading week". After a great week: "not looking to trade anymore this week". [45:08]; [46:51]
- Three trades in a day "I generally advise against". [28:27]
- "The whole point of day trading is not to win big every single time… take profit when you are up." [35:11]–[35:44]
- Adapt the bias day to day; being wrong on the weekly outlook didn't cause losses. [37:25]
- Stop is for invalidation; widen for spread rather than move the TP to fake a win (he refused to adjust TP after the fact). [10:42]–[11:18]
