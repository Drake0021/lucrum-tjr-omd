## Summary
A pre-market bias for Mon 2024-01-29 (no news). ES is at all-time highs and bullish on the daily, but the 4H/1H are in a short-term range (≈4899–4919/4933), and price action has been "really, really ugly" since the prior Wednesday. He almost says not to trade, and decides to **play it as a range** and **de-risk if he trades**. NQ: weekly, daily and 4H are in an uptrend. NQ already had the HTF liquidity sweep of lows, a BOS, and now an FVG fill / sweep of hourly lows, so he is more bullish on NQ. ES has only higher lows (untouched liquidity), with no sweep. A bullish trigger would be a sweep of 15m lows, then longs to the highs or the pre-market highs.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Range-bound market** — the 4H/1H are trapped between levels. "Range bound market, play it as one and that way you will more than likely not get super super beamed" [02:50–03:27].
- **Easy vs hard trading conditions** — a clean trend (e.g. a clean 5m trend) = "really easy trading conditions"; slow, non-moving price action is not [05:08–05:47].
- **Untouched higher lows = open liquidity** — on ES "all of these lows are just untouched liquidity" (sell-side liquidity resting below a staircase of higher lows) [06:58–07:31].
- **Head and shoulders** — "even though I'm not a pattern trader ... take that with a grain of salt cuz it really doesn't mean anything to me" [03:27–03:59].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Bad or ranging price action → if trading at all, **de-risk** [03:27].
- A range needs a break: he needs the range highs (4933) taken out, or major lows (4902/4898) taken, before he gets an HTF bias [02:17–02:50].

- Prefer the index that has already swept liquidity (NQ) over the one with only untouched higher lows (ES) [06:58–07:31].
