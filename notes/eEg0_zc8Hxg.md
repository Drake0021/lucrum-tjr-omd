## Concepts & definitions
- **Bar-close countdown** — "most of the time our entry executions are based off of waiting for a candlestick to close above or below a high or a low"; the countdown tells you when the candle closes [07:39–08:46].
- **Equal highs / sweep check** — hover to read the exact OHLC values and decide whether a high was actually exceeded. In his example the second high exceeded the first by about $0.75–$1 (on ES ~7,633), and he counted that as "price pushed above this high for maybe a potential liquidity sweep" [44:02–45:12]. Even a one-tick excess counts as a push above (inferred).
- **Gann box = equilibrium** — 50% of low→high or high→low; no background, only the 0.5 line [25:00–26:07].
- **Fibonacci retracement** — used to find where price may retrace for the next higher low. He shows his settings but doesn't explain the level choices [23:17–24:58].
- **Session high/low indicator** — his only indicator; it plots each session's high and low, which he marks every day anyway [37:23–38:29].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Set the chart time zone to **New York** (the market you trade), whatever your local time [05:27–06:33].
- Mark the **9:30 ET market open** with a red dashed vertical line every day [21:00–22:44].
- Mark levels with a **horizontal ray** [19:19–20:26]; mark FVGs with the **rectangle tool** [35:40–36:15].
- Use the long/short position tool before every trade to check stop and TP distance in points and the R:R; reject poor-R:R trades (e.g. risk 1 to make 0.51) [30:29–32:48].
- Trading View order panel: enter, set the TP, then **drag the stop to breakeven** and/or move the TP to a nearer high during the trade [50:56–51:30].
- No indicators besides session H/L; no harmonic patterns, Fib circles/wedges or chart patterns drawn [26:40–29:57; 37:55].
- Use Japanese candlesticks, not line charts or other candle types, for trading [38:29–39:34].
- Keep favorite timeframes for fast switching: **1m, 5m, 15m, 1H** (plus 4H, daily, weekly in examples) [40:40].

## Process / order of operations
- Backtest with bar replay: go back (e.g. to December 2025), play to the 9:30 open, drop to the 5m, look for a BOS, then the retrace [47:26–48:08].
