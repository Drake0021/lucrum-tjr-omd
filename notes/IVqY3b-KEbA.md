## Concepts & definitions
- **London manipulation → reversal → NY continuation** — the session pattern behind his bearish bias [00:00; 00:36].
- **New day opening gap (NDOG)** — his "objective for the day" on NQ; on ES he wanted the NDOG filled and the previous-day high retested [00:36; 02:21].
- **Retrace vs extension in a downtrend** — "the legs down are larger than the legs up ... because we're in a downtrend". Retraces "can be very choppy and/or just sideways", so a long on a retrace has poor draws and no clear target [02:54–04:01].
- **Big imbalance = no target** — inside a "fat imbalance" you "can't really pinpoint where price would want to retrace to" [04:01; 46:16].
- **Hourly candle density** — after a drastic move, expect the hourly candle to close "dense" (little wick), so wait until the candle is close to closing before entering a counter move [05:10–06:15].
- **Sub-1m SMT via candle anatomy** — reading 30-second/15-second swing lows from 1m wicks: ES low → higher low vs NQ low → lower low = bullish SMT [11:28–12:03; 49:33–50:40].
- **Flash-crash retrace logic** — after a flush takes the *last* draw on liquidity before a large imbalance and the next draw is far away, expect a LTF retrace. Market makers buy back the resting sell orders below the lows, which produces the retrace [45:44–47:54].
- **79% extension closure** — a Fib from the swing high to the low; a candle close above the 79% level is the trigger when no IFVG exists and the BOS level is too far for good RR [12:03; 51:13].
- **"Market experience" / half-court-shot trade** — "statistically not a good trade to take" even for him; not taught; beginners should not copy it [15:12; 19:27–21:47].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- If the open flushes straight into the bias objective with no entry, stay out: "when the market makes a move this drastic ... it's kind of best to stay out" [01:45].
- Don't long retraces inside HTF downtrends (poor draws, chop) — the general rule, broken here deliberately [02:54–04:35; 48:26].
- Don't long purely off a 1m BOS during a strong down move; prefer an SMT divergence combined with a break [09:46–10:19].
- LTF retrace scalp conditions (his exception): HTF draw(s) taken on both indexes (NQ last lows; ES NDOG filled + previous-day high tap) → no remaining draw before a large imbalance → LTF SMT → 1m confluence (IFVG / BOS / 79% extension close) → enter [45:12–51:47].
- When the BOS level is too far (RR too poor) and there's no IFVG, use the 79% extension close as the trigger [51:13].
- Targets must match the timeframe: a LTF retrace means LTF targets ("only trading it for six points") [50:40–51:13].
- Stop: under the lows [51:47].
- TPs: TP1 = the top of the 1m downtrend leg (1m highs; the main objective), TP2 = the end of the 5m FVG, TP3 = London session lows, TP4 = 1m highs / equal highs, TP5 = 5m highs [48:26–51:47].
- Management: at TP1 close 50% and move the stop to BE; partial again when the 5m gap fully fills ("price will fully fill a gap but it won't throw a wick past it") [16:58–17:41].
- He considered a full TP on ES once NQ hit its 5m equilibrium (cross-index exit cue) [18:15].
- News: FOMC (previous day) produced sideways price until the release. He plans to "stay away from trading" on NFP Friday [00:00–00:36; 53:31].
