## Summary
Full walkthrough (May 2023, just before Boot Camp 1) of his self-made Forex session strategy, "the strategy that turned me profitable": sessions respect prior sessions' highs/lows, so mark the previous session's highs/lows on the 30m as body-to-wick boxes, wait for the next session's open to trade into them, drop to the 5m and enter on a BOS away from the box. Stop beyond the session high/low, TP1 at the opposite previous-session high/low (or an FVG/OB/EQ in between if too far/counter-trend), later TPs at hourly lows / HTF highs. He gives exact session clock times (London time), several bar-replay examples (wins and counter-trend losses), a live morning markup of GJ/GU/gold, and a recap of that morning's gold short. Intuition filters: trade with HTF bias, reaction must happen at/near the box, avoid chop and late-session setups.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Session theory** — "sessions tend to respect previous sessions' highs and lows"; each new session's money (new money entering) takes out the previous session's traders (liquidity) to move price its way. [01:40] [07:12] [10:35]
- **Session clock (chart set to London time)** — Asian open 00:00, London open 08:00, New York open 13:00 (this is NY *pre-market*; he deliberately uses 13:00, not 14:30 NYSE open, because FX pairs start moving earlier than the S&P). 22:00 = "spread hour"; 23:00 = Asian pre-market. Only opens matter, not closes. [04:24] [04:58] [05:34] [06:08]
- **Session high/low box** — drawn on the **30m**, from the start of the candle bodies to the tip of the wick ("from the start of the bodies all the way up to the top of the wick"); same for lows. [12:14] [12:46]
- **Swing low counting** — "move down then a move up"; he ignores a candle that isn't part of the swing ("imagine that candle is not even there"). [24:49]
- **Shorts off lows / longs off highs** — the theory is about respecting session levels "whether liquidity is taken out or not"; a push *up into a session low* (from below) can be shorted (gold example). [34:55] [35:29]
- London is the shortest session. [07:46]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Mark previous session highs/lows (30m body-to-wick boxes) before/near the next session open. [13:19]
- WHEN price interacts with/hits a box after the new session opens THEN drop from 30m to **5m** and wait for a BOS away from the box. [13:19] [13:52]
- Long: low swept + 5m BOS up → enter on the close of the BOS candle; stop under the lows (sweep low); add ~2 pips for spread. [13:52] [21:57] [26:31]
- Short: push into high + 5m BOS down → enter on BOS candle close; stop above the high (safer: above the higher high). [16:45] [36:03]
- TP1 = previous session high/low (opposite side). If too far or counter-trend, use an FVG, OB or EQ in between. Further TPs at higher highs / hourly lows / long-term levels. [13:52] [14:24] [36:03]
- Partial at TP1 then stop to breakeven implied ("see if we get stopped out at breakeven"). [26:31]
- Reaction must be AT or close to the box: if price runs far past the low before finally breaking structure, don't take it ("we want to see a reaction off of the box"). [19:00] [19:33]
- Opposing setup while in a trade: if a new session high holds and gives a BOS down, cut the long (and optionally short). [27:41] [28:13]
- Skip if a BOS forms but an untaken high is right on top of the entry (no room). [31:32]
- Skip if highs and lows are "too far spread apart"; sessions sometimes offer nothing — be patient. [17:52] [30:58]
- Time: setups should come "right when Market opens ... within the first two hours at least"; late-Asian-session setups skipped. [23:35]
- Trade with HTF bias; counter-trend setups were the losses. If taking one against bias: bring TPs closer, take more partials. [17:19] [22:29] [38:49]
- Avoid chop ("chop chop chop ... bouncing back and forth"). [28:46]
- Forex strategy does NOT require a third confirmation here (entry on 5m BOS close). [13:52]

## Process / order of operations
1. Set TradingView to London time; know opens 00:00/08:00/13:00. [06:40]
2. 30m: box the prior session's high and low (body→wick). [12:46]
3. Check HTF bias (zoom out: HH/HL or LH/LL). [17:19] [38:15]
4. New session opens; wait for a box to be hit. [13:19]
5. 5m: wait for a BOS in the direction away from the box; it must react at/near the box. [13:52] [19:33]
6. Enter at the BOS candle close; stop beyond the extreme + spread. [21:57] [26:31]
7. TP1 previous session opposite level; TP2+ hourly/HTF levels. [36:03]
8. Manage: BE after TP1; cut if an opposing session-level BOS forms. [28:13]
