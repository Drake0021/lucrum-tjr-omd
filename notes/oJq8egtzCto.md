## Summary
Recap of a losing GBPJPY long and the continuing S&P long from Day 5.5 (TP2 and TP3 hit). The GJ trade followed the full accumulation/order-block model and every confluence. It lost only because the market changed direction and broke the uptrend. He calls this "the best loss to take": a loss with no execution mistake. He also says this "accumulation → distribution → change of trend" method will get its own standalone strategy video, and he reviews the gold trade, whose HTF bias stayed correct after a BE stop. The first ~1:50 is music/intro.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Where orders were filled** — the accumulation price range before a breakout leg. After the breakout, price should "draw back into where those orders were filled". [03:05]
- **Change of trend** — a structure break against the prior trend. He is bundling "accumulation, distribution, change of trend" into a new strategy video, using the same concepts explained differently. [03:38]–[04:11]
- **The losses we want to take** — losses where every confluence was met and the market simply changed direction, "flipping bias and sentiment". [05:17]–[06:23]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Model: accumulation → breakout leg (after a liquidity sweep and BOS up) → wait for price to revisit the accumulation / order block → wait for a 5-minute BOS → wait for a push back into the area and a reaction → enter. [03:05]–[04:44]
- Extra patience: he did not enter on the first reaction candle. He waited "an extra two candles" (a small dip) and entered on a later candle. [04:44], [05:50]
- Stop: under the 5-minute low. [04:44]
- Target: highs, assuming the uptrend continues. He admits the RR was "not necessarily a great risk reward". [04:44]
- Hourly trend is used as the bias check ("look at the hourly... trend up up up"). [05:17]
- Gold: a 4H retracement into the accumulation area that caused the initial leg, followed by a 5-minute BOS, gives continuation. A trade within the monthly bias can be held much further. [08:04]–[08:39]

## Process / order of operations
1. HTF (hourly) bias: uptrend.
2. Find the accumulation that preceded the breakout leg (with a liquidity sweep + BOS up).
3. Wait for price to revisit it.
4. Get a 5m BOS up, then a push back into the area and a reaction.
5. Wait for extra confirmation candles, enter, and put the stop under the 5m low. Target the highs.
