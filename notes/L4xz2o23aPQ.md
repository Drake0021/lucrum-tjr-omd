## Summary
Short lesson in TJR's 2026 "Path to Profitability" series on session times and the key New York-session time windows for trading US indexes (ES/NQ). He defines Asia, London and New York session boundaries (all Eastern time), spread hour, and then the intraday template: 9:30 open, 9:30-9:50 "manipulation" window, 9:50-10:10 entry window ("the macro"), done for the day if no trade by 10:30. He shows several recent ES examples where price manipulates a draw on liquidity in the first 20 minutes then delivers an entry around 10:00 toward the HTF draw.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Session times (Eastern time)** — Asian session 18:00-03:00; London session 03:00-08:30 (London pre-market opens 02:00, London open 03:00; "technically" London runs to 11:30 NY time but he ends each session when the next opens); New York pre-market 08:30-09:30; New York market open 09:30 to 17:00. [00:33] [01:09] [01:43]
- **8:30 pre-market** — marked because "very typically" there is pre-market manipulation at 8:30; that is when new money / US traders enter. [01:09]
- **Spread hour** — 17:00-18:00, no market open; spreads widen massively on CFD/forex/commodity brokers. [01:43] [02:15]
- **Session highs/lows as liquidity** — new money entering at each session open manipulates the prior session highs/lows and then reverses toward a draw on liquidity; session highs/lows usable as targets or as entry points (sweeps). [02:15] [02:47] [03:55]
- **Market open** — 9:30 a.m. Eastern. [03:22]
- **Manipulation time frame** — 9:30-9:50. Sometimes manipulation is 9:30-9:40 with an entry at 9:45. Manipulation can be of a HTF or LTF draw on liquidity. [03:22] [05:02]
- **Entry period / "the macro"** — 9:50-10:10, "typically the ideal entry time"; entry "for the overall hourly trend". [03:55] [05:02] [09:30]
- **PM session** — opens around 1:00 p.m.; TJR personally does not trade it. [03:55]
- **Cutoff** — "if I can't find a trade by 10:30, I'm done for the day" because the market tends to slow down. [05:02]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Trade only the New York session for US indexes (S&P 500 / Nasdaq). [00:33]
- Expect manipulation (a sweep of a HTF or LTF draw on liquidity) between 9:30 and 9:50; then look for the entry 9:50-10:10 in the direction of the reversal toward the draw on liquidity. [05:02]
- Times are guidelines, not hard limits: he takes trades as early as 9:45 and as late as 10:20; manipulation can complete a couple of minutes late (e.g. 9:52). [08:56] [09:30]
- If no trade found by 10:30, stop for the day. [05:02]
- High-impact news days are poor examples / less reliable for this template (he skips one). [08:18]

## Process / order of operations
1. Mark session highs/lows (Asia, London, NY pre-market) as draws on liquidity. [02:47]
2. At 9:30 open, watch for manipulation (sweep) into a draw on liquidity during 9:30-9:50. [05:02]
3. From ~9:50-10:10 look for the entry in the reversal direction toward the opposing HTF draw on liquidity. [05:02]
4. Quit at 10:30 if nothing. [05:02]
