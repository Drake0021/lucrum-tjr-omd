## Summary
A daily bias on S&P, GU, GJ and gold, followed by a recap of a GJ short. He partialled 50% at TP1 for about $19k and moved his stop to break even. The setup is what he calls the "TJR forex strategy". Daily structure was bearish and price had tapped a daily order block. London highs and then Asian highs were swept. He entered on a 1-minute BOS after a 5-minute BOS, and TP1 was at an order block. On S&P he would flip to long only after a 15-minute high forms and price closes above it. He again stresses following the daily trend over the weekly, as with gold.

## Concepts & definitions
- **15-minute change in structure as a bias-flip trigger** — HTF (daily, 4H) is bullish but the 1H is down. He would look for longs only once a 15m high forms and a candle closes above it. Without that, no trade. [00:00]–[01:07]
- **Order block** — The up-move (last leg up before the drop) that broke structure and then "collapsed on down". He describes "this leg up followed by this leg down pretty much being an order block". [02:14], [03:22]
- **Hourly equilibrium** — He expects price to "bonk" into hourly equilibrium, then wants a 5m close for a short. [02:48]
- **TJR forex strategy ("TJR 4X Strat")** — Daily bias plus a daily POI tap. Price sweeps the London session highs, then the Asian session highs, then a LTF break of structure gives the entry. TP1 is at an opposing order block. A variant: price takes out the prior New York highs during Asian session, then a 5m break continues the move. [07:57]–[09:05]
- **Daily over weekly** — "Yes the weekly is telling us bears, that don't matter because the daily is telling us bulls." Follow the daily until the daily breaks structure and the weekly shows continuation. [04:29]
- **Bias alignment upgrade** — If gold closes above the weekly level, flip the weekly to bullish so it aligns with the monthly bullish trend: "now we're catching some big dog trades". [06:09]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- IF daily and 4H are bullish but 1H is bearish, THEN wait for a 15m high to form and a close above it before looking for longs. If that does not happen, do not trade. Do not wait for a 4H close that falls outside his session hours. [00:35]–[01:07]
- IF the daily is overbought or retracing and the LTF gives little price action, THEN skip the pair (GU). [01:07]–[01:41]
- Short setup (GJ): daily and weekly bearish, price reacting from a bearish (daily) order block, and a 4H break. Wait for London and Asian highs (or pre-market New York highs) to be swept, then enter on a LTF BOS down. [02:14]–[03:54], [07:57]
- Entry timeframe: the 5m showed a BOS, but he entered on the 1m BOS candle because he "loved the movement". [08:31]
- TP1 is at the next order block in profit. At TP1 he closes 50% and moves the stop to break even. [08:31], [09:38]
- Remaining position targets: New York lows and previous London session lows. [09:05]
- Gold: long only. Ideal entry is an hourly order block (the one that caused the BOS up) after the London high is taken. Targets are liquidity above, toward the weekly highs. [05:03]–[05:35]

## Process / order of operations
1. Check the HTF trend on each pair: daily, weekly and monthly where relevant. [00:00], [06:09]
2. Mark the daily order block, equilibrium and session highs/lows (London, Asian, pre-market NY). [02:14]–[03:22]
3. Wait for the session-high sweep into the POI. [03:54]
4. Look for a LTF BOS (5m or 1m) to enter. [08:31]
5. Take TP1 at an order block, close 50% and move the stop to BE. [09:38]
