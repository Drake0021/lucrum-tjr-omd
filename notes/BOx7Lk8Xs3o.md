## Summary
TJR lays out a simple, explicitly step-by-step index strategy. He says it is "not necessarily exactly how I trade" but works well and was one of the "mock trades" in a recent market recap. The steps: (1) HTF liquidity sweep on the 1H or 4H, with no daily bias needed for this strategy; (2) LTF SMT divergence on the 5m or 15m (not 1m; HTF SMT even better); (3) go to the "lagging index"; (4) 1m BOS or IFVG; (5) optional but recommended "third confluence" (FVG / OB / breaker / EQ) → enter. Trades only after 9:50 ET. He backtests the week of Jul 1–5, 2024 on ES/NQ (Mon Jul 1, Wed Jul 3, Fri Jul 5 NFP) and concludes it is "probably for the best if we wait for that third confluence". He uses a basketball analogy: the confluences are an arsenal of moves against the market.

## Concepts & definitions
- **HTF liquidity sweep** — a sweep of 1H or 4H highs/lows (on either index). [02:18] [03:55] [07:11]
- **LTF SMT** — on the 5m or 15m; "I would prefer not to go any lower than the 5 minute, like 1 minute SMT pretty freaking weak"; an SMT on the same TF as the sweep (1H) or higher is "even better, higher time frame holds higher power". [03:55]–[04:29] [16:52]
- **Lagging index** — the index that made the new extreme (lower low in a bullish setup) while the other made a higher low; you execute on it. E.g. NQ low at 10:15 → higher low at 10:55; ES low at 10:15 → lower low at 10:55 → ES is lagging → trade ES. [08:56]–[09:31]
- **Third confluence** — "I call this like third confluence but it's really just fair value gap, order block, breaker block, equilibrium" after the BOS/IFVG; optional, "more so on the safer side". [05:01]
- **Order block (in example)** — "this down candle turns into an order block because technically this is a sweep then a break"; the blue candle next to it = breaker block. [10:39]
- **Macro start** — 9:50 ET: "950 is when the macro starts". [08:20]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
1. HTF (1H or 4H) liquidity sweep. With this specific strategy, daily bias is "not necessarily" needed. [02:18] [03:23]–[03:55]
2. LTF SMT on the 5m or 15m (not 1m). [03:55]–[04:29]
3. Go to the lagging index. [04:29]
4. On the 1m: BOS or IFVG. [04:29] [09:31]
5. Optional: third confluence (FVG / OB / breaker / EQ) retrace with rejection → enter. [05:01]
- Time filter: only after 9:50 ET; "I rarely will ever take a trade before 9:50". A pre-market setup = "would I have taken this trade? Probably not". [08:20] [17:25] [18:00]
- About 10:30 is late: "now we're getting into like 10:30 territory, I'll probably be off the charts by then". [20:12]
- Stop: under the low that formed the BOS / under the FVG / under the lows ("stops underneath here"). [11:12] [17:25] [23:39]
- Targets: LTF draws on liquidity (previous highs), then HTF highs; R:R examples 1:3.6, 1:5, 1:1, 1:1.5, 1:2, 1:2.75. At all-time highs with no liquidity above, use a fib extension. [11:12] [20:44] [22:32]
- Take profits along the way: "if you were a little bit greedy and didn't take any profits ... probably would have ... got stopped out". [22:32]–[23:06]
- After an extension, he would want price to retrace into EQ (bottom of range → high) before looking for the BOS. [18:32]
- When the move off the BOS is a "big ass candle", wait for a better entry (FVG retrace). [17:59]
- Conclusion from backtest: waiting for the third confluence is "probably for the best". It kept him out of a BOS entry that would have been stopped (NFP day). [23:06] [24:43]
- Don't run a rigid step-by-step program; have all the confluences in your head, "a tool for every problem". [11:48]

## Process / order of operations
1. Mark 1H/4H highs/lows on ES and NQ; wait for a sweep. [07:11]
2. After the sweep (after 9:50), compare 5m/15m swings at the same timestamps on ES and NQ → SMT → pick the lagging index. [08:20]–[09:31]
3. 1m on the lagging index: BOS or IFVG. [09:31]–[10:06]
4. Wait for the FVG/OB/breaker/EQ retrace and rejection → enter. [10:39]–[11:12]
5. Stop below the swing low / FVG; TP at LTF draws, then HTF highs. [11:12]

## Changes vs. earlier teaching / contradictions
- Timeline: Jul 2024 (Trading Transformation era). The strategy is almost identical to the 2026 canonical: HTF (1H/4H) sweep → SMT → 1m BOS/IFVG → FVG/EQ (plus OB/breaker here) → TP at draws on liquidity; entries after 9:50.
- **Daily bias not required** for this strategy ("do we need to have a daily bias with this? For me not necessarily"). This contradicts d5uOjnuzYhE ("SMT is nothing without bias", one week later). [03:23]
- **SMT index choice**: trade the lagging index = the one that made the new extreme / swept (ES made the lower low → long ES). This is consistent with d5uOjnuzYhE and Qk-mHUlSJGU, and opposite to the brief's 2026 wording. [09:31] [21:56]
- SMT timeframe: 5m/15m; 1m SMT "pretty freaking weak"; 1H SMT better. [04:29] [16:52]
- Third confluence: optional but recommended after backtest, matching 2024–25 "third confluence". Order blocks are still used (sweep-then-break candle), and breakers too. [05:01] [10:39] [24:43]
- **Entry time**: rarely before 9:50; 10:30 is "territory where I'll probably be off the charts" (answers OPEN: 7, partially; the entry cutoff is about 10:30) [08:20] [20:12]
- **News days**: walks through an NFP day as a valid example ("sometimes news gives good entries") but doesn't say he traded it. Wed Jul 3 had 10:00 news; he waited for after the release. [21:19] [18:32]
- TP timeframe: LTF draws first (1m/5m highs), then HTF highs; fib extension at ATH. [11:12] [20:44]
- Not stated: stop in points, contracts/risk %, daily limits.
