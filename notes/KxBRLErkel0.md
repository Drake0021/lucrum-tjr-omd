## Summary
TJR presents his May-2025 "aggressive" 1-minute scalping entry, used *in addition to* (not instead of) his standard 5m strategy (5m BOS → FVG/breaker/EQ/OB fill → 1m entry). Preconditions: a strong bias with two strong draws on liquidity (HTF draw → LTF low-resistance liquidity or another HTF draw; or LTF low-resistance → HTF draw). After the first draw is swept at the open he drops to the 1m and requires (a) a "confirmation confluence" — 1m BOS, 1m IFVG, or a close beyond the 79% Fibonacci "extension" level — then (b) a "continuation confluence" — retrace into FVG/EQ/OB/breaker with a closing candle in trade direction. He de-risks (~half the day's risk) on the aggressive entry, keeping the rest for the slower 5m setup. Stops at the invalidation (beyond the sweep), targets at the next draws on liquidity. Examples on NQ and ES from the prior week, including an ES-vs-NQ SMT mention.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Two strategies coexist** — "higher time frame strategy": on 5m look for BOS, then FVG / breaker block / equilibrium / order block fill, then scale to 1m for entry. Still "completely valid ... I still use it on a daily basis." The new aggressive 1m strategy gets "skin in the game" earlier. [00:34–01:40]
- **Strong bias + strong draws on liquidity** — prerequisite. No aggressive trade if bias is weak, "price could go in either direction," or the market is choppy. Need **two** draws: option A = HTF draw (e.g., 4H low) swept → target LTF low-resistance liquidity and/or another HTF draw; option B = LTF low-resistance liquidity swept → target the HTF draw. [05:36–07:55]
- **Low-resistance liquidity ("trend line liquidity")** — a stack of highs (or lows) close to each other; market prefers to take all at once ("three times the amount of orders"). A distant swing separated by "a huge gap" is not part of the stack. Applies on HTF and 5m. [20:06–22:54]
- **Why it works (his logic)** — when an HTF draw is swept, profits are taken → price either bounces to LTF liquidity or reverses to continue the HTF trend; when LTF low-resistance liquidity is swept toward an obvious HTF draw, that sweep is the fuel to reach the HTF draw. [09:33–12:20]
- **Confirmation confluences (1m)** — any one of: break of structure, inverse FVG ("the bearish order flow ... got closed above"), or **closure beyond the 79% extension** ("another example of us disrespecting [the prior] order flow"). [08:28; 12:55–13:29]
- **79% extension** — draw a Fibonacci "just like a regular Fibonacci ... from the high down to the low as if ... drawing a equilibrium" across the swing into the sweep; a candle **close** above the 0.79 level (for longs; below for shorts) counts as confirmation even without BOS/IFVG. He shows his Fib settings on screen (not captured). First time explained on YouTube. [24:32–25:05]
- **Continuation confluences** — after confirmation, price retraces into FVG, equilibrium, order block or breaker block and a candle closes in trade direction → enter. "We don't need every single one of those to get hit." [13:29–14:02; 27:19]
- **Invalidation stop** — "exit where trade idea gets invalidated." [14:36]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Only use the aggressive 1m entry when bias is strong and there are two strong draws on liquidity; never in chop. [02:47; 06:10]
- Risk: aggressive entry at a **de-risked** size, "around like half of what I'm willing to risk for the day"; the remaining half is for the 5m higher-confluence entry (or a re-entry if the aggressive entry stops out). Total day risk = both together. [03:55–04:28]
- Sequence: first draw swept (at/after the open) → 1m confirmation (BOS / IFVG / close beyond 79%) → 1m continuation (FVG/EQ/OB/breaker fill + candle close in trade direction) → enter. [07:55–14:02; 16:15]
- If a 1m BOS forms but no continuation confluence fills, no entry (price ran without retrace). [23:28–24:00]
- If the first BOS gets no continuation and price comes back, keep waiting for a fresh BOS/IFVG/79% close. [31:48]
- Stop: under the sweep lows / under the candle wick (long). [32:23]
- Targets: LTF low-resistance liquidity, hourly highs, 4H high/HTF draw. In option B "our one and really only target should just be this high time frame draw." If HTF draws are themselves stacked (low-resistance), skip the nearer one and use the farthest as the single TP. [16:48; 17:54; 31:16]
- 5m strategy re-entry/add: only take the 5m entry if the draw on liquidity has not already been taken out; if it has, "just take profits ... and be completely satisfied." Don't take the 5m entry when it appears "so late in the day" after the move/draws already hit. [26:11; 33:32]
- He doesn't like entering right at the open ("I don't like entering right when the market opens"); one example shown is "during a time that I wouldn't necessarily be willing to trade." [23:28; 29:34] (vague on exact times)

## Process / order of operations
1. Pre-market: determine a strong bias and mark draws: HTF (4H/1H) highs/lows, stacked HTF lows, 5m stacked highs/lows, pre-market lows. [18:27; 22:19; 28:27]
2. Confirm two-draw structure (A: HTF→LTF/HTF; B: LTF low-resistance→HTF). [06:46–07:21]
3. Wait for the first draw to be swept after the 9:30 open. [15:09]
4. Drop to 1m; need BOS or IFVG or close beyond 79%. [16:15]
5. Wait for retrace into FVG/EQ/OB/breaker + close in direction; enter at ~half day risk. [16:15–16:48]
6. Stop at invalidation (beyond sweep); TPs at next draws. [16:48]
7. Optionally add the 5m-strategy entry (5m BOS → 5m FVG fill → 1m BOS/IFVG/79%) with the other half if the draw is not yet hit. [32:59–34:09]
