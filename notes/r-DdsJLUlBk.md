## Summary
Recap of an ES short (TP1 at pre-market lows, rest BE) and a gold short still in drawdown. He took no trade the previous day (PMI news, no setup he liked). The bias was bearish overall. Unemployment claims gave a bearish bias, and most of the pairs he traded agreed. The model is "areas of accumulation after our high time frame building blocks get hit". The lesson: be confident in your analysis. On gold, the sweep + change of structure + FVG fill + accumulation fill + reaction should have been enough to enter earlier with better RR. A loss from a pure direction change is "the best loss".

## Concepts & definitions
- **HTF building block hit → LTF accumulation** — the 2.0 model: wait for a high-timeframe building block (e.g., an HTF order block) to be hit, then trade the lower-timeframe change of trend and its area of accumulation. [01:40]
- **Stacked confluences** — "order block, fair value gap, equilibrium" all at the entry zone. [01:40]
- **Valid liquidity sweep vs TP** — TP1 at pre-market lows. After TP1, price rallied because that was a valid liquidity sweep. [02:14]
- **Best losses** — when all confluences align with your bias, RR is good, and the only thing wrong is price changing direction. That is not a stop-placement mistake or other error. [06:42]–[07:16]

## Process / order of operations
1. Overall bias (4H/daily/weekly; news direction).
2. Mark HTF building blocks (order blocks) and LTF OBs.
3. Wait for price to hit the HTF block.
4. LTF change of trend / BOS in the bias direction.
5. Enter on a reaction at the stacked zone (FVG/OB/EQ). Stop beyond the swing.
6. TP1 at nearby session liquidity (pre-market lows) → 50% off, BE.
