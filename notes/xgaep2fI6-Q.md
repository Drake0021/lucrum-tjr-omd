## Summary
A short career-path video on capital. The path: start on demo/paper (no live money) and learn from his free Boot Camp / Trading Transformation. After about 3 straight months of 5–10% gains on demo, choose (a) a prop-firm funded account if you have little capital ($100–$1,000 challenges), or (b) a live futures account with a $10k bare minimum. No strategy content.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Demo / paper trading** — trading without real money; the first stage for everyone. [00:33] [01:07]
- **Funded account / prop firm challenge** — pay a fee (e.g. about $1,000 for $200k, about $100 for $6k), hit a profit target without breaching drawdown, then get paid out; many firms refund the fee. [02:48]–[04:29]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- No live capital (funded or live) when you first start; demo first. [00:33]
- Readiness threshold for going live / funded: about 3 months of consistent gains, 5–10% on a demo account. He would not call that "fully profitable", but it gives "the confidence of a profitable trader". [01:41]–[02:14]
- Low capital (≤ about $1,000) → prop firm. [02:48]
- Live futures account minimum: about $10,000. [05:34]

## Process / order of operations
1. Learn from the free courses (Boot Camp, Trading Transformation) and apply on demo. [01:41]
2. 3 months of 5–10% demo gains. [02:14]
3. Funded challenge (low capital) or live account ($10k+). [02:48] [05:34] [06:42]
