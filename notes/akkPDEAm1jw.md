## Summary
TJR compares futures prop-firm account types at Lucid (Pro/Flex/Direct), Tradeify (Growth/Select/Lightning) and Alpha Futures (Zero/Advanced/Direct), using 150K accounts. He then gives his own risk plan. **Evaluation stage:** risk the entire max loss limit on one trade at 1:2 R (one-day-pass accounts), or at 1:1 R per day (accounts with an eval consistency rule), entering only when his strategy presents itself. **Funded stage:** risk 1/4 to 1/8 of the max loss limit per trade, then scale down to farm minimum winning days or meet the consistency rule. No chart analysis or trades. The risk/sizing content is what matters for a bot.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Prop firm / eval** — pay a fee, hit a profit target without breaching the max loss limit, then trade the funded stage for payouts (firm keeps 10–20%). "90% of traders end up failing" [00:33–01:38].
- **Max loss limit (MLL)** — breach = account lost. **Daily loss limit (DLL)** — breach = done for the day (the firm closes the trade), account not lost [04:27–05:34].
- **Consistency rule** — the largest single trade/day profit as % of total profit must be ≤ X% (e.g., 40% needs ≥3 roughly equal wins; 50% needs ≥2) [07:46–10:36].
- **Days to payout / minimum winning days** — e.g., Lucid Pro funded = 3 trading days; Lucid Flex funded = 5 winning days of ≥$250 (150K) [10:36; 15:04].
- 150K figures quoted: Lucid Pro eval profit target $9,000 (caption also says "$900", likely mis-transcribed, inferred), MLL $4,500, DLL $2,700, fee ~$222; funded min profit $1,000 for payout, 40% consistency, 3 days [04:27–10:36]. Lucid Flex: no DLL; 50% consistency on eval; funded no consistency, 5 winning days ≥$250 [12:50–15:04]. Lucid Direct: 20% consistency, ~5 equal wins; he dislikes it [16:11–17:17]. Tradeify Growth ≈ Lucid Pro but consistency "5% lower than Lucid" (≈35%, inferred) and 5-day payout; Select: 40% eval consistency, funded "daily" or "flex" path (he prefers flex); Lightning: 20% consistency [17:52–20:37]. Alpha Direct: 20% consistency, $2,000 payout cap, $4,000 MLL; Zero: 1-day pass; Advanced: 40% eval consistency, 3 days min, funded 5 min days, $15,000 max payout [20:37–22:16].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- **Eval, one-day-pass accounts (Lucid Pro, Tradeify Growth, Alpha Zero):** risk the full MLL ($4,500 on 150K) on one trade at **1:2 R** to hit the $9,000 target in one day [23:56–25:41].
- **Eval, consistency-rule accounts (Lucid Flex 50%, Tradeify Select / Alpha Advanced 40%):** risk the full MLL at **1:1 R** per day, one trade per day, to pass in 2 days (50%) or 3 days (40%) [26:47–27:58].
- Only take that max-risk trade when "my strategy presents itself". No blind entries [25:06].
- Rationale: an eval can only end in pass or fail and makes no money, so reach the outcome fastest. Slow-rolling leads to upsizing after wins and giving gains back [22:49–24:32; 28:33–30:47].
- **Funded stage:** risk **1/4 to 1/8 of the MLL** per trade (e.g., MLL $2,000 → $500 or $250), on every firm [31:20–32:28].
- Funded with consistency rule (e.g., Lucid Pro): steady equal-sized wins (e.g., $250 × 3 days) to meet consistency and days-to-payout [32:28–33:01].
- Funded without consistency (Flex/Select/Advanced): start at 1/4 MLL ($500). After the first big win, or once in profit, scale down to the minimum winning-day amount (e.g., risk $150 for ≥$150 days) to collect the remaining winning days, then request the payout [33:36–34:47].
- Prefer accounts with no DLL when trying to pass quickly. He favors eval-then-funded over direct-funding accounts [16:11; 12:50].

## Process / order of operations
1. Pick an account type for your style. One-day-pass evals are fastest [06:06].
2. Eval: wait for a setup; risk the full MLL; target 2R (1-day pass) or 1R per day (consistency eval) [25:06; 27:22].
3. Funded: risk 1/4–1/8 MLL; manage for consistency and minimum days; request the payout [31:20–34:47].

## Risk & psychology rules
- Eval: maximum risk, aiming for fast pass or fail; he calls this "a little bit reckless" himself [02:12].
- Funded: conservative, 1/4–1/8 of MLL per trade; "this is the account that we actually want to treasure" [31:20].

## Changes vs. earlier teaching / contradictions
- Timeline: Jul 2026, CANONICAL era. Sizing is expressed in prop-firm dollars (fraction of MLL), not % of account. This contradicts the Boot Camp's 1%/day risk (§6) for eval accounts. Funded-stage risk of 1/8–1/4 of the MLL is far above 1% of nominal balance (e.g., $500 on a 50K = 1%; $500 on MLL $2,000 = 25% of drawdown).
- **Daily trade limit:** the eval plan uses one trade per day. This partially **answers OPEN 20 (daily max loss)** for prop accounts: the effective daily loss cap = the per-trade risk (eval: whole MLL; funded: 1/4–1/8 MLL), since one trade per day is implied (inferred).
- **TP R-multiple:** 1:2 (one-day eval) or 1:1 (consistency eval) [25:06; 27:22]. That is a fixed-R target, a different framing from "targets at DOLs".
- Not mentioned: news days, SMT, TP timeframe, OB, entry time, stop size in points, contract counts.
