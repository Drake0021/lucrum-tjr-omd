## Summary
A live session on Wednesday 2025-04-30, recorded from ~9:35 ET. GDP/GDP price index came out at 8:30; PCE price index and pending home sales at 10:00; NFP was due Friday. The 8:30 news flushed price down through the "low resistance liquidity" (which he equates with **trend-line liquidity**) after a bearish SMT at the Asian/London highs (ES lower high, NQ higher high). With the targets nearly achieved, he expected only a small LTF relief or more downside. He explains his newer **aggressive half-position entry**: enter half on an early signal (here an inverse gap ~10:01 ET, after the 10:00 news), with a tight stop. The second half is reserved for the full confirmation sequence: 5m order flow disrespected (5m BOS or close above the 79% extension) → 5m continuation confluence (FVG/OB/breaker/EQ) → 1m break. The ES long was managed with partials at the highs (+$9K), the breaker (+$10K), more partials, and a final TP when NQ took its highs (~10:29 ET). Total +$31K. He never added the second half because it was getting late.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Low resistance liquidity = trend-line liquidity** — "a whole bunch of lows that are stacked up within a trend that are super easy and super achievable that tend to get swept out within one massive move" [01:12].
- **Bearish SMT at session highs** — ES lower high vs NQ higher high → sends price down [00:02].
- **Post-flush behavior** — after a big move takes a cluster of liquidity, "it's very rarely a V-shaped recovery". The typical sequence is "down, chop up, down, up, or down, chop, sideways, down, up" [13:40–14:14].
- **Midnight open** — "we already balanced that out" (price already traded through it) [03:30].
- **Aggressive half entry** — "entering half of my position first on like an aggressive entry ... it gives me the opportunity to be aggressive on the first entry and then wait for full confirmation on the remainder." If stopped, "I still have my second jump shot for like full confirmation" [26:45–27:52].
- **Full confirmation sequence (for a HTF / 1-hour retrace)** — "I would want to see five minute order flow disrespected by either a break of structure ... or a closure above the 79% extension. And then ... wait for a five minute continuation confluence, so either five minute fair value gap, five minute order block, five minute breaker block or equilibrium to get hit. And then following that, I would need one minute ... break in order flow to the upside" [29:48–31:29].
- **Don't enter a HTF-retrace idea off a 1m FVG** — that "would kind of ruin the purpose of trying to get this higher time frame retracement" [43:20].
- **RSI divergence** — "similar to SMT divergence" but on an indicator. He used it in forex and warns that indicators lag, so backtests make them look 100% right. He does not use it; he dislikes buy/sell-signal indicators [21:14–23:30].
- **TJR indicators** — session highs/lows, draws on liquidity (set to 1H highs), FVG, SMT, BOS. He personally uses only the session H/L one [11:24–11:59].
