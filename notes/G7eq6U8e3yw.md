## Summary
TJR recaps a GJ (GBPJPY) forex-strategy short taken at only 0.25% risk because of high-impact US news (Powell). He waited for the London highs to actually be taken out, not just tapped, then entered on the close of the BOS-down candle with a stop 4 pips above the highs. News spiked price about 6 pips further and stopped him out within about 20 minutes, after which price moved his way. His lesson is to wait for high-impact US news to come out first. He also took profitable ES and gold scalps that he will not show or endorse. The rest is risk: know your risk in dollars, use a fixed lot or calculated-risk approach, trade demo until profitable, and only risk money you can lose. He stops disclosing P&L because he is moving to a new broker with a larger account.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Forex strategy short (GJ)** — Price pushes into the London highs. A BOS on the tap alone was not enough here; he wanted the highs taken out. Then a BOS down, entry on the close of that candle, and the stop 4 pips above the highs. [02:13]
- **De-risked "reps" trades** — Trades under 1% risk are treated as practice or reps. He is unbothered when they lose. [06:42]–[07:15]
- **Risk management methods** — (a) a set lot size for each pair, (b) calculating risk for every trade, or (c) an estimated loss size with a few preset lot sizes depending on how he feels. His own approach: a fixed lot size, usually 100–150 lots on forex. He used 25 lots on this trade. His usual risk tolerance is about $20k per trade. [11:41]–[12:48]
- **Risk math example** — 1% of a $1,000 account is $10; 0.25% is $2.50. [05:36]–[06:09]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)

## Risk & psychology rules
- Know exactly what you are risking, in dollars, before every trade. [11:41]
- If a 0.25% or 1% loss makes you emotional, you over-risked or did not understand your risk, and you should not be trading live. [06:09], [15:03]
- A profitable trader is unfazed by losing days, weeks or even months. One great month (January: undefeated, about +40%) can cover several losing months. [08:23]–[08:56], [10:03]
- Trade as if you were a profitable trader: after a loss, "I'll make it back". [09:29]
- If you are unprofitable and trading live with money you need, fix that immediately. [13:55]
- An emotional trader with money they cannot lose is effectively gambling, and that leads to revenge trading. He says they are better off at a casino or on demo. [15:35]–[17:13]
- He will no longer disclose P&L dollar amounts so viewers focus on the trade, not the money. [00:37]–[01:41]
