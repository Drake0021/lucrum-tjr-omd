## Summary
Continuation of Day 31: TJR completes his forex session strategy and backtests it on GBPJPY, GBPUSD and gold. Mark 30m session highs/lows (Asian 20:00, London 03:00, NY 08:00 ET), drop to the 5m, wait for price to hit/revisit a prior session level, then require a 5m BOS plus a "third confluence" (FVG, equilibrium, order block, breaker) — the same structure as his futures model. If price closes beyond a session level on the 30m, the level "flips" and you look for a revisit from the other side. Targets are other session highs/lows (draws on liquidity). Results: GBPJPY ~4W/2L, GBPUSD mostly losses/no trades, gold 2W/1L in NY. He says the futures strategy also works on forex. Low direct relevance for ES, but it shows his generic sweep → BOS → confluence logic.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Session high/low revisit** — When a session level is broken, next trade chance is a revisit of that session level. [06:50]
- **Flip** — "you could literally just wait for a 30-minute closure above" a session level → treat it as flipped (old high becomes support). [17:06][24:57]
- **Session candle assignment** — A wick belongs to the session in which that candle occurs; a high made by a candle in the next session is not part of the prior session's high/low. [19:27][20:00]
- **Third confluence** — FVG, equilibrium, order block, breaker block — "anything"; required after BOS. [07:24]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- At session-level hit/revisit: need 5m BOS, then a confluence fill and reaction; BOS without the imbalance getting filled and moved off of → ignore. [07:24][07:56]
- If no confluence (no FVG, EQ, breaker) after BOS → no trade. [30:49][35:02]
- Stop: under the low formed out of the imbalance, under the FVG, or above equilibrium/highs; he shows multiple options and how stop choice changes outcome. [14:14][18:15][32:44][39:34]
- Targets: previous session highs/lows (draws on liquidity); reject targets with negative RR; Fibonacci extensions if nothing to target. [08:29][14:50][18:15]
- Avoid trading Asian session (low volume) — though he backtests it. [11:24][28:30]
- Entry "so high up" / too extended → he probably wouldn't take it. [07:56][10:13]
- Use market intuition/trend: avoid shorts in an obvious uptrend. [22:21]
- News candles distort examples. [33:18][33:53]

## Process / order of operations
1. Mark session opens and 30m session highs/lows (NY time). [01:45][04:01]
2. Drop to 5m; wait for a session level to be hit/swept or broken and revisited. [06:18]
3. 5m BOS. [07:24]
4. Third confluence fill (FVG/EQ/OB/breaker) → enter. [07:24]
5. Stop beyond the confluence/low; target other session highs/lows. [08:29]
