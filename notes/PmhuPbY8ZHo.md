## Summary
Core definitional lesson on swing highs/lows and break of structure (BOS), shown on gold, GU (GBPUSD) and GJ on daily/4h/weekly/15m. TJR defines a high as a move up then a move down (minimum two candles: an up candle then a down candle, high = the higher wick of the two) and a low as the mirror. A BOS is a trend shift confirmed only by a candle BODY CLOSE beyond the MOST RECENT high/low; wicks through a level are not BOS and are usually liquidity sweeps that fake traders out. He gives a 5-step checklist: identify trend; in an uptrend watch the most recent low; in a downtrend watch the most recent high; BOS down = close below most recent low; BOS up = close above most recent high. Higher-timeframe structure still dominates: a daily BOS down inside a bullish weekly is just a retracement.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Break of structure (simplest form)** — "a shift in the trend"; he uses BOS and "market structure shift" interchangeably here. [01:07], [06:39]
- **High (swing high)** — a move up then a move down; minimally two candles: one up (bullish) candle followed by one down (bearish) candle. The high's price is the highest wick of whichever of those two candles is higher. Does not need to look "pretty" or symmetric; don't lump several candles together into a "bigger" high. [01:39]-[04:58]
- **Low (swing low)** — a move down then a move up; one down candle followed by one up candle; the low is the lowest wick of the two. [02:15], [04:58]
- **Lower-timeframe check of a swing point** — zooming into the 4h can confirm which wick is the true low. [05:33]-[06:04]
- **BOS to the upside** — in a downtrend, a single candle closes fully above the most recent high: the candle BODY must close above the WICK of the high. [07:47]-[08:20]
- **BOS to the downside** — in an uptrend, a candle body closes below the most recent low. [11:46], [16:38]-[17:44]
- **Not a BOS** — a wick above a high or below a low without a body close beyond it; "we do not care about wicks going above highs or going below lows". Such wicks are typically **liquidity sweeps** that fake people out. [12:21]-[13:33], [23:22]-[23:58], [26:15]
- **Most recent swing only** — only the most recently formed high/low matters; older highs/lows are ignored for BOS. [08:54], [17:44]-[18:52], [27:26]-[28:33]
- **Updating the swing high** — a still-forming candle or its wick is not a high; if the next candle is down, then the high moves there. [20:35]-[21:08]
- **Consolidation** — alternating highs and lows with no trend and no BOS; "terrible trading week" (gold example). [19:27]-[20:01]
- **HTF dominance over BOS** — monthly > weekly > daily; a daily BOS down while the weekly is still bullish is "just a retracement"; BOS does not mean price goes one way forever. [22:14]-[22:47]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- A BOS requires a candle CLOSE (body) beyond the most recent swing high/low — never a wick. "It is not a break of structure unless the candle closes above a high or below a low." [12:57]
- In an uptrend, watch only the most recent low; in a downtrend, watch only the most recent high. [14:40]-[15:13], [26:48]
- Wait for the close on the timeframe you're reading (e.g., wait for the daily candle to close above the most recent high to call an uptrend). [20:35]-[21:08]
- Do not short a wick below a low ("good enough, sell") — wait for the low to be closed below; taking such setups lowers probability. [23:58]-[24:33]
- Don't treat a BOS as permanent direction; check it against the higher timeframe (a counter-HTF BOS may just be a retracement). [22:14]-[22:47]

## Process / order of operations
BOS checklist (he types it on screen): [14:07]-[17:44], [26:48]-[29:07]
1. Identify the trend (HH/HL = uptrend; LH/LL = downtrend).
2. If uptrend → watch the (most recent) lows.
3. If downtrend → watch the (most recent) highs.
4. BOS to the downside (uptrend → downtrend) = candle closes below the most recent low.
5. BOS to the upside (downtrend → uptrend) = candle closes above the most recent high.
Always track the newest swing points and forget the old ones.
