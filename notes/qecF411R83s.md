## Summary
This video puts FVGs together with liquidity and BOS in real chart examples, including TJR's live S&P 500 short that day. The pattern is: higher-timeframe (15m/1H/4H) BOS sets direction, price retraces into the higher-timeframe FVG, then a lower-timeframe BOS (5m/1m) gives the entry. Stop goes beyond the local high. Targets are prior liquidity, accumulation areas or imbalances. He says he takes no trades except at or after the market open. FVGs are his "patient entry" and retracement tool, used when the initial move offers poor risk:reward. An FVG that price trades fully through is no longer valid. If candle wicks have partly filled an FVG, only the truly unfilled portion counts. He uses imbalances more as confirmation than as entries.

## Concepts & definitions
- **FVG validity (recap)** — There is no FVG where one candle's wick overlaps another's. He checks each gap after the BOS and discards the overlapped ones. [01:41], [02:12]
- **Refining the true imbalance** — When a later wick has already filled part of an imbalance, only the remaining untouched part (from that wick to the far edge) is the real FVG. He prioritised a 1H FVG over a 4H one because the 1H wick showed the 4H gap was already filled. [11:59], [12:33]
- **Invalidation** — If price rallies "all the way up through this, past it", the FVG is no longer considered ("not of interest to me"). [09:09], [09:45]
- **Lower-timeframe BOS against the higher-timeframe trend = retracement** — A 5m BOS up while the 15m and 1H are bearish doesn't mean a full trend shift. It signals the higher-timeframe retracement is starting, so look for that retracement to fill the higher-timeframe FVG and then short. [06:15], [06:48]
- **Market open** — A key time reference (value explained later in the course). [02:12]
- **Doji** — Indecision. He uses it while waiting for a BOS (references the candlestick lesson). [13:06], [13:40]
- **Accumulation / distribution** — He references the "accumulation that caused the rally" as a target or reaction area. To be covered later. [11:27]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Time filter: "we're not going to take any trades that aren't off of market open or aren't after market open". Pre-market moves are ignored for execution. [02:50]
- Model: (1) higher-timeframe BOS / trend shift, (2) higher-timeframe FVG filled on the retracement, (3) scale down for a lower-timeframe BOS, (4) optionally scale down again (for example 5m FVG then 1m BOS) for a "prime time" entry. Enter on the candle close that breaks structure. [03:25]–[04:00]
- Don't enter just because price taps the FVG, and don't enter on a BOS alone without the retracement. In the ES example both would have been stopped out. [04:36], [05:10]
- FVG retracement entries are the "patient entry": if a liquidity sweep plus BOS dumps without giving good risk:reward, wait for a retracement into the FVG, then a lower-timeframe BOS. [05:10]
- Stop above the highs (for shorts) formed on the retracement into the FVG. [06:48]
- Targets: previous areas of liquidity, accumulation zones, an order block, or an imbalance's start, 50% or end. Use multiple TPs. [04:36], [06:48], [10:20]
- Management in the ES trade: TP1 and TP2 hit, then the remainder was stopped at break even (inferred: stop moved to BE after the partials). [04:36]
- Liquidity plus FVG confluence: price spiking into liquidity that lines up with a higher-timeframe imbalance, then a 15m BOS, gives a short. [09:45], [10:20]
- Ideal extra: a liquidity sweep inside the FVG before the lower-timeframe BOS. [12:33], [15:54]
- Imbalances are mainly retracement tools. Look for other reasons to enter, such as an order block, a BOS, or a liquidity sweep inside the imbalance followed by a BOS. "I use it as more of a confirmation rather than an entry." [15:54]

## Process / order of operations
1. Set higher-timeframe direction (daily, 4H, 1H or 15m BOS).
2. Mark valid FVGs created by the displacement (check wick overlap and refine to the unfilled portion).
3. Wait for the retracement into the FVG (ideally after the market open, or with liquidity resting there or swept).
4. Scale down one timeframe (15m from 1H, 5m from 15m, 1m from 5m) and wait for a BOS in the trend direction.
5. Enter on the BOS candle close. Stop beyond the retracement high/low.
6. Target liquidity, accumulation or imbalances with partials. The runner goes to BE.

## Trade examples
- **Live trade, the video's day (date unknown, about June 2023) | S&P 500 (ES/SPX) | NY open | bias:** small uptrend, then a 15m BOS down leaves one valid 15m FVG. Pre-market tapped it with a BOS, but that was ignored (before the open). Then a lower low confirmed the downtrend. At the market open price pushed up into another imbalance (5m). **Confirmation:** 1m BOS on a candle close. **Entry:** short on that BOS. **Targets:** liquidity below, and a small order block. **Outcome:** both TPs hit, the rest stopped at break even. **Lesson:** entering on the first tap or on BOS alone would have been stopped out, so wait for the retracement plus lower-timeframe BOS. [01:41]–[05:10]
- **GBPJPY (GJ) | 15m/5m:** higher-timeframe highs swept, then a 15m BOS down, then two FVGs marked. A 5m BOS up was read as the start of a 15m retracement. Price filled the first imbalance, then a 5m BOS down. Short, stop above the highs, targets prior liquidity, an accumulation area, and imbalance/accumulation on 1H/4H. All hit. Summary: "liquidity sweep, break structure, imbalance fill, break of structure". [05:42]–[07:57]
- **GBPUSD 4H (he also says "gold" mid-example, so the instrument is ambiguous):** 4H BOS down leaves two imbalances. Price rallied through the first, so it was discarded. The second lined up with liquidity. Price spiked into the liquidity, then a 15m BOS down. Short. Targets: prior liquidity, the accumulation that started the rally, or the imbalance's start, 50% or end. [08:34]–[10:55]
- **Instrument not stated | 1H/4H/15m:** daily BOS up means bullish. A 1H BOS up, then a pullback into the accumulation. The 4H imbalance was partly filled by a 1H wick, so he prioritised the 1H imbalance's unfilled portion. Doji indecision, then a 15m BOS up (and 1H BOS) gives the long. A dip back into the FVG missed TP1. Then the rally took previous liquidity and filled the imbalances above, and the next target was the liquidity above. [10:55]–[14:47]
