## Summary
A no-chart discipline video about patience. TJR says impatience (chasing the dopamine hit of clicking buy or sell) leads to rushed, unconfirmed entries. The main fix is a written step-by-step trading plan, for example liquidity sweep, then BOS, then FVG entry, then reaction, then enter. If the steps aren't met, don't trade. Even when every step is met, skip the trade for news, or wait one or two more candles for price to develop. Come to the market expecting not to trade and let the market give you the trade. He takes about one trade a day. The homework is a 30-minute dark-room breathing meditation.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Dopamine of placing the trade** — The hit comes from placing the bet, not from winning (he cites a gambling study). This makes traders think only about potential profit. Also think about what you could lose. [01:09], [01:42], [02:15]
- **Trading plan as the patience mechanism** — A step-by-step list of conditions to execute. If the conditions aren't met, there is no trade, so patience is built into the rules. [03:22], [03:54]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Example plan he gives: (1) liquidity sweep, (2) break of structure, (3) wait for price to enter the fair value gap, (4) reaction, (5) enter. If none of these apply, don't trade. [03:22], [03:54]
- Approach each session thinking "I do not want to trade today". The market must give you a reason, and you should not invent one. [04:26], [05:01]
- Don't force your bias on the market. Check plan items objectively before judging whether it "looks good". [05:33]
- Even with every plan step met, you may skip the trade, for example if there's news. Be open to not trading and encourage it. [06:07]
- Skip low-quality trades, for example where only some conditions are met, it's against the daily bias and it's off a retracement. [07:46]
- It's fine to wait one or two extra candles after the execution points are hit, to let price develop and see volume or volatility before entering. [08:21]
- Take only high-probability setups. Don't enter on "one confluence". [02:49], [08:52]
- He takes about one trade a day: "you do not need to place 10 trades a day". [11:03]

## Process / order of operations
1. Have a written step-by-step execution checklist.
2. Come in expecting not to trade.
3. Tick off each plan condition objectively.
4. Check filters (news).
5. Optionally wait one or two more candles for confirmation.
6. Execute only when the market has "proved" the setup.

## Risk & psychology rules
- Don't trade with friends, because their biases differ. Trading is individual, and the same strategy and parameters still produce different trades for different people. [09:58]
- It took him two years to become profitable. Timelines differ from person to person. [09:25]
- Skill first, money later. [11:03]
- Homework: 30 minutes in a fully dark room, eyes closed, phone outside the door on a timer, focusing on breathing. He used to do 10 minutes every morning after a cold shower. [11:36]–[14:21]
