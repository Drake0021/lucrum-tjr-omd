## Summary
TJR demonstrates his Sunday weekly analysis for the week of ~Jul 10–14, 2023: first the economic calendar (which days to avoid trading because of red-folder news), then top-down bias on S&P 500, GBPUSD, GBPJPY and gold. He treats weekly analysis like any market-open analysis: mark building blocks (BOS, order blocks, equilibrium, imbalances, liquidity), identify likely retracement zones and draws on liquidity, and adapt if proven wrong.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Weekly analysis** — Same as market-open analysis but on the higher timeframes: mark building blocks, where price will likely retrace to, and draws on liquidity, via top-down analysis (monthly → weekly → daily → 4H). [08:14]
- **4H BOS confirms daily** — "a break of structure on the four hour means we're kind of good to go on the daily" (a 4H BOS in the direction of the daily bias confirms the daily leg is continuing). [03:15]
- **HTF trend flip hierarchy** — A higher-timeframe trend change is only confirmed by a BOS on the next-lower timeframe: e.g., gold monthly uptrend vs weekly downtrend; "we won't know when the monthly wants to push higher until we get a weekly break of structure", and that requires a daily BOS first. Until then, follow the weekly trend. [07:42], [08:14]
- **Weekly retrace via daily BOS** — A daily BOS against the weekly trend = the weekly retracement; after it fills weekly equilibrium, a daily BOS back with the weekly trend resumes it (GU example). [04:21]
- **Divergence** — mentioned on gold daily as a hint of retracement; not defined (vague). [07:09]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- News filter: likely do NOT trade on CPI day (Wednesday) and PPI/unemployment claims day (Thursday); these releases are an hour before the (NY equities) market open. Still watch/mark levels to see how price reacts. [01:03], [01:35]
- Consumer sentiment (Friday) — he doesn't care much; tradable. [02:07]
- For London traders: avoid GBP pairs around GBP news (or wait until it comes out). GBP news at ~11 AM US time Monday — be careful with GBP pairs. [00:32]
- When the market breaks structure against your fundamental preference (S&P monthly BOS up), accept it and trade with it — "it's no longer this is what I want to happen". [02:41]
- IF weekly and daily bullish, price touched daily OB without a daily BOS down, and 4H broke structure up THEN look for buys Mon–Tue as long as the 1H and 4H keep following that sentiment. [03:15], [03:48]
- Targets = imbalances (FVGs) and liquidity on the weekly/daily (top of imbalance first, then liquidity pools). [04:55]
- If proven wrong, change bias and adapt. [08:47]

## Process / order of operations
1. Review the economic calendar for each day; flag red-folder news days (CPI, PPI, NFP etc.) as no/low-trade days, and session-specific news for specific currencies. [00:00]–[02:07]
2. For each instrument: monthly structure → weekly → daily → 4H. [02:41]
3. Mark order blocks, equilibrium of the relevant leg, imbalances (FVGs), and liquidity to be taken. [03:15], [04:55]
4. Define the bias for the week and conditions to keep it (lower TF still aligned). [03:48]
5. Define target draws on liquidity. [04:55]

## Confluences mentioned
- Daily order block draw-back (S&P; GU NFP order block where liquidity was swept before rally). [03:15], [05:29]
- Equilibrium — daily equilibrium coinciding with weekly equilibrium (S&P). [03:48]
- Imbalance (FVG) as target/draw. [04:55], [07:42]

Plan: buys Mon–Tue if 1H/4H agree. [02:41]–[03:48]
