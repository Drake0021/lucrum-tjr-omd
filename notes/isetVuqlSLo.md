## Summary

TJR describes his own learning effort (8 hrs/day of YouTube, 10+ trading books, 10+ psychology books) and tells viewers that minimum effort yields minimum results. No strategy content. Homework: rewatch Boot Camp Day 1 and revisit the goals/short-term goals set earlier in the boot camp.
