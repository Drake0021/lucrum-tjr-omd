## Summary
Despite the "2026" title (a later retitle; uploaded Jul 2024), this is his mid-2024 daily-bias update. The base method (from the Trading Transformation) is the 4H trend plus the 1H trend. The addition is **premium/discount (equilibrium)**: in a 4H uptrend, if price is in a premium after an extension, expect a retrace to EQ. Then the daily bias can be flipped to bearish with the target = EQ, provided LTF bearish signs appear (liquidity sweep, 15m BOS / "capitulation"). If price is already in a discount within the uptrend, look for longs. He says he rarely looks at the daily; the 4H is "as high as I want to go" for daily bias. Examples: Jul 1 2024 (4H BOS up after a daily retrace into daily EQ → long), a bearish trade inside bullish 4H structure (extension + sweep + 15m BOS → short to the 4H EQ), and Jun 4 2024 ES (4H EQ hit in pre-market, discount, 15m sweep + SMT where ES was lagging → long, his own trade).

## Concepts & definitions
- **Equilibrium (EQ) / premium / discount** — in an uptrend, draw from the low to the high: above the 0.5 line = premium, below = discount. In a downtrend, the reverse (inferred standard). [03:21]–[03:55]
- **Extension** — "every single leg up is an extension". After an extension, assume price is "due for a retrace down to fill equilibrium"; "almost always price will always be trying to seek out an equilibrium level before moving up and higher". [09:33]–[10:06]
- **Range lows / range highs** — the significant swing low and high that define the range to anchor EQ: the daily range for a daily retrace, hourly range highs/lows for a 4H retrace. How to know which is the range high: the one from which structure starts breaking down. [06:47] [07:19] [10:40]
- **Capitulation** — LTF structure breaking against the HTF trend (e.g. 4H capitulation within a daily extension → expect daily EQ; 1H sweep + 1H capitulation in a 4H uptrend → expect 4H EQ). [12:54]
- **Imbalance needs to be balanced** — a big imbalance (FVG) toward the EQ adds to the retrace expectation. [10:40]–[11:14]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Base daily bias (prior method): 4H uptrend + 1H uptrend → buys only. Updated because "it's limited the amount of trades I've been able to take". [03:55]
- **Updated rule:** in a 4H uptrend with price in a premium (after an extension) at the market open → expect price to go down to the HTF discount/EQ first. Shorts toward EQ are allowed ("I'll flip my daily bias and pretty much make the overall target the equilibrium level"). [02:15]–[02:48] [04:30]
- In a 4H uptrend with price at a discount → keep the long bias ("I want to see price move higher"). [04:30]–[05:03]
- To flip to bearish inside bullish structure you need "bearish signs on lower time frames": a liquidity sweep of highs plus a BOS down (e.g. 15m BOS), at the top of an extension. [10:06]–[10:40] [12:20]
- Daily bias TF ceiling: "I'm never really looking at the daily time frame ... the 4 hour time frame is really as high as I want to go" for daily bias. (He still uses daily ranges in one example.) [08:28]–[09:00]
- Target of a counter-trend (retrace) trade = the 4H (or daily) EQ level. [10:40] [12:54]
- If the 4H EQ was already hit in pre-market and price is in a discount within a 4H uptrend → look for longs. [14:04]–[14:36]

## Process / order of operations
1. Determine the 4H (and 1H) trend by structure. [03:55] [16:23]
2. Identify the current leg: has the extension been made? Anchor EQ on the relevant range (range low → range high). [06:47] [09:33]
3. Premium at the open → expect a retrace to EQ; flip the bias only after LTF bearish signs (sweep + 15m BOS). Discount → with-trend bias. [04:30] [10:06]
4. Execute with the normal model: NY open sweep → 15m/5m BOS → imbalance fill → (1m confluences). [11:14]–[12:20]
5. Optional: SMT ES vs NQ at the sweep to choose the index. [15:47]
