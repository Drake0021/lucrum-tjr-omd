## Summary
Recap of a GBPUSD long (3 TPs hit, rest BE) on a no-news Friday, executed with the "new strategy". That means a breakout above prominent highs, the area of accumulation that caused the leg, a revisit, a liquidity sweep, and a 1m BOS / close above a prominent 1m high, repeated fractally on smaller timeframes. The lesson is take-profit placement. Set multiple TPs at high-confluence HTF building blocks (liquidity, OBs, FVGs) where a reaction is likely, not at Fibonacci or random levels. For a swept-then-reversed high, the orders sit above the high, so the TP goes at that high. Beginners should set multiple TPs and move the stop to BE.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Prominent high breakout** — the setup trigger from the strategy video. Price breaks through a series of prominent highs; non-prominent highs are ignored. [01:27]
- **Order block = leg down that caused the leg up** — the area where orders accumulated. [01:27]–[02:02]
- **Fractal scaling** — "we just keep scaling and keep finding these miniature trends". HTF breakout → accumulation → revisit, then the same pattern on 5m, then 1m. [04:50]
- **Liquidity (definition here)** — "it's just a higher low within the market" (likely a misstatement for swing highs/lows. TJR is vague). HTF highs and lows are potential liquidity sweeps and reaction points. [11:00]
- **TP at a swept high** — if a high was swept and then price legged down, the orders that caused the drop were filled from that high and above. Price may not come back to sweep it again. Set the TP at that high, because everything from it upward is where the opposing orders were filled. [03:07]–[04:14]
- **Retracement/extension within the HTF bias** — LTF moves are a series of retracements and extensions. The retracements occur off HTF building blocks, so you must take profit at them. [08:12]–[08:47]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Setup (GU): daily down, but the hourly broke prominent highs ("dick to the upside" = displacement leg). Find where orders accumulated (OB). Extra confluence is a liquidity sweep to the downside. [00:55]–[01:27]
- 5m: tap into the OB plus a liquidity sweep, then a break of the mini-downtrend (BOS up). [02:02]
- Entry: after a revisit of the new accumulation area and a sweep of highs → leg down, wait for a 1m BOS up / close above a prominent 1m high. Stop under the accumulation that caused the leg up. [02:34]–[03:07]
- TP1: a high. TP2: a swept high (orders above it). TP3: an area of liquidity. TP4 and TP5: HTF highs. [03:07]–[04:14]
- Set TPs at high-confluence areas likely to cause a reaction (HTF FVGs, OBs, liquidity, hourly liquidity). Do not use Fibonacci levels or "random" levels. [05:25]–[07:06]
- Use multiple TPs because you don't know which block will cause the reaction. Move the stop to BE "as much as possible". [06:33], [09:20]–[09:52]
- Do not wait for a BOS against you to exit. Take profit into levels, because a 1m BOS can be a fake-out. [11:00]–[11:34]
- No-news day noted as a plus. [04:14]

## Process / order of operations
1. Daily trend context (here, counter-trend to the daily is allowed after an hourly breakout of prominent highs).
2. Hourly: breakout of prominent highs → mark the accumulation/OB that caused it, plus a liquidity sweep.
3. 5m: tap the OB, sweep, break the mini-downtrend → mark the new accumulation.
4. 1m: after a revisit, BOS / close above a prominent 1m high → enter. Stop under the accumulation.
5. Layer TPs at successive HTF building blocks. After TP1, BE. Partial at each TP.
