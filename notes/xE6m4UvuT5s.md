## Summary
An NQ short on Thursday 2025-01-16 (the day after CPI; unemployment claims at 8:30 ET), for about +$21K with over-leveraged size. Bias was bearish: after a pre-market manipulation up and an overextended up-leg, the draws were the London session lows and Asian session lows. At the open NQ broke 5m structure down and hit every 5m continuation confluence (5m OB, FVG, EQ, breaker). The 1m IFVG entry came *before the macro*, so he skipped it and waited. After a further 5m extension and retrace with no FVG/OB/breaker left, he used a rarely used confluence: the **accumulation candle/zone** (the last up-move in a downtrend). - **5m continuation confluences** — after a 5m BOS down, look for bearish order flow continuation "via this five minute order block, via this five minute fair value gap, via five minute equilibrium, this breaker block" [02:48].

- **Accumulation zones / candle** — "in an uptrend every single move down is an accumulation range ... in a downtrend ... every single move up is essentially an accumulation prior to the distribution".
