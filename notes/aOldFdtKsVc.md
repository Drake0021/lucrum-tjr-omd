## Summary
An early (Nov 2022, pre-Boot Camp) lesson on break of structure (BOS), wrapped in a day-in-the-life vlog. TJR defines BOS as a candle **body close beyond the wick** of the most recent swing high or low. A wick through is a liquidity sweep, not a BOS. He has viewers trace every up/down candle sequence to find swings. Then he walks top-down: monthly → weekly → daily → 4H → 1H to set bias, and says that a BOS on a lower timeframe marks a retracement swing on the next-higher timeframe. Execution is on the 1m: sell-side sweep at the NYSE open → 1m BOS of the most recent high → enter on the BOS, at the FVG, or at the OB. He closes with the "three tools together" summary: sweep (volume/volatility), BOS (confirmation), FVG (prime entry). He explicitly rejects change of character (CHoCH) and indicators. The vlog part mentions a 4-trade day and a 6-trade over-trading day. Examples are mostly unnamed charts; the daily bias discussion is on S&P 500.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Uptrend / downtrend** — uptrend = higher highs and higher lows; downtrend = lower lows and lower highs. [08:41]
- **Break of structure** — "a change in market direction": in an uptrend, a low gets taken out (closed below) → bearish; in a downtrend, a close above a high → bullish. [09:14]–[09:48]
- **Current market structure, not hand-drawn macro trend lines** — a big zig-zag drawn on the daily is really monthly structure. On the daily there are many BOS up and down, so you can't just say "it's going down"; you need the most recent BOS on the timeframe you read. [10:23]–[11:29]
- **Swing identification by candle tracing** — "each blue candle is an up move… you have to highlight each blue candle"; even a single up candle counts as an up move; ignoring them "invalidates price data". High = move up then down; low = move down then up. [13:40]–[15:31]; [20:53] (in the FOMC video he restates "move up, leg down" = a high)
- **BOS validity: close beyond the wick** — "we need a candle closure below the wick, not the body… the very last pip is the low… the next candle has to close below it, can't wick it". [16:39]
- **Wick through = liquidity sweep, not BOS** — "a liquidity sweep is pretty much just a wick above a high and then moving down… we know that it's not a break of structure until it closes above that high". Uses a 4H example: a large blue candle forming above a high that closed back below = "just a wick". [17:16]–[18:27]
- **Change of character (CHoCH)** — "I do not trade based off of change of character, I think it's kind of dumb." [07:33]
- **Nested retracement rule** — a BOS on a timeframe usually marks a retracement/swing on the next-higher timeframe: 4H BOS down = "making a daily low"; 1H BOS = making a 4H low/high; 30m BOS → 1H retrace; 15m shows retracements on the 1H; 1H on the 4H; 4H on the daily; daily on the weekly; weekly BOS → monthly retrace. "It's the same price data… there's no best time frame." [37:30]–[39:43]
- **Equilibrium** — 50% from swing high to swing low (preview; full video promised next). [22:54]
- **Why a sweep matters for FVGs** — when there was no sweep, "the move's not going to be volatile enough to make a liquidity void" (FVG). [36:21]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- A BOS requires a candle **body close** beyond the **wick** extreme of the most recent swing; a wick only = not a BOS. [16:39]–[18:27]
- Daily bias holds until the specific daily low (in a bullish daily) is **closed below**: "we continue to be bullish on the daily until a low gets closed below… still looking for buys until this low gets violated." [30:45]
- **Top-down alignment:** daily bullish → on the 4H, a bearish BOS is a retracement, so wait for a 4H bullish BOS → then on the 1H wait for a bullish BOS → long. A 1H bearish BOS against a bullish daily is ignored: "we're not shorting that because on the daily we're bullish… we need to use everything in confluence." [24:33]–[28:28]
- If the 4H BOS is down (retracement), scale down and look for an LTF bullish BOS "after the low has been made", or wait for 4H confirmation. [26:48]; [29:38]
- If the BOS leg never retraces, no entry: "we don't even ever get a retracement down into here… that's one that you're just going to have to sit out." [30:10]
- **Entry options after a BOS:** enter on the BOS candle close, at an FVG (or its 50% mark), or at a bullish OB. [28:28]; [32:56]; [36:21]
- **1m execution (NY open):** mark sell-side and buy-side liquidity → whichever is hit first (here sell-side at the NYSE open) → "once we see this candle sweep we're instantly hawking for highs" → track the most recent high after the sweep → close above = BOS → enter (FVG / BOS / OB). "Once you get a liquidity sweep then you see break of structure on the one minute time frame, those are your indications." [32:23]–[32:56]
- **HTF POI + LTF BOS:** price tapped the 50% of a 4H FVG → bullish if a BOS to the upside forms → with no LTF FVG, "enter purely off of break of structure"; stop below the low that tapped the 4H FVG. [34:00]–[34:39]
- **Stops:** under the low before the BOS / under the swept low (shown, no number). [26:14]; [29:00]; [34:39]
- **Targets:** "previous areas of support and resistance", "previous areas of liquidity", up to TP1–TP5; move stop to BE as TPs hit. [26:14]; [29:00]; [35:11]
- **Management (vlog):** take 50% at TP1 and move stop to BE ("secure your profits"). [05:20]–[05:52]

## Process / order of operations
1. Mark the trend on monthly/weekly/daily using the latest BOS; ignore wicks through levels (sweeps). [20:08]–[24:00]
2. Daily bias = latest daily BOS; it stays until the key opposing swing is closed beyond. [24:33]; [30:45]
3. 4H: wait for a BOS in the daily direction (a counter BOS = retracement). [25:06]; [27:55]
4. 1H: wait for a BOS in the bias direction; enter (BOS close / FVG / OB); stop under the low; TPs at previous liquidity. [25:39]–[29:00]
5. Intraday execution on the 1m: sweep of sell-side (or buy-side) liquidity at the NYSE open → 1m BOS → FVG/OB/BOS entry. [32:23]–[32:56]
6. Summary combo: liquidity sweep (volume and volatility) → BOS (confirmation the structure is changing) → FVG (prime entry, price returns to fill). [40:16]

## Confluences mentioned
- Liquidity sweep: needed so the move has "volume and volatility"; without it no FVG forms. [36:21]; [40:16]
- BOS: required confirmation. [40:16]
- FVG: "prime entry"; also 50% of a 4H FVG as an HTF POI. [34:00]; [40:16]
- Order block (bullish OB): alternative entry. [28:28]; [32:56]
- Equilibrium: target/zone (preview). [22:54]; [24:00]
- "Support and resistance" used loosely as target wording (= previous liquidity). [25:06]; [29:00]
- Don't use: indicators, EMA crosses, CHoCH. [07:33]; [12:33]–[13:06]

## Risk & psychology rules
- Risk 0.25–0.5% per trade in the vlog; adding a second 0.5% entry if price retraces. [03:41]–[04:15]
- Over-trading admitted: 6 trades in a day; "stopped trading after the second loss, I lost two in a row, should have just cut it at four". Next day: "my only trade for today because I kind of over traded yesterday". [05:52]–[06:25]
- First week of daylight saving time: markets "whipsaw", "everything's kind of out of whack". [04:47]
- Secure profits: small TP1 wins add up. [05:52]
- Analogy: chasing losses after a lost parlay = over-trading; "trading is gambling" (said loosely). [01:17]

## Changes vs. earlier teaching / contradictions
- **Timeline placement:** Nov 2022, pre-2023 Boot Camp. BOS definition (body close beyond the wick) exactly matches the Boot Camp definition (framework §2.4) — confirms it.
- Entering directly on the BOS candle close is a primary entry here (BC1 model 1). Later Boot Camp 2.0 and the 2026 model require a retrace into FVG/EQ plus a trigger; this is the early, looser version.
- "Support and resistance" used for targets here, while the Boot Camp calls S/R "guessing" — wording inconsistency; he means previous liquidity/swing levels.
- OB is listed as an entry option, consistent with BC1, not with 2026 (no OBs).
- Rejects CHoCH ("kind of dumb"), consistent with the framework's §2.6 (CHoCH only once).
- Trades per day: 4–6 trades in one day in the vlog vs. later 1–2 limits; he calls it over-trading himself.
- No news-day, SMT, time-window, or contract-size rules stated. Execution timeframe is 1m at the NYSE open; entry time not otherwise given.
- Answers OPEN #13 (intraday bias update) partly: daily bias persists until the specific daily swing low is closed below; LTF BOS against it are retracements. [30:45]
- Answers OPEN #6 partially: bias invalidation = a **close** below the low ("violated" = closed below). [30:45]
