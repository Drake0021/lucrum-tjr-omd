## Concepts & definitions
- **New day opening gap / new week opening gap** — the gap between the prior close (Friday) and the new open; he notes they were filled at the open of the week. [01:48]–[02:25]
- **Highly executional price range** — the HTF zone in the bias direction where he will look for entries: 1H FVG, 1H draws on liquidity (hourly lows), an hourly FVG/imbalance. [04:06] [05:14] [07:30]
- **Profit taking** — when price takes previous 4H swing highs, expect profit taking that brings price down into a 1H FVG or to sweep hourly lows before continuing. [03:32]
- **Extension** — for bullish structure to continue, the 4H move needs to extend beyond the prior high by more than "just this poke". [02:58] [06:21]

## Process / order of operations
1. Check the news calendar; set the earliest trade time and no-trade days. [05:46]
2. HTF read: weekly context, day/week opening gaps, 4H structure and FVGs, whether the extension is complete → daily bias. [06:21]–[06:57]
3. Mark the executional price ranges in the bias direction (1H FVGs, 1H liquidity). [06:57]–[07:30]
4. Mark the market open; wait for a range to be hit after the open (usually after 9:50). [03:32] [05:46]
5. 1m: BOS / IFVG → FVG / OB / breaker / EQ entry with rejection → long. [08:03]–[09:48]
- He summarizes: "overall where do we want the market to go → executional price ranges → lower time frame execution". [10:30]
