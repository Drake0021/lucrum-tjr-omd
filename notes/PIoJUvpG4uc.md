## Summary
A pre-market bias for Fri 2024-01-19. Prelim consumer sentiment is released 30 minutes after the open (10:00 ET). ES rallied in London toward all-time highs (not yet breached), so ATH is the next draw. He is "very bullish" and looks for retrace points: an hourly imbalance overlapping the equilibrium of the latest leg, 15m lows, another imbalance, and the area of accumulation before the leg up. NQ is above ATH with no resting liquidity above ("a guessing game"), so he looks for the same kind of long from a hourly imbalance or a sweep of 15m lows. Key rules: **no trading until the 10:00 news is out**, and **de-risk because of the news**.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Retrace points** — imbalances (hourly FVG), the EQ of the latest leg (lows → high) "pretty much in line with this imbalance" (overlap = extra confluence), 15m lows, and the area of accumulation before the leg up [01:11–01:47].
- **No resting liquidity above ATH** — when price is beyond all-time highs, "where is resting liquidity, we don't have it ... it's literally a guessing game" [02:53].
- **Relative equal highs** — liquidity that could be swept to send price lower [03:29–04:04].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- **News 30 minutes after the open:** "I will not be trading until then", and "we have to de-risk ourselves because of the news" [02:21]. Still tradable "because it's not super super high impact news" [02:21–02:53].
- Bullish plan: execute within the hourly imbalance or on a sweep of 15m lows (ES and NQ alike) [04:38].
- Alternate: if the open rips to the highs, sweeps them and reverses down, he will look for shorts [01:47–02:21].
- Overlap of FVG + EQ = higher confluence [01:11].

## Process / order of operations
1. Check the news timing (consumer sentiment at 10:00) [00:00].
2. Note the overnight/London move and the next HTF draw (ATH) [00:00–00:35].
3. On the hourly mark the imbalances and the EQ of the latest leg; on the 15m mark lows and the accumulation area [01:11–01:47].
4. Wait for the news, de-risk, then execute at the marked zones [02:21; 04:38].
