## Summary
Beginner guide built on three skills: strategy, risk management, psychology. Strategy section gives a compact "day trading plan" for small/funded accounts: trade only with the higher-timeframe trend/order flow; setup = HTF liquidity sweep → LTF BOS or inverse FVG → LTF continuation confluence (FVG / order block / breaker / equilibrium) → target HTF liquidity and FVGs. Prerequisites: 3 straight months profitable on demo, proper risk, discipline, backtesting and journaling. Risk: size from your own win rate and RR (high WR/low RR → ~2–3% per trade; low WR/high RR → below 1%); funded accounts = limited number of trades (3–10) so only high-quality setups. Psychology: fear of being wrong, losing money, missing out. No live trade example; much ranting.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Three skills** — strategy (edge), psychology, risk management; all three required. [02:10–02:42; 04:22]
- **HTF trend / order flow filter** — "we're only looking for trades that are within the high time frame trend." Counter-trend retracements in a downtrend are mostly choppy/small; of 11 retraces on the shown HTF downtrend only one offered good RR. [08:21–10:05]
- **Day-trading plan (written on screen)** — "High time frame liquidity sweep followed by low time frame break of structure or inverse fair value gap, followed by low time frame confluence (fair value gap, order block, breaker block, equilibrium) in line with the high time frame trend; from there target high time frame liquidity and fair value gaps." [11:17–13:29]
- **Win-rate / RR-based risk** — high win rate + low RR → slightly higher risk; low win rate + high RR → slightly lower risk; baseline 1% per trade. [28:05–30:55]
- **Funded account = limited trades** — a $100 evaluation buys ~3–10 losing trades before DD; think of each trade as real money (e.g., $33 or $10 per loss). [23:32–26:59]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Only take trades in the HTF trend direction (e.g., bearish order flow → sells only), especially with small capital; he himself trades counter-trend "very frequently" because he has capital to spare — beginners should not. [08:21–08:57; 12:23]
- Entry model: HTF liquidity sweep → LTF BOS or IFVG → LTF continuation confluence (FVG/OB/breaker/EQ) → enter, in line with HTF trend. [11:17–13:29]
- Targets: HTF liquidity and HTF FVGs. [13:29]
- Before risking money: be profitable on demo for 3 straight months (green P&L each month). [14:37; 17:28–18:34]
- Risk example: $1,000 live account, 80% WR at 1:1.5 RR → he'd risk 2–3% per trade; low WR/high RR → lower than 1% (20 losses at 1% = −20%). [29:11–30:55]
- Funded: consistency rules penalize low-WR/high-RR; tight daily DD penalizes higher risk (3% loss leaves 1%). Consider buying 2–3 smaller accounts rather than one max account to get more trades. [31:28–32:02; 39:25–41:06]
- Personally: "I'm a high win rate and low risk-to-reward person, so I have slightly higher risk and I'm on a live account"; used less risk on his recent funded account (passed in one week). [32:36; 07:44]
- No FOMO or revenge trades; "the trade that you missed today is going to be here tomorrow." [26:59; 36:36]

## Process / order of operations
1. Learn the confluences (free course / boot camp). [03:14–03:48]
2. Read HTF trend/order flow; only trade with it. [08:21]
3. Wait for HTF liquidity sweep. [11:17]
4. LTF BOS or IFVG. [11:51]
5. LTF continuation (FVG/OB/breaker/EQ) → enter. [11:51]
6. Target HTF liquidity / FVGs. [13:29]
7. Journal; compute win rate and average RR; size accordingly. [21:19; 28:38–29:11]
