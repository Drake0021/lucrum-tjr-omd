## Summary
TJR applies the Day 23 basic strategy (4H bias → 1H picks the exec TF → 1H/4H liquidity sweep → exec-TF BOS → third confluence + reaction close) to ~8 consecutive **ES** New York sessions, about two weeks before recording. Dates are inferred as roughly late Feb–early Mar 2024. Results: 3 trade signals that worked (one on the 15m), 1 loss (which he took live), several no-trade days, and one day he says should be skipped despite a technical signal (a sweep and BOS ~3 hours after the open, choppy, no FVG). Bias is set from candles closed **before** 9:30 ET. A chopping 4H and a low-range pre-CPI day are no-trade conditions. Stops and TPs are still not taught.

## Concepts & definitions
- **NY session only** — "first of all we're only trading New York session." He highlights each NY session from 9:30 ET. [01:50–03:01]
- **Bias uses closed pre-open candles** — "We don't know that this 4-hour candle is going to close bullish until like 30 minutes into the market open." The 1H is the same: "we do not understand that price is going to be bearish until we see this hourly candle close, which is 30 minutes into market open." So the bias stays per pre-open structure. [03:36–04:08]
- **Only mark highs/lows that exist before the open** — "prior to market open we didn't even know that this was a high ... so we're not even going to mark that one out." [26:35–27:09]
- **Already-swept and equal levels** — He skips a low that was "already swept." Two pretty-much-equal hourly lows are treated as one level. [18:33–19:07]
- **Near-miss ≠ sweep** — "We see market push all the way down, barely miss it." That was not counted. [19:07]
- **Third-confluence marking** — After the 5m BOS, mark every candidate (FVG(s), OB = the leg that swept, breaker = the leg before the BOS, EQ = swing high → low). Then see which one price actually retraces into. [10:23–11:00; 13:20–13:56; 17:23]
- **Reaction close** — In example 1, price wicks into the FVG and a bearish candle forms. "Remember I want to see the candle close out of there ... finally see this candle close out of there, perfect entry." Entry is on the close of the candle that closes out of the zone. [06:27–07:37]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Pre-open: read the 4H trend and the 1H trend from closed candles; mark 1H (and 4H) highs for a bearish bias, lows for a bullish one. [04:40–05:15]
- After the open, wait for price to hit a marked 1H/4H level, then wait for an exec-TF BOS, then a third confluence hit plus a close out of it → enter. [05:53–07:01]
- If no confluence gets retraced into after the BOS → no trade (Thursday example). [17:23–17:56]
- **Late-session filter** — A sweep + BOS "already 3 hours into market open ... isn't necessarily what we're looking for." Don't trade it even if the steps technically complete. [22:31–23:06]
- **Ignore sweeps near the Asian session** ("seconds away from Asian session ... 11 hours, no thank you"). [19:07–19:40]
- **Wide or poor confluences** — An OB that is "not necessarily the best confluence" and a breaker with a "super wide price range" (with no FVG formed) count as low quality. The day is not tradable. [21:23–22:31]
- **Chop filter** — When the 4H is "moving sideways" it is "not necessarily ideal trading conditions." A day with no BOS and sideways chop (moving "only 21 ticks" for the day) is no trade. "We want to be catching trending market moves." [26:01; 27:44]
- **News** — He keeps in mind that some days were high-impact news days. The last day had CPI next (he's unsure: "I believe this was CPI"). [02:27; 27:44]
- The 15m execution (1H opposite to 4H) example is valid by the rules. But "ideally we don't necessarily want to be taking this trade"; future additions (time filters, SL/TP) will filter such days. [24:14–26:01]

## Process / order of operations
1. Before 9:30 ET: 4H trend (closed candles) → bias.
2. 1H trend → 5m (aligned) or 15m (opposite).
3. Mark 1H/4H swing highs (bearish) or lows (bullish) that formed before the open, excluding ones already swept.
4. After the open: a marked level is traded through.
5. Exec TF: BOS in the bias direction.
6. Mark FVG/OB/breaker/EQ; wait for a retrace into one.
7. A candle closes out of the zone in the bias direction → enter on the close.
