## Summary
TJR teaches his original (pre-ICT-vocabulary) forex strategy, the "London session fake-out," on GBPJPY: mark Asian and prior New York session highs/lows; when London opens and pushes beyond a session high (or low), drop to the 5m and wait for a candle-close break of structure in the opposite direction; enter on that BOS close, stop above the fake-out high, target the opposite session lows/highs. He explicitly says it is NOT his current strategy (current one is in the free course and "plays off of session highs and session lows and break of structure" with more advanced additions). He shows three examples (two London, one NY session) on GBPJPY. Promised index application was not delivered.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Three skills** — strategy, risk management, psychology. [02:44–03:16]
- **London session fake-out** — at London open price "move[s] up drastically and then ... go[es] in the opposite direction"; i.e., a sweep of the Asian/New York session extreme at the session open. Discovered trading GJ/GU in high school from California. [04:56–07:41]
- **Session times (chart on UTC-4 New York time)** — Asia 18:00–03:00; London pre-market from 02:00 but mark London from 03:00, London "ends at 11:30" but he cuts it at 08:00 because NY pre-market starts at 08:00; New York 08:00–16:00. [12:45–15:36] (answers OPEN §12.8)
- **Swing low / high** — "a move down, a move up and the lowest point of those two candlestick wicks"; high mirror. [17:56–18:28]
- **Break of structure** — in an uptrend, a candle **close** below the most recent low; in a downtrend, close above the most recent lower high. Wicks below a low are not a BOS. [16:45–19:02]
- **Level already taken** — if a session level was already pushed past (e.g., Asia took the NY low), discard it. [20:45–21:17]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Before London open mark Asian H/L and New York H/L (London H/L not needed for the London trade; add London H/L before NY session). [20:45; 24:39]
- When the session open pushes beyond a session high (Asia or NY high), go to the 5m and monitor the most recent swing lows; each new low that forms becomes the level to watch. [21:17–21:50]
- Entry: on the 5m candle that **closes** below the most recent swing low (BOS) after the fake-out → short. Mirror for longs. [19:35–21:50]
- Stop: above the fake-out highs (the sweep extreme). [11:36; 21:50]
- Targets: previous session lows (Asia low; NY low as TP2 if still unswept). [21:50–22:23]
- Without waiting for the BOS (just shorting the London pop), he lost often. [09:23–09:55; 18:28]
- He calls Asian session "a horrible time to trade." [05:29]

## Process / order of operations
1. Set chart to New York time; mark Asia (18:00–03:00) H/L and prior NY (08:00–16:00) H/L; drop levels already taken. [12:45–15:36; 20:45]
2. At London open (03:00 ET) wait for price to push beyond a session high or low. [21:17]
3. Drop to 5m; track the latest opposing swing; wait for a candle close beyond it. [21:50]
4. Enter on the BOS close; stop beyond the fake-out extreme; TP at opposite session H/L. [21:50–22:23]
5. Repeat for NY open using London H/L, Asia H/L, NY H/L. [24:39]
