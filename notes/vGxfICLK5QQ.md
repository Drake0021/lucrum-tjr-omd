## Summary
Despite the title, this is **not** about session timing or time windows. "Time" here means time and experience as the key to profitability. TJR says he will lay out a toolbox of confluences (BOS, liquidity, order blocks, FVGs, equilibrium, **breaker blocks**) plus a "super duper systematic strategy". Students should keep the tools that make sense and discard those that don't after months of honest testing. No strategy is a secret; profitability = strategy you understand + discipline + risk management + time.

- **Toolbox of confluences to be taught** — break of structure, liquidity, order blocks, fair value gaps, equilibrium, breaker blocks. [02:54–03:27]
