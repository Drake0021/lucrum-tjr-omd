## Summary
TJR lays out his trade-journal template: columns for pair, session, risk, then written confluences (including daily/4H/1H/15m structure and the building blocks used), whether the plan was followed, emotions felt and why, and how the trade could be improved (for wins and losses). He states that unprofitable traders should trade only one instrument, trade New York or London (not Asia), and risk 1–3% (ideally 1%). He recorded this while in a live S&P trade near TP3, which he credits to his new rule of requiring an OB/FVG entry after sweep+BOS.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Journal fields** — pair | session | risk (single-word data) + confluences (sentences) + followed plan? + emotions & why + how to improve. [02:15]–[07:48]
- **Confluences entry** — record the entry model (sweep+BOS / sweep+BOS+FVG / sweep+BOS+OB), plus what the daily, 4H, 1H and 15m structure said and which building blocks on 15m/1H/4H the position was taken from. [03:52], [05:02]
- **"Followed my plan" example** — "took the trade off a liquidity sweep, break of structure, order block entry, waited for confluence, waited for reaction, and everything was in line with my daily bias." [06:09]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Unprofitable traders: trade only one pair/instrument; delete the rest from the watchlist. [01:41], [02:15]
- Session: trade New York or London session, not Asia (low volatility/volume; "waiting an hour for price to move five pips"). [02:48], [03:21]
- Risk per trade: 1–3%, ideally 1% (or a set calculated lot size). More than 3% = "immediately doing something wrong"; less is fine. [05:36]
- IF the journal shows repeated losses taking LTF sweep+BOS where only the 1H agreed and all higher timeframes disagreed THEN stop taking those. [04:27], [05:02]
- Entry confirmation: a bare sweep+BOS is insufficient — wait for OB or FVG entry for more confirmation (his current rule; avoided a loss and produced a winner today). [08:19], [09:25]
- Take profit at building blocks where a reversal could occur; don't keep extending TP out of greed. [06:42], [07:14]

## Process / order of operations
1. After each trade, write pair, session, risk. [02:48]
2. Write all confluences in sentences, HTF→LTF (daily, 4H, 1H, 15m) and entry building blocks. [05:02]
3. Followed plan? yes/no and specifically what was/wasn't followed. [06:09], [06:42]
4. Emotions and why (fear, overconfidence, greed, over-risking). [06:42], [07:14]
5. How could it be improved (R:R, execution, emotions) — for wins and losses. [07:48], [08:19]
6. Reread the journal weekly to spot patterns. [04:27], [09:25]
