## Summary
A prop-firm "hack" experiment, not his trading model. He buys four $200k challenges at his own prop firm (One of One Funding), then on a **CPI** release goes "full port" (max size, 5% risk) long on two and short on two of them, in GBPUSD, with no stop and no TP. The winners pass phase 1 (8% target); the losers hit the 5% daily loss. Price dipped and then ripped up; the two long accounts passed within seconds and the two shorts were breached. There is no technical analysis. He notes in passing that he also had an S&P 500 trade on that CPI day that hit take-profit.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Hedged "full port" news straddle** — opposite max-size positions on separate challenge accounts at a high-impact release; the winner passes, the loser is breached. Explicitly "not necessarily a strategy." [00:00]–[02:10]; [03:50]
- **Forex lot-size calculation** — choose the pair, account currency, balance ($200k), a stop in pips (35 assumed), contract size (100,000 units/lot standard on FX; it differs for indices, crypto and commodities) and risk % (5% = the daily loss limit) → 28.57 lots. [06:08]–[11:49]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- He chose forex (GBPUSD) over indices for this, because "indexes tend to be super super volatile during these news events... it'll go up and then it'll go down... lots of liquidity sweeps back and forth"; forex tends to move one way. [03:50]–[04:25]
- Not an ES-bot rule. Phase 1 can fail if the loser hits −5% before the winner reaches +8%. [09:37]–[10:41]

## Process / order of operations
1. CPI release (his local 9:30 = 8:30 ET); set up at ~9:15 local. [03:17]
2. Compute the lot size for 5% at a 35-pip stop → enter long ×2 / short ×2 at the same time. [11:49]–[12:21]
3. Close the winners at the profit target. [16:18]–[18:37]
