## Summary
Nov 2022 (earliest video in batch; ~first 12.5 min is vlog: weekly P&L, a swing trade update, LED lamp/PS5/clothes). Lesson: equilibrium = the 50% of a swing (Fibonacci tool); banks buy in discount (below 50%) and sell in premium (above 50%). Anchor swing high → swing low after a bearish BOS (or low → high after a bullish BOS); if price never reaches the 50% before the next BOS, drag the anchor to the next swing. Never sell just because 50% is hit — wait for a BOS (LTF, e.g., 1H/4H) inside premium/discount, ideally after a liquidity sweep; stop beyond the swing; target previous swing lows/highs. He also uses EQ for bias/targets: a BOS on a timeframe likely retraces to the next-higher timeframe's EQ (15m → 1H EQ, 1H → 4H, 4H → daily, daily → weekly, weekly → monthly). Examples mostly on S&P weekly/daily/4H/1H and gold, with RR 1:2–1:8.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Premium / discount** — banks buy at a discount (cheap, lower half of a range) and short at a premium (upper half). [13:30] [14:04]
- **Equilibrium** — "the 50 mark of premium ... and discount"; found with the Fibonacci tool on a swing. [14:36]
- **Anchoring** — after a bearish BOS: swing high → swing low; everything above 50% = where banks likely reverse price down. After a bullish BOS: swing low → swing high. [15:11] [15:44] [22:23]
- **Re-anchor** — if price breaks structure again without reaching the 50%, "we don't take a trade ... we just drag it to the next swing low", repeat until a swing's 50% is reached. [18:32] [19:06] [25:49] [30:23]
- **Invalidation** — a long in discount "is not invalidated ... until it closes below" the swing low; monthly close above the swing high flips bias bullish. [22:23] [24:05]
- **HTF EQ as draw / retracement target** — a BOS on TF X → price "probably" goes to the next higher TF's EQ: weekly BOS → monthly EQ; daily → weekly; 4H → daily; 1H → 4H; 15m → 1H. "It doesn't reach it every single time." [27:31] [28:05] [28:38] [33:45]
- **OTE (optimal trade entry)** — his current swing short was "an OTE ... entry" off equilibrium. [00:33]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Don't click sell at the 50% mark; wait for a BOS in the trade direction while price is in premium (shorts) / discount (longs). [16:18]
- Higher probability: premium + liquidity sweep + BOS ("super high probability trade"). [17:28] [18:00] [35:28]
- Entry on the BOS (candle close); stop above the swing high that formed in premium (shorts) / below the swing low (longs) or above a news wick. [16:18] [17:28] [25:13]
- Targets: previous swing low/high (incl. the swing the EQ was drawn from), previous areas of liquidity; multiple TPs. [16:52] [22:58] [24:05]
- EQ is a confluence, "not something to be used just on its own". [12:57] [26:57]
- Execution TF one step below the EQ TF (weekly/daily EQ → 4H BOS; 4H EQ → 1H BOS). [20:13] [22:58] [23:31]
- Re-entry: after TPs, if price returns into discount without invalidation, take another BOS long. [24:05] [24:38]
- He did not trade on CPI day (Thu Nov 10 2022): "I'm not trading tomorrow CPI news data comes out". [02:14]

## Process / order of operations
1. After a BOS, draw Fib from the swing extreme to the opposite extreme; mark 50%. [15:44]
2. If price never reaches 50% and breaks structure again, re-anchor. [19:06]
3. When price is in premium/discount, drop a TF and wait for a sweep + BOS. [20:13] [36:00]
4. Enter on BOS; stop beyond swing; TPs at previous swing points/liquidity. [22:58]
