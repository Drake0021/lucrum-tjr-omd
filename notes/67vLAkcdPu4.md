## Summary
This is the first written-out version of TJR's 2024 "basic" systemized strategy. The first ~6 minutes are the national anthem and banter. Bias comes from the **4H trend**, not the daily, because he wants to catch 1–3 hour moves. The **1H trend picks the execution timeframe**: aligned = 5m, opposite = 15m, and the bias stays with the 4H. Pre-market he marks **1H and 4H highs/lows** as draws on liquidity. He waits for one to be swept, then needs a BOS on the execution TF, then a **third confluence**: an OB, FVG, breaker block or EQ on that TF. He enters only when a candle shows buying or selling pressure out of it. Stops, TPs and further additions come later. He says it yields few trades: none in the past two weeks.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Trade horizon** — "I like to catch one to three hour moves ... ideally I'm in the market for 1 to 3 hours." [08:44]
- **Bias timeframe** — "Treat the 4-hour and the 1-hour as our daily bias." The daily predicts "the next couple days ... I don't really give a f*** about that." [08:11–09:51]
- **4H trend = bias** — Uptrend (HH/HL, BOS up) = bullish; downtrend = bearish. "We're not trying to predict reversals ... too bad so sad if we take a loss because the 4-hour broke structure." [09:51–11:32]
- **1H trend = execution-TF selector** — If 1H agrees with 4H, scale to the **5m**. If 1H is opposite, still keep the 4H bias but execute on the **15m** ("we need higher time frame confirmation"). [11:32–14:35]
- **Execution points** — (A) a liquidity sweep of **1H or 4H** highs/lows ("not on the 15 minute, not on the 5 minute") plus (B) a BOS on the execution TF (5m or 15m). [14:35–16:26]
- **Draws of liquidity don't change with the 1H** — Bullish 4H means mark 1H and 4H **lows** even if the 1H is bearish. [17:32]
- **Third confluence** — One of: order block, fair value gap, breaker block, equilibrium, on the execution timeframe. [18:45; 20:34]
- **Pressure / reaction** — "literally just a green candle up and out of the order block / FVG / breaker block, a green candle closing above equilibrium," and for shorts a red candle closing underneath them. Rationale: a zone that just gets blown through becomes a breaker against you. [21:12–21:46]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Bias: 4H trend direction. Longs only if 4H is bullish; shorts only if bearish. [10:56]
- Exec TF: 1H aligned → 5m; 1H opposite → 15m. [12:08–13:28]
- Pre-open: mark all 1H and 4H lows (bullish bias) or highs (bearish). "Hands in our ass" until one is hit; if none is hit, there is no trade. [16:26–17:00]
- After the HTF level is swept, wait for a BOS in the bias direction on the exec TF. [17:32–18:04]
- After the BOS, wait for price to hit one of OB/FVG/breaker/EQ on the exec TF. Entering on the hit alone is not allowed. [19:27–20:01]
- **Entry trigger** — A candle closes up and out of the zone (longs) or down and out (shorts). For EQ: a close above it (longs) or below it (shorts). Then execute. [21:46–22:52]
- SL/TP are not covered yet. [22:52]

## Process / order of operations
1. 4H trend → bias.
2. 1H trend → exec TF (5m if aligned, 15m if opposite).
3. Pre-market: mark 1H/4H highs/lows in the bias direction (draws on liquidity).
4. Wait for one to be swept.
5. Exec TF: BOS in the bias direction.
6. Exec TF: price hits an OB/FVG/breaker/EQ (third confluence).
7. A reaction candle closes out of the zone → enter.
