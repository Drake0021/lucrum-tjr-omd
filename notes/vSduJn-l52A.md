## Summary
A recap of a day (≈2025-03-03) with one NQ short on the live account (~+$15K) and two counter-trend longs on a Topstep 150K evaluation, which he passed in one day. Bias was bearish: the draws were London session lows, relative equal lows and Asian session lows. At the open price swept the London session highs and made a 5m SMT, with NASDAQ as the lagging index. Then came a 5m BOS down and a 5m FVG; he entered on the fill "right after the macro", with the stop above the candle, and hit the full TP at the relative equal lows within minutes. The two later longs came off the Asian session lows sweep plus a HTF imbalance ("dead-cat bounce"). The first was entered on a bullish news-candle close; the second waited for a 5m retrace and an **inverse gap** (IFVG) close. He tells beginners not to copy those longs.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Draws on liquidity for the day** — London session lows, relative equal lows and Asian session lows below; "it was obvious that price wanted to go down here" [02:21].
- **External draw on liquidity** — the relative equal lows; price "isn't going to ... take out external ... draw on liquidity and then come all the way back up". If it did, the bias was wrong [03:28–04:01].
- **Lagging index** — NASDAQ was "the lagging index" after the 5m SMT off the London session highs, and he traded NQ [02:55].
- **Inverse gap ("inverse gap attack")** — a long entry "right when that candle closes" after a 5m retrace [10:42].
- **HTF imbalance** — a higher-timeframe FVG. After a big sell-off that took every draw, price at a HTF imbalance should give "at least some sort of a relief ... dead bounce" [10:09–10:42].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Bearish sequence: bearish draws below → NY open sweeps London session highs → 5m SMT (trade the lagging index) → 5m BOS down → 5m FVG forms → enter on the FVG fill after the macro → stop above the entry candle/high → TP at the relative equal lows [02:21–03:28].
- He used a tight stop above the displacement ("Drake") candle because of strong bias conviction. The reasoning: if price came back up there, the bias was wrong [03:28–04:01].
- He took the full TP at the first draw instead of an extended TP at the Asian lows, because recent price action had been poor: "I would rather de-risk and take profits early" [04:34–05:07].
- Size back up and look for extended TPs only once volatility and volume return [04:34–05:07].
- Counter-trend relief longs (not for beginners): after all draws are taken and price is in a HTF imbalance, a long off a sweep of the Asian lows. Better version: wait for the first bullish 5m candle → 5m retrace → enter on the IFVG candle close; stop under two protecting lows; exit above the 5m highs (~1:1) [10:09–11:15].

## Process / order of operations
1. Pre-market: mark session lows/highs and relative equal lows; pick the draw (bearish) [02:21].
2. Open: watch for a sweep of the opposite session high (London highs) plus a 5m SMT; choose the lagging index [02:21–02:55].
3. 5m BOS in the bias direction → mark the 5m FVG → wait for the fill → enter after the macro [02:55–03:28].
4. Stop above the entry candle/high; TP at the draw [03:28–04:01].
