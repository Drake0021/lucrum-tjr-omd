## Summary
Career-path advice for a beginner with $100: don't trade it; build capital with a job, learn basics (BabyPips), then his free Boot Camp; prove profitability on demo, then live with tiny risk, then buy the largest funded account and move payouts into a live account. No chart content; the only trading-method elements are the progression gates and risk sizing.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Funded account** — pay a firm a flat fee for a two-phase challenge (~10% profit target phase 1, ~5% phase 2, with drawdown rules), then trade ~$200–300K of firm capital. [06:03]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Progression gate: 3 profitable months on demo/paper before going live; if a month is unprofitable, restart the count. [04:23] [05:29]
- Then 3 profitable months live with $100 risking $1 per trade (1%). [04:56] [05:29]
- Prefers live forward testing over bar-replay backtesting ("not a huge fan of back testing on trade replay"). [03:49]
- Buy the largest funded account (~$1,000 for 250K); take payouts immediately into a live account; funded accounts "not sustainable" long-term. [06:38] [07:44]
- Don't be a professional trader without ≥$10K in the account and a full year of proven profitability. [08:16]
- On a well-capitalized live account you "can risk more than one percent per trade". [08:48]

## Process / order of operations
1. Keep the $100; open a demo account; BabyPips course. [00:33] [01:06]
2. Get a job / sell things; stack capital. [01:38]
3. Learn a strategy (his Boot Camp, ~50 free videos). [03:17]
4. Demo until 3 green months. [04:23]
5. Live $100, $1 risk, 3 green months. [04:56]
6. Largest funded account; payouts → live account; repeat. [06:38]

## Risk & psychology rules
- Separate emotion from money; demo has zero emotion, live money changes behavior. [04:23] [05:29]
- He mentions risking "15 or 10 grand per trade" himself and being down ~15K without caring. [05:29]
- 1% per trade implied as the baseline ($1 on $100). [04:56] [08:48]
