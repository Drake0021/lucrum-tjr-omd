## Summary
Five psychology tips from TJR ("trading for 7 years"): stick to one strategy, accept losses as part of probability, take responsibility rather than blaming the market, feel emotions but don't act on them, and follow your plan because it exists to protect you. The one method reference is his example plan: "wait for a liquidity sweep followed by a break of structure followed by a fair value gap."

## Concepts & definitions
- **Strategy = edge, not a holy grail** — a strategy only gives a higher probability of winning more often (win rate) and winning more than you lose (risk to reward). None has a 100% win rate [01:06–02:15].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Once a strategy shows consistent profitability, stick to it. Don't switch after one loss [00:34–01:06].
- Find your strategy by backtesting candidates for win rate and risk to reward [03:23].
- Example plan: wait for a **liquidity sweep → break of structure → fair value gap**. Don't buy just because a low was swept without the BOS and FVG [08:25–09:00].
- After a loss, don't take an extra revenge trade or increase risk on the next trade [07:18].

## Process / order of operations
- Before acting: "How am I feeling right now?" Think before acting [07:51].

## Confluences mentioned
- Liquidity sweep, BOS, FVG — named as the plan sequence [09:00].

## Trade examples
- None.

## Risk & psychology rules
- Losses are part of the strategy's probability; accept them [02:51–03:56].
- The market owes you nothing. Own the outcome ("I am the reason why I got stopped out") [03:56–05:37].
- All traders feel emotions; profitable ones don't act on them. Emotional reactions after a loss = extra trades and upsized risk [06:11–07:51].
- The plan and the risk plan are "built to save you", not to make money [08:25; 09:34].
