## Summary
An ES long recap from early February 2025 (caption says "$116,000"; the title says $16,320). His pre-open bias was bearish: sweep the London session highs, then rotate down to the London/Asian lows, because the new-week opening gap had filled and could act as an imbalance. At the open, however, price had retraced on the hourly into an **hourly breaker** and broke 5m structure up. He flipped long because "my bias will and has to change based on 5 minute order flow". The sequence: hourly breaker → 5m BOS up → 5m retrace into continuation confluences (EQ, breaker block, 1m OB) → 1m BOS up → entry, stop under the lows. TPs: 25% at 1:0.8 at nearby highs, then hourly equal highs, the 62% "OTE" extension, and the start of a 1H FVG, for a final 1:4. He also explains why a bias is "washed" if 5m structure has already reversed by the time the macro opens.

## Concepts & definitions
- **5m order flow overrides pre-market bias** — "my bias will and has to change based on 5 minute order flow ... if 5 minute bullish order flow is getting respected to the upside consistently that is undeniable reason for me to look for longs" [02:46].
- **Hourly breaker** — the HTF POI for the long. He says "if you want to consider this an order block ... maybe you could; me personally I don't consider those order blocks" [02:13].
- **Continuation confluence** — once in bullish 5m order flow, "all that I'm looking for after that is just some sort of continuation confluence, whether that be a bullish fair value gap, ... equilibrium, ... a breaker block, ... an order block" [03:50].
- **LTF trigger** — after the 5m BOS and the 5m retrace into the continuation confluence, "either a one minute inverse fair value gap or a one minute break of structure to the upside" [04:24].
- **Equal highs (triple)** — one, two, three equal highs that also sit at London session highs: "one trillion percent going to get taken out" [07:09–07:45].
- **OTE / 62% extension** — he first calls a level equilibrium, then corrects himself: "it was OTE from these highs down to these lows, the 62% extension" [05:30].
- **Macro timing logic** — if structure was bearish but 5m structure has reversed bullish and held by the time the macro hits, the bearish idea is "probably washed": "there's just not enough time" for a sweep and reversal [09:23]. If instead the London highs had been swept right at the open and bullish order flow was being invalidated as the macro opened, shorts would be valid [10:30].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- IF the 5m breaks structure against the pre-market bias at the open and holds that order flow, THEN trade in the 5m direction [02:46; 08:17].
- IF the expected sweep of a session high happens but 5m order flow gives "no reaction", THEN drop the short idea [03:19].
- Long entry sequence: HTF POI (1H breaker) hit → 5m BOS up → 5m retrace into a continuation confluence (EQ / breaker / OB / FVG) → 1m BOS up (or 1m IFVG) → enter [03:50–04:56].
- Stop: under the lows (the 5m retrace low, inferred) [04:56].
- TPs: TP1 25% at nearby highs (1:0.8); TP2 at hourly equal highs; TP3 at the 62% extension; final at the start of the 1H FVG; final RR 1:4 [04:56–06:02].
- Index choice: ES over NQ, because ES got the 5m retrace into the breaker/EQ continuation confluences, while NQ was already above London session/hourly highs before the macro [07:09].
- By the time the macro opens, the 5m order flow must still support the idea; if it has already reversed, the original bias is void [09:23–10:30].

## Process / order of operations
1. Pre-market (~8:30 ET on charts): mark London/Asian session highs and lows and HTF imbalances (NWOG filled); form a provisional bias [01:06–02:13; 08:50].
2. At the open: watch the 5m structure. Did the expected sweep get a reaction? [03:19].
3. If the 5m breaks against the bias after a HTF POI (hourly breaker) is filled, flip [02:13–02:46].
4. Wait for the 5m retrace into a continuation confluence [03:50–04:24].
5. Trigger: 1m BOS or 1m IFVG → enter; stop below the lows [04:24–04:56].
6. Ladder TPs: nearest highs (25%) → hourly equal highs → 62% extension → 1H FVG start [04:56–06:02].
7. Pick the index with the cleaner continuation confluence and liquidity still above it [07:09].
