## Summary
Daily bias for Fri Jan 5, 2024 (NFP, ISM Services, unemployment rate, average hourly earnings, an FOMC member). **No trading because of NFP**; he says the FOMC member alone would not have stopped him. ES HTF bias is bearish: the next stop is the end of the daily FVG, then the 4H lows, which line up with the 50% of the weekly FVG ("pretty high confluence price range"). On news days he would want a sweep of a 4H high or an hourly high with HTF confirmation to send price lower. He notes the daily bias has been correct every day this week and the previous day's trade hit three take-profits.

## Concepts & definitions
- **High-confluence price range** — the end of the 4H FVG + the end of the daily FVG + the start of the next daily FVG + the 50% of the weekly FVG all in one price window. [01:47]–[02:22]
- **News-day liquidity sweeps** — "on news days, liquidity sweeps happen often, and on high time frames that's typically when the high time frames will break structure, cause a liquidity sweep and then go in the other direction." [03:28]–[04:02]
- **Daily bias vs execution** — "daily bias on this is really the important thing, especially on high-impact news days; we know where the market wants to go, we can kind of adapt." [04:37]–[05:13]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- **No trade on NFP day.** "As long as NFP wasn't happening then I should have been completely fine to trade"; the FOMC member speaking "I probably wouldn't have even cared about." [00:00]–[00:37]
- ES (bearish): want "a sweep of this 4-hour high to send price lower" or "a sweep of any of the hourly highs to send price lower with a higher time frame confirmation." Mark the 4H high and the hourly highs; no need to scale lower. [03:28]–[04:02]
- If the market opens and dumps straight to the target: "too bad so sad, today was probably just a day that we shouldn't have traded." [04:02]–[04:37]
- Targets: the end of the daily FVG ("next stop"), then the 4H lows. [01:11]–[01:47]
- After a heavy sell-off, expect possible sideways chop: "I wouldn't be surprised if today just went sideways and chopped." [05:13]–[05:46]
- NQ: seeking an area of accumulation / the end of the weekly FVG below; may first push up into the 4H/hourly highs, "stall going into the weekend to accumulate orders, and then send price lower." [05:46]–[07:30]

## Process / order of operations
1. Check the news → NFP = no trade (still do the bias). [00:00]
2. Daily/weekly/4H FVG stack → HTF target range. [01:11]–[02:22]
3. Mark the sweep candidates in the bias direction (4H high, hourly highs). [03:28]–[04:02]
4. Recap after the close. [08:05]

## Confluences mentioned
- FVG ends and 50% of the weekly FVG as targets; 4H/hourly highs as sweep levels; HTF confirmation after the sweep. [02:22]; [03:28]
