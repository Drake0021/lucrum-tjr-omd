## Summary
TJR's take-profit rules. He targets 1–3 hour moves on 5m/15m/1H timeframes, so TPs must come from the 4H and 1H (his bias timeframes), not daily levels. TPs are placed at "high confluence areas where price could potentially reverse" against the trade — i.e., the same confluences he uses for entries (draws on liquidity, order blocks, FVGs, breakers, sometimes equilibrium). He uses multiple TPs (scaling out) and never moves/extends TPs or stops once set. Example: a recent ES (inferred) 5m short with 3 TPs at hourly liquidity, hourly liquidity, and a 4H order block.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Holding horizon** — "we're trying to catch like one to three hour moves we're not trying to catch daily moves unless you're a swing trader." [01:53][02:28]
- **TP purpose** — Marks where price could reverse against your trade; a set place to cash out. [04:43][05:18]
- **TP locations = confluences** — draws on liquidity, order blocks, FVGs, breakers, sometimes equilibrium (the opposite-direction entry confluences). [10:24][10:56]
- **TP timeframe** — primarily 4H and 1H confluences/liquidity; if none, scale to 15m liquidity; he doesn't like setting TPs off 15m/5m otherwise. [11:31][12:39]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Don't set 5m-entry TPs at daily levels/draws. [02:28][03:35][04:08]
- No arbitrary fixed-tick TPs or stops (e.g., "five ticks"); use structure-based levels. [04:43][05:18][05:51]
- Use multiple TPs so runners can run. [05:51]
- Stop goes above/below the area that invalidates the trade idea. [05:51][06:24]
- "Never extend your take profits, never move your take profits" (or stop) once set. [06:57][07:30]
- Account for HTF opposite bias: if daily is bullish but 4H & 1H bearish, shorts are OK but take profits at 4H/1H levels where daily could resume up. [08:39][09:13]

## Process / order of operations
1. Enter per model.
2. Mark 1H/4H draws on liquidity and confluences in trade direction → TP1, TP2, TP3.
3. If none, use 15m liquidity. [12:39]

## Confluences mentioned
- Same as entry confluences, used as targets. [10:56]

## Trade examples
- ~Apr 1 2024 ("yesterday") | ES (inferred) | NY AM (inferred) | Daily bullish, 4H bearish, 1H bearish → short bias | swept 5m highs | 5m BOS, FVG fill, 1m BOS → short entry | stop above the FVG | TP1 hourly liquidity, TP2 hourly liquidity, TP3 4H order block | exited 1/2 at TP1, half of remaining at TP2, rest at TP3 (as he describes) | win ("crushed this trade") | lesson: TPs protect against daily bias reasserting. ## Risk & psychology rules
- Don't be greedy/over-ambitious; never extend TPs/stops — moving them makes it impossible to learn why you won/lost. [04:43][06:57][07:30]
- Losses are learning experiences. [06:57]
