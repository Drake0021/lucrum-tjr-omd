## Summary
This is a short conceptual intro to equilibrium (EQ). The first ~3 minutes are banter about his new Miami studio setup. EQ measures from swing high to swing low (or the reverse) to split price into premium and discount. Market makers want to buy at a discount, like a "50% off" grocery item. EQ is a continuation confluence. It is meant to be paired with an FVG, OB or breaker block, not used to predict reversals. Identification comes in pt.2.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Equilibrium** — It "measures the point from swing high to swing low or vice versa," where price is in a premium or a discount, depending on the trend. [04:15–04:47]
- **Rationale** — Banks and market makers want the best price: buy in a discount, not a premium (grocery 50%-off analogy). [05:55–07:07]
- **Continuation confluence** — EQ does not identify reversals. In an uptrend (HH/HL), if price dips into discount and buying power comes out, price likely continues up. [07:07–07:43]
- **Quality of the dip** — If price "barely" dips and continues, that "probably wasn't the best time to enter." A dip into discount with strong buying pressure "or buying confluences out of it" is what he wants. [08:17]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Use EQ **paired with another confluence** (FVG, OB, breaker block), with a reaction (buying/selling power) out of the discount or premium area. [08:17–08:52]
