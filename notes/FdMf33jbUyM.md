## Summary
A pre-market daily-bias video for Fri 2024-01-12, a **PPI day** (CPI was the day before). ES: the daily is bullish after breaking highs, ES is near all-time highs, and the 4H/1H swept lows and filled an imbalance. PPI prints bullish during the recording and a 15m BOS up is forming, so the draws are the highs above. NQ: the daily is not yet bullish but has closed above a daily FVG, which he calls an inverse FVG while saying he is unsure how he feels about IFVGs. He says he is **not trading** because it is a news day, and he advises viewers not to trade. He describes high-impact news days as a "coin flip" and as the days when HTF draws on liquidity get hit. The second half (from ~[09:41]) is a non-trading PC-building stream, ignored.

## Concepts & definitions
- **HTF draws on liquidity get hit on news days** — "the high time frame draws on liquidity ... those are the days that those high time frame draws are going to get hit ... your high impact news days" [04:02–04:35].
- **News day = coin flip** — the hard part on a news day is knowing whether the impulsive move will continue through a high or sweep it and reverse: "that's why I really call it like a coin flip" [02:55–03:30].
- **Inverse FVG (early 2024 view)** — NQ closed above a daily FVG: "I know a lot of jits use this and I don't really know how I feel about it but this is essentially an inverse fair value gap" [05:12].
- **Profit taking at liquidity** — when liquidity is hit, "there's going to be profit taking from these areas", so expect at least a slight reaction against the move [06:53].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- **News-day filter:** "I'm personally not trading today, I would suggest that you guys don't trade today" (PPI) [04:02]. "Even though I don't condone trading on news day whatsoever" [09:06].
- Bias stack: the daily (bullish after a close above highs) → weekly continuation (near ATH) → the 4H/1H sweep of lows plus an imbalance fill = bullish [00:35–01:47].
- Targets = HTF areas of liquidity (highs), the major draws in order, then all-time highs [01:47–02:21].
- Play both sides once targets are hit [06:21–07:26]:
  - (a) Continuation: liquidity hit → price retraces into the new imbalance → reactions there → long.
  - (b) Reversal: a sweep of the high → 5m/1m BOS down → FVG forms → the FVG fills → reaction → enter short (1m execution possible). Target the retracement points (an OB, the FVG left by the up-move, the EQ of swing low → swing high), with extended TPs in case the sweep is valid [07:26–09:06].
- Targets/TPs sit at high-confluence areas that could reverse price; this is "why I do profit taking and take profits the way that I do" [08:34–09:06].

## Process / order of operations
1. Check the news (CPI yesterday, PPI today) [00:00].
2. Daily structure → weekly context → 4H → 1H; mark sweeps and imbalance fills [00:35–01:47].
3. Mark draws on liquidity in order (nearest highs → major draw → ATH) [01:47].
4. After the release, read the 15m for a BOS [02:21].
5. At the open: has price reacted off the first high or pushed through it? Decide from that [03:30].
6. Execute with building blocks (sweep → BOS → FVG fill → reaction), on the 5m/1m [07:26–08:34].
