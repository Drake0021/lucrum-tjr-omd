## Summary
TJR introduces an old forex strategy (favorite pair GBPJPY): mark Asian (20:00 ET), London (03:00 ET) and New York (08:00 ET) session opens (ignore Australian), then mark each session's high and low on the 30-minute chart as boxes (candle body base to wick extreme). Price tends to sweep a session high/low then draw to the opposite session level ("liquidity sweeps of session highs and lows, then taking a trade to the next draw"). He says this is forex-specific and he has never used it on indexes and doesn't think it applies well to them; his futures/boot-camp strategy still works on forex. No executions yet — homework is to mark sessions and observe.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Session opens (NY time)** — Asian 20:00, London 03:00, New York 08:00; ignore Australian. Set chart to New York time. [02:50][03:23]
- **Session high/low boxes** — On the 30m chart, box from the base (body) of the extreme candle to its wick high/low. Different from general liquidity marking (which marks every high/low). [04:38][05:13]
- **What makes a high** — "a move up then a move down" (used to define the session high). [09:08]
- **Concept** — session highs/lows act as liquidity sweep points or "support and resistance"; trades go from one session's level to the next draw (previous session high/low). [06:19][06:52][12:13]
- **Market sentiment** — Use overall trend (e.g., hourly sweep of lows then strong move up = bullish sentiment). [05:47][06:19]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Mark session opens and 30m session highs/lows every session. [07:27][14:23]
- Pattern: price drives into a prior session low (base-to-low box), sweeps it, 5m BOS, then targets previous session highs. [08:36]
- Not every session works ("failed attempt" examples); execution details deferred to next video. [12:13][12:48]

## Process / order of operations
1. Set chart to NY time; mark Asian/London/NY opens. [02:50]
2. Mark each session's 30m high/low as boxes. [05:13]
3. Read overall sentiment. [05:47]
4. Watch for sweep of a session level + 5m BOS → target opposite session level. [08:36]

## Confluences mentioned
- Session highs/lows (forex) — primary for this strategy. [06:52]

## Trade examples
- GBPJPY | Asian session drives into NY session lows, sweeps, 5m BOS → price targets previous session highs. [08:03][08:36]
- GBPJPY | London lows swept → NY session hits London highs. [09:08][09:40]
- Failed attempt: drive into London lows, small push up, no London highs, sell-off. [12:48]
