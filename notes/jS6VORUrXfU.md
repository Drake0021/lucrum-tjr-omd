## Summary
Daily bias for Thu Jan 11, 2024 (CPI + unemployment claims day). **He is not trading because of CPI** and says it is "best rule of thumb to not trade on high-impact news days." The previous day's bias played out: NQ swept lows and a trade hit every TP. ES broke daily structure back up (reacting off the weekly), so the HTF draw = the weekly highs, with a 4H imbalance and an area of accumulation below as long zones. NQ is bullish above its 4H imbalance and he wants a sweep of the hourly lows, then a run to the equal highs and 4H highs. The rest is Bitcoin ETF commentary.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Daily BOS up after weekly reaction** — "we could just be reacting off the weekly time frame right now and it looks like we are, because we just broke daily structure back to the upside, so now our draw on liquidity is the top of these weekly highs." [00:35]
- **"Poke and go" / quick dip** — if price wants the highs today, "it'll just be quick boom, dip and then push higher"; don't expect a deep drop into a far zone. [02:49]
- **Uncertain closes** — when a candle close above an imbalance is marginal: "you would much rather just be certain about it, so... just wait for one more candle." [04:31]
- **Untouched liquidity** — hourly lows that are "close, but still untouched" remain liquidity. [05:40]
- **Relatively equal highs** — a draw on liquidity. [04:31]–[05:04]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
