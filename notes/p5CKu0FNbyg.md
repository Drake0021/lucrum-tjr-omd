## Summary
TJR explains his "Seek and Destroy" observation: after the NY-open move (sweep → BOS → FVG/IFVG entry), price often comes back at around 10:15–11:00 ET to take out the early entries / breakeven stops / 5m–15m lows made on the first leg. Then it completes the real HTF move toward the 4H/1H draw. He frames it as (1) a management lesson: do not be greedy, take profits on the early AM trades because breakeven stops often get hit; and (2) a possible second entry: after the "seek and destroy" liquidation, look for a sweep → BOS → FVG/BPR entry toward the unfilled HTF draw. He walks through about a week of NQ and ES examples (late Aug 2024) and says he had not been trading it but will start. He also says his normal entries are in the 9:50–10:10 "macro" (9:45–10:10) and he does not trade the PM session.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Seek and Destroy** — after the initial open move, price retraces to "seek out" the typical entry points: the BOS level, the filled FVG, or the consolidation-breakout candle. It also takes out the lows (for longs) or highs (for shorts) made during the first leg, i.e. 5m/15m draws on liquidity, stopping out early entrants at breakeven or fully. Then it continues to the HTF target. "Find somebody's typical entry and then you look for when price is looking to wipe that out." [01:36]–[03:16] [07:46] [24:30]
- **Timing** — "typically ... around 10:30"; a range of 10:15/10:20 to 11:00 ET, examples at 10:15, 10:20, 10:25, 10:40, 10:45, 11:05, 11:15, and 11:30 (later, weaker). [01:36] [05:34] [07:14] [11:39] [38:20]
- **Typical entry location being hunted** — on the 5m and 15m: the consolidation breakout candle, the BOS candle, the FVG fill. [08:19] [25:03]
- **Low resistance liquidity** — lows/highs left on the first leg that price can easily run; he uses the phrase for sell-side below and for liquidity on the way back. [05:34] [35:27]
- **Equal highs** — "one of the strongest draws on liquidity", especially when they are an HTF high. [34:53]–[35:27]
- **Macro (9:50–10:10)** — the window where most first trades are taken ("that macro of 9:50 to 10:10"); later stated as "9:45 to like 10:10". [05:34] [38:20]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- First trade window: the NY open "macro" 9:50–10:10 (also stated as 9:45–10:10). [05:34] [38:20]
- Second window (Seek and Destroy): about 10:20 to 11:00. "Really three [windows], I just don't trade PM session." [38:20]
- Management: "don't get too optimistic or too greedy with your take profits ... take profits, reward yourself for being right", especially on early market-open trades, because seek-and-destroy often stops you out at breakeven or fully. [36:06]–[36:40]
- If you are stopped at BE and price then liquidates the first-leg lows, and the daily-bias draw (4H high / 1H high) has not been hit yet, start looking for entries in the bias direction again. [37:14]–[37:46]
- Seek-and-destroy re-entry: after the liquidation sweep (e.g. 5m lows taken, the early entry wicked), wait for a reversal: BOS → FVG/imbalance/BPR fill → enter, targeting the HTF draw (4H high, 1H high, equal highs). [06:40] [15:17] [33:45]
- How to recognise it: price "comes down, barely hits [the early entry] and then it's off to the races" with a strong, heavy reversal and smooth moves in the bias direction. [36:40]–[37:14]
- Ask whether the first leg took out any significant liquidity. If it did not even take 15m draws, the HTF move is still pending, so expect seek-and-destroy and a continuation. [06:06]–[06:40]
- If no market-open setup appeared, look for optimal trade entries on the 15m/5m being "swept out, seek and destroyed", and enter off that. [37:46]
- He is explicit that this should not deter you from taking the morning trades; it is "an extra opportunity", and he warns against overtrading. [20:57] [31:51]–[32:25]

## Process / order of operations
1. Know the HTF draw and bias for the day (e.g. a 4H high, 1H high or equal highs). [30:08] [34:53]
2. NY open: sweep of liquidity → BOS → FVG/IFVG entry within ~9:50–10:10 (his normal trade). [11:39] [05:34]
3. Take partial profits; do not get greedy. [36:06]
4. 10:15–11:00: watch for price returning to the early entry / BOS / FVG area and taking the 5m/15m lows (highs) made on the first leg. [05:34] [11:39]
5. On a strong reversal from there: BOS → FVG/BPR fill → re-enter toward the unfilled HTF draw. [06:40] [33:45]
