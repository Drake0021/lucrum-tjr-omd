## Summary
A beginner price-action lesson. Market makers need opposite-side orders to fill their size ("opposite day"), so moves start with **manipulation**: a run above highs to induce buys before a drop, or below lows to induce sells before a rally. That is followed by a **break of structure** (the trend change) and then **trend continuation**. For a late entry he uses a multi-timeframe fractal: a higher-timeframe (15m) retrace appears on the lower timeframe (5m) as a mini counter-trend. Enter when the 5m breaks structure back in the HTF direction. Examples: S&P weekly (2025 tariff crash = HTF liquidity sweep) and a 15m/5m short on a news day.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Order-filling logic** — to push price up, market makers need sell orders to fill their buys, and vice versa. The market "needs to induce" the opposite side [01:06–02:45; 05:32].
- **Uptrend / downtrend** — higher highs and higher lows / lower highs and lower lows. Trends move in extension-retrace steps, not straight lines, because price retraces "to fill more buy orders" [03:17–04:23].
- **Manipulation (= liquidity sweep)** — price moves above a high (inducing retail buys) or below a low (inducing sells), which opens the possibility of a reversal. "It's when price moves above a high and then boom, starts breaking structure to the downside" [07:47; 11:06]. Example: the S&P tariff crash took "one, two, and even three very key lows" on the weekly [07:14–08:21].
- **Break of structure (BOS)** — uptrend broken when "the most recent low gets closed underneath"; downtrend broken by closing above the most recent high, specifically the high that led into the lows being taken [12:47–13:20; 14:58]. This is the confirmation that the opposite orders were filled.
- **Price action sequence** — "We look for manipulation. Then we look for a break of structure. Then from there, we're looking for a trend continuation" [21:04]. "This is the only way that price action is able to move" [06:39].
- **Retracement seen on a lower timeframe** — a single 15m counter-candle can be a mini BOS / mini trend on the 5m [17:43–18:16].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Never sell just because price pushed above a high, or buy because it pushed below a low. In a trend that loses repeatedly. The sweep only "opens our eyes" [12:13].
- Reversal entry: IF a high is swept AND the most recent low is then closed below (BOS), THEN short. Mirror for longs [12:47–13:20; 14:25–14:58].
- Don't buy into new highs ("we don't want to get into an uptrend when we're all the way up here"); buy on pullbacks [11:39; 16:05].
- Continuation entry (late): with the 15m trend down, wait for a 15m retrace. On the 5m that retrace forms a mini uptrend (5m bullish BOS). Enter short when the 5m breaks structure back down (close below its most recent low) [17:11–20:31].
- Mirror for longs: when the 15m retrace shows as a 5m downtrend, enter on a 5m BOS back up [18:16–18:50].
- He accepts some drawdown after the 5m BOS entry ("you get stuck in a little bit of draw down") [20:31].
- Price action > news: "This was very drastic because there was a news event today, but still price action remains king" [10:33].
- Applies on all timeframes: weekly, 4H, 1H, 15m, 5m, 1m [09:27; 21:04].

## Process / order of operations
1. Identify the current trend [03:17].
2. Wait for manipulation: a sweep of a high (for shorts) or a low (for longs) [11:39].
3. Wait for the BOS: a close beyond the most recent opposite swing [13:20].
4. Enter on the BOS, or, if late, wait for the next HTF retrace and enter on the LTF BOS back in the trend direction [19:57–20:31].
