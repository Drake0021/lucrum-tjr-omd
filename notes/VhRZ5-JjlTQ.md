## Summary
First episode of a series with Sri, an 18-year-old student in India. The goal is to turn $100 into a funded-account payout and eventually $10k months. The video covers basics only: what trading is (buy low/sell high, or sell high/buy low), candlestick anatomy (open/high/low/close), timeframes on TradingView (4H, 1H, 1m) and what a prop firm is. They pick a Lucid Trading "daily" evaluation (25K, end-of-day drawdown, no daily loss limit, ~$100). No confluences or trades. Confluences are promised for the next episodes.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Candlestick** — shows open, high, low, close for its period. Up candle: opens low, closes high. Down candle: opens high, closes low. Wicks = the high and low reached [09:02–13:04].
- **Timeframe** — how much time each candle represents (4H, 1H, down to 1m). TradingView shows a countdown to the next candle close [14:09–16:20].
- **Prop firm / evaluation** — pay a challenge fee; hit a profit target without exceeding max loss; then trade firm capital for payouts [18:33–20:14].
- **Consistency rule (eval)** — e.g., 50%: no single day/trade may exceed 50% of total profit, so at least two winning days are needed [21:57–22:32].
- **End-of-day vs intraday drawdown** — EOD drawdown resets at the day's end. Intraday trails the balance in real time, which makes passing harder [23:06–23:41].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Lucid "daily" 50K example: profit target $3,000, max loss $2,000, 50% consistency, so make ~$1,500 on day 1 and ~$1,500 on day 2 [21:20–22:32].
- Account chosen for the $100 budget: **$25K Lucid daily**, EOD drawdown, **no daily loss limit** (to reach the target "as quick as possible"), ≈$100.40 [22:32–24:16].

- Expect several attempts (e.g., 3 × $100 challenges vs a $1,000 payout is still positive ROI) [27:04–27:37].
