## Summary
Short update video on a second trade of the day, an S&P 500 long, taken with the same "accumulation area" approach as that day's gold trade. Price swept 4-hour lows, broke structure up, and extended out of an accumulation area. He did not enter on the first touch. He waited for a sweep of nearby lows and a retest of the accumulation area / order block, then entered on a 1-minute break of structure. TP1 hit, half closed, stop to breakeven. This is an early look at the Boot Camp 2.0 "area of accumulation prior to the leg" model, which he says he will explain fully in a separate strategy video.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Area of accumulation** — the consolidation / range of candles just before a strong extension (leg). He treats it as the place "where orders were filled" before the leg and expects price to come back to it. He uses it interchangeably with "order block". [00:32], [01:06]
- **Extension** — the strong leg out of the accumulation area after a liquidity sweep + break of structure. [00:32]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Context: higher-timeframe liquidity sweep (here 4H lows) → break of structure in the bias direction → extension out of an accumulation area. Then wait for price to come back into that accumulation area / order block. [00:00]–[00:32]
- If price leaves without filling the FVG / accumulation area and instead sweeps nearby lows, keep waiting. The "retest of this order block after sweeping these lows" is the trigger zone. [00:32]–[01:06]
- Entry: after the retest, drop to the 1-minute and enter on a 1m break of structure. [01:06]
- Stop: under the lows (the sweep lows) made in the retest. [01:39]
- TP1: the nearby highs. At TP1, close half the position and move the stop to breakeven ("risk-free trade"). Leave a runner with alerts set at BE and TP2. TP2 is the accumulation area before the prior big drop (inferred from "this little accumulation area prior to this big drop"). [01:39]–[02:13]
- Session filter: he does not expect anything significant during Asian pre-market / Asian session hours and is not actively managing then. [01:39]

## Process / order of operations
1. Identify the HTF liquidity sweep (4H lows) and the structure break in that direction.
2. Mark the accumulation area / order block that the extension came out of.
3. Wait for price to return to it. Not every first touch counts: here a first touch failed, then lows were swept.
4. Scale to the 1m and enter on a 1m BOS. Stop goes under the sweep lows.
5. At TP1, take 50% off and move the stop to BE. Hold the runner to further targets.
