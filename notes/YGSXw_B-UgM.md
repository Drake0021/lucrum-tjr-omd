## Summary
First daily-bias video of 2024, recorded about an hour before the NY open (from a cruise ship). He announces the switch from offshore MT4/MT5 forex brokers to **futures (S&P and NASDAQ focus)** and says he will trade on demo all week while learning the platform. ES/NQ bias: monthly/weekly/daily draw = all-time highs, but price is moving down on the 4H. The intraday bias is bearish: wait for a push up into a 15m imbalance (or a higher order block), get a 15m/5m BOS down, and target the daily lows and the weekly FVG. The rest is Bitcoin HTF commentary ("buy the chop in a bull market") and mentorship promotion.

## Concepts & definitions
- **HTF draw on liquidity by timeframe** — monthly, weekly and daily draws all = all-time highs on ES, yet "does that mean we're instantly going to push up to it? no, not necessarily, because currently we're moving down." The HTF draw does not override the current 4H direction for the day. [03:26]
- **4H sweep pending candle close** — price is "already past these lows on the 4 hour... this could be a sweep of liquidity; we've got 32 minutes until this 4-hour candle closes." Whether it is a sweep or a break depends on the close (closed-candle rule). [03:26]–[03:59]
- **Order block (NQ)** — "boom push up before big flood to the downside": the last up-leg before the displacement down. "The leg up before the leg down has some significance." [07:26]–[08:33]
- **Imbalance (FVG) refinement on lower TF** — he scales into the 15m to find exactly where the daily/4H imbalance starts: "it's here down to here; anything within here is going to be fair game." [07:26]–[08:01]
- **Prior day's low as significant level** — "that was the low of the previous daily candle, so that's pretty significant." [08:01]
- **Bank holiday / New Year** — "yesterday was a bank holiday, technically no money put into the market for the new year... people will come out firing once the bell rings." Expect a push up at the open, then chop, then the move. [05:46]; [08:33]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- ES bias for the day (bearish): ideally price draws up into the 15m imbalance (or, less likely, the higher order block), then gives "some sort of action... on the 15 minute or the 5 minute", "probably looking for break of structure on the 15 or the 5 minute", then targets first the daily-low draw, then the weekly imbalance. [05:13]–[06:20]
- Preferred scenario: the next 4H candle wicks up into the imbalance and closes back down, then price seeks the downside draws. [06:20]
- The HTF reversal long is only considered after the daily draw/weekly imbalance is reached: "then from there we can wait... monitor the lower time frames like on the 4 hour and wait for reversal." [04:36]
- NQ: same idea. Mark the 15m-refined imbalance, the OB (leg up before the flood down) and the prior day low; look for action off the imbalance and target the daily lows and 50% of the weekly imbalance. [07:26]–[08:33]
- Day after a bank holiday / first trading day of the year: "there shouldn't be too much action today... don't feel the pressure to trade today." [15:18]

## Process / order of operations
1. Monthly → weekly → daily draws (all-time highs). [03:26]
2. 4H: is the current move a sweep (wait for the 4H close)? If not, shift to the next daily draw below. [03:59]
3. Mark the 15m imbalance / OB above as the retracement zone and the downside draws as targets. [05:13]
4. At the open: wait for the retracement into the zone + 15m/5m BOS down → execute. [05:13]
