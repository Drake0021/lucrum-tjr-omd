## Summary
A 2026 Friday live stream. TJR pulls viewers onto a Discord call and asks for their bias and plan. Their answers use the 2026 vocabulary (hourly IFVG, 5m retrace into FVG/EQ, 1m BOS trigger, SMT), and he comments on each. His own read at the open: bullish 1H and 4H, previous-day high taken, all-time highs, so he prefers longs. He spells out a bearish SMT scenario and a bullish alignment scenario. Price rips higher without a retrace and he gets no long entry (no 1m BOS down on the retrace). At ~10:32 ET he takes a quick short scalp from a 5m FVG retrace without a 1m BOS, targeting stacked lows. He is stopped for −$52,660 and says he "learned my lesson" about assuming low-resistance lows will be taken. There is also a joke "TJR funded account" game (1:1, 100-tick stop/target) that has no method value.

## Concepts & definitions
- **All-time-high bias** — "higher time frame holds higher power and we're trading at all-time highs ... more often than not at all-time highs typically you just want to look for longs." [10:58]–[11:32]
- **Bearish SMT setup (ES vs NQ)** — NQ takes out its highs while ES sweeps into its 5m gap but stays *under* the pre-market highs. That is a bearish SMT; then NQ should inverse the gap it puts in. "We don't need to wait for any sort of change in order flow on ES" because ES is already bearish. [12:41]–[13:47]; [14:19]–[14:53]
- **Bullish alignment scenario** — NQ already breaking 5m structure up. ES must flip too ("put in a high, break structure to the upside" or "inverse this gap"), then a 5m retrace, then long into price discovery. "So that both the indexes are aligned." [12:06]; [13:47]; [14:53]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- **A with-trend retrace must first show a 1m BOS against the trend:** "we had this mini retrace here, but we never broke structure to the downside on the one minute. And most of the time when we do [stuff] like that, I want to see a break structure to the downside on the one minute." For a long, the pullback must break 1m structure down; only then does he look for the retrace into FVG/EQ and a 1m BOS back up. The same sequence was endorsed for students at [37:56] ("we're waiting for a break of structure to the downside") and [40:10] (5m/15m BOS or IFVG → 5m retrace → 1m BOS). Without that pullback break, no entry. [1:09:23]–[1:09:55]
- **After a stop-out, re-entry needs a new 1m order-flow change:** to chat "enter now": "I wouldn't do all that. I would at least wait for the one minute to change order flow back to the downside." [1:24:41]–[1:25:14]
- **Earliest entry time:** a viewer longed at 9:45 after a 1m BOS. TJR: "that was too early into the market for me ... I usually wait until 9:50." [43:27]–[43:58]
