## Summary
A no-chart talk on how top traders differ: not strategy, but risk management and **selective participation**. They trade only high-probability days (about 8 days/month, 5–8 trades/month) and their large accounts make 1% days meaningful. He gives his own day filters. High-probability day = **no high-impact news** (CPI, PPI, NFP, FOMC, Powell; "if I see a single red folder... gone") **and** the timeframes aligned (weekly + daily + 4H retracing into the next daily leg). Skewed bias across timeframes = walk away. If price isn't in a Boot Camp building block, or there are no confluences, he leaves. He also lays out the account progression: demo → $100 → $1,000 → funded → scale slowly, to build risk tolerance.

## Concepts & definitions
- **High-probability day** — (1) no high-impact news; (2) the instrument's timeframes are aligned with the daily bias, e.g. weekly downtrend + daily downtrend + 4H "retracing perfectly on the daily and setting up to make that next daily leg down" = trade. [03:57]–[04:31]
- **Skewed bias** — e.g. "weekly up, daily down, 4H up, hourly down" (or weekly down / daily up / 4H consolidating / 1H bouncing off the walls) → "I'm leaving, I'm not trading that day." [04:31]–[05:41]
- **Psychology of size** — moving to a larger account changes behavior (closing profits early, cutting trades before SL/TP); scale size slowly. [06:17]–[09:36]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- **News filter:** "I try and avoid high-impact news days like CPI, PPI, NFP and FOMC, or whenever Federal Chairman Powell steps on the stage." [02:45]
- **Red-folder rule:** "If I go in, type up Forex Factory and I see a single red folder out of there — gone." [03:57]
- **No setup = leave:** "If my daily bias is not set up, if it's not within a building block that I talk about in my boot camps... if I don't see any confluences... I'm walking out." [03:19]–[03:57]
- **Bias-alignment filter:** trade only when weekly, daily and 4H agree (the 4H retracing within the daily trend and setting up the next leg). Conflicting TFs = no trade. [04:31]–[05:41]
- Risk: 1% per trade as the reference ("if you got a $10 million account you risk 1%... six figures in a day at 1:1"). [02:11]–[02:45]
- Ideal frequency (for big accounts): about 8 trading days per month, "five to eight trades a month". He trades more for now, to grow capital. [01:39]; [05:41]; [15:42]
- Capital progression: demo (3 months profitable) → $100 live (3 months) → $1,000 → funded → grow slowly; withdraw 50% of profits and compound 50%. [07:22]–[11:18]

## Process / order of operations
1. Morning: check ForexFactory; any red folder (high-impact) → no trade. [03:57]
2. Check weekly/daily/4H/1H alignment → skewed = no trade. [04:31]–[05:06]
3. Is price within a Boot Camp building block with confluences? No → leave. [03:19]
4. Take the setup, let it hit TP/SL, walk away. [11:52]; [14:37]

## Risk & psychology rules
- Don't trade every day; avoid low-probability days; "the market's still going to be here tomorrow." [12:24]; [15:10]
- Don't full-port; use risk management. [14:01]
- Increase account size and risk gradually so your psychology adapts (a $1k → $2k → $3k per-trade progression on $100k; doubling the account → $2k/$4k/$6k). [09:36]–[10:45]
- When moving to a funded account with "real" withdrawable money, beware closing profits early or cutting trades before SL/TP. [08:29]–[09:02]
