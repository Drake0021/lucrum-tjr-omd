## Summary
Opinion piece (Aug 2023, during/just after Boot Camp 1) arguing that smart money concepts (SMC) cannot "get patched" because liquidity sweeps are how orders get filled and how the market moves. The trading substance is his account of a losing week caused by entering on a bare liquidity sweep + BOS, after which he added a mandatory third confirmation (EQ fill / OB tap / FVG respected, each with a rejection) and alignment with daily bias, followed by ~2 weeks without a loss. He restates his thought process (sweep → BOS → retrace into FVG/OB/EQ → rejection) and tells viewers to stop strategy-hopping and fix execution.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Liquidity / liquidity sweep** — the market "has to take out liquidity" to fill large orders; liquidity is "the most important thing"; OBs, FVGs, EQ are "ticky-tack" execution tools by comparison. [02:13]
- **Origin: London session fake-out** — his first discovery (~3 years before) on GBPJPY: London open spikes high → he sold, spikes low → he bought; later recognized as a liquidity sweep. [01:09]
- Credits ICT for order blocks, FVGs, equilibrium. [01:41]
- He trades two strategies: "my Forex strategy" and "my S&P 500 strategy", both built on these concepts. [01:41]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Do NOT enter purely off liquidity sweep + BOS. [04:26]
- Required sequence (as of Aug 2023): daily bias (e.g., bullish) → liquidity sweep against it (to the downside for longs) → BOS in bias direction (upside) → third confirmation: EQ filled, OR OB tapped, OR FVG respected — then a rejection off it. [04:26] [06:40]
- Everything must be in line with daily bias. [04:26]
- Third confirmation = price retraces into FVG / OB / EQ "and then give us a rejection". Touch alone not stated as enough. [06:40]

## Process / order of operations
1. Liquidity swept → potential move. [06:07]
2. Wait for BOS (confirms orders were filled). [06:07]
3. "Hold off" — wait for retrace into FVG, OB, or EQ. [06:40]
4. Rejection off that zone → enter. [06:40]
