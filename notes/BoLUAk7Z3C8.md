## Concepts & definitions
- **Weekly retrace from a daily BOS** — "the S&P broke daily structure to the downside as of last week, which to me kind of confirms we're looking for a weekly retrace": targets = the midline of the weekly FVG, then the bottom; after that, reversals back to the ATH. [01:12]–[01:44]
- **Equilibrium via Gann box** — "we can use Gann box to find equilibrium" of the daily retrace leg. [02:17]
- **Order block (NQ hourly)** — after the sweep, the leg that started the move up is the OB price may return to. [04:30]
- **Relative equal lows** — a liquidity target for a market-open sweep. [04:30]
