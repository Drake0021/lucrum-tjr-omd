## Summary
A full NY session on a Friday in April 2026 with ES at new all-time highs. TJR explains a key SMT/index rule: **to confirm a direction, listen to the index that is most opposed to it** (for longs, wait for the more bearish index; for shorts, wait for the more bullish index), then trade the index that already leans your way. He first shorted NQ when ES (the more bullish index) broke 5m structure down at ~9:48 ET. That went against him, a bullish SMT formed (NQ higher low vs ES lower low), and price started inverting gaps. He cut the short for −$44K and flipped long on NQ (~9:50 ET). He held through a long, choppy retrace, moved the stop to BE, and took partials at NQ 5m highs (~10:40 ET) with NQ's all-time high as final TP. He closed everything ~11:11 ET for +$75,210 net (2 trades: 1 loss, 1 win).

## Concepts & definitions
- **Which index to listen to** — "why should we listen to the more bullish index about why we should go higher when instead we just have to wait for the more bearish index to finally say, you know what, we are going to go higher" (water-lover vs water-hater analogy) [04:39–05:48]. For shorts: "I would go over to ES because ES is more bullish. And I would wait for ES to tell me ... it's actually bearish" [06:24–06:56]. Applied: "We did break structure on NASDAQ. So ... we don't want to take the trade on NASDAQ because we need ES to break structure to the downside as well ... If ES breaks structure ... I'll want to take shorts on NASDAQ" [13:50–14:24].
- **More bullish/bearish index** — the index that took the HTF high (ES took ATHs) is more bullish [03:28–04:03]. Midway he reassesses with SMT: "NQ's more bullish ... because of the bullish SMT at the bottom" [19:57–20:31]. Later he calls NQ "clearly the more bearish index today" [59:06].
- **Don't fade fresh ATH breaks with SMT** — "saying that we have a bearish SMT at all-time highs right now is kind of stupid ... trying to be bearish against an index that just took out previous all-time highs ... is pretty dumb" [01:42].
- **Mark the opposing confluences and want them disrespected** — after a 5m BOS against your bias, mark the bearish FVGs that "should hold". Bullish confirmation = those gaps being disrespected (inverted). The relevant gap is the highest one; "even if ES disrespects this first gap, I don't care ... there's a gap above it" [02:54–04:39].
- **Fake respect candle before inversion** — "before inversing a gap, we'll come up and then we'll give a candle ... like it's getting respected [a hammer/inverted hammer] and then we'll end up inversing it" [09:49].
- **5m extension can retrace via a 1m BOS** — within a 5m uptrend, a 1m BOS down that fills a 5m gap or sweeps 1m lows is still valid trend behavior. "Just sweep these lows and then continue higher" [34:05–34:39; 39:10; 44:33].
- **Liquidation event** — a big down candle that is fully engulfed by the next up candle ("ate that entire down candle") = the retrace is done; continuation likely [1:23:36–1:24:11].
