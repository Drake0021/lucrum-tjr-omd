## Summary
A dedicated equilibrium (EQ) lesson from February 2026. EQ is the 50% of the **most recent** swing low → swing high (uptrend) or most recent swing high → swing low (downtrend). He draws it with a Gann box. It divides premium from discount. EQ is a **continuation confluence**: price retraces into discount (longs) or premium (shorts), shows orders, then continues. He shows 4H downtrend examples, a 1H/15m bullish example and a daily context read. He restates the strategy: manipulation/liquidity sweep → market structure shift (BOS or IFVG) → continuation via EQ or FVG → entry → target significant draws on liquidity. He states plainly: **"I no longer use order blocks. I no longer use breaker blocks."** The only continuation confluences he uses are EQ and FVGs.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Equilibrium** — in an uptrend, the 0.5 of the range from the **most recent swing low up to the most recent swing high**. In a downtrend, from the **most recent swing high down to the most recent swing low**. Tool: Gann box [01:06–01:40; 02:47–03:19; 06:04–07:09; 08:51].
- **Premium / discount** — above EQ = premium, below EQ = discount. Buy in discount in an uptrend; short in premium in a downtrend [01:40–02:14; 03:52].
- **Anchoring rule** — always the latest swing pair. Do not anchor from an older low when there are more recent lows in front of it [06:38–07:09]. EQ can be drawn only once the new swing (the high in an uptrend) is confirmed, i.e. after the first opposite candle prints ("once we put in this downward candlestick ... we have a low, then we have a high") [06:04; 13:15].
- **Continuation vs reversal confluence** — EQ is a **continuation confluence** (retrace into it, then the trend continues). A **liquidity sweep** is a **reversal confluence** [07:42]. Fits the IFVG lesson (BdBxXKGWVjk), where FVGs are continuation and IFVG/BOS are shift signals.
- **Confirmation out of EQ** — after price enters premium/discount, "scale down to a lower time frame" and look for a "change in order flow ... whether it's a break of structure or an inverse fair value gap" → entry [08:51]. On the 4H example, a candle closing below the bodies of the prior candles counted as early confirmation before a "full break of structure" [11:04].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Longs only from discount (≤ EQ) in an uptrend; shorts only from premium (≥ EQ) in a downtrend [02:14; 03:52].
- EQ alone is not a trade: "these confluences are literally nothing without context". He needs two other confluences (a sweep + a structure shift) on top of EQ [14:20; 15:58].
- Entry trigger inside the EQ zone = a lower-timeframe BOS or IFVG in the trade direction [08:51; 10:32].
- Targets: the trend's last low/high (to make a new lower low / higher high), then the next "significant draw on liquidity" to take profit [10:32; 17:05].
- Use EQ on both the HTF (where price will retrace to) and the LTF (execution) [12:41].
- Do not use order blocks or breaker blocks. Only EQ and FVGs as continuation confluences [17:05–18:10].

## Process / order of operations
1. Potential order fill: manipulation = liquidity sweep above a high / below a low (a draw on liquidity) [15:25; 16:32].
2. Confirmation that orders filled: a market structure shift (BOS; IFVG also mentioned earlier) [15:25; 08:51].
3. After the shift, mark EQ of the new leg (most recent high → new low for shorts) [15:58–16:32].
4. Wait for price to retrace into premium (shorts) / discount (longs) and show orders (an LTF BOS/IFVG) → enter [16:32].
5. Target: the low just made, then the next significant draw on liquidity [17:05].
