## Summary
After two sub-par weeks and a weekend of backtesting, TJR describes a shift toward trading from the big-picture (monthly, weekly, daily) bias and letting trades come to him, rather than reading ticky-tack lower timeframes. He walks through a weekly news-based trading schedule: no trading on a UK holiday for GBP pairs, likely sitting out heavy 10am news days, trading when news lands an hour before the open, and skipping NFP Friday. He recaps a live gold long built top-down. Monthly lows were swept with the monthly still bullish. The 4H broke the downtrend with a big extension and then filled the FVG back into the accumulation. On the 1H there was a chop down. In New York session, price swept the lows and broke out of the consolidation, and he entered on the 1-minute close through an FVG. TP1 paid about $11k and TP2 about $8.3k, with partials. He also outlines an S&P weekly view based on accumulation areas and why retracements chop while trend extensions are clean.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Big-picture bias first** — Determine where price wants to go long term from the monthly, weekly and daily, and then wait for the trade to come to you. This gives "stop-loss wiggle room" and a higher probability. He contrasts it with getting caught up in lower timeframes. [08:26], [13:28]–[14:01]
- **Monthly liquidity sweep plus monthly trend continuation** — Gold: the weekly was bearish (broke structure down), but price swept the monthly lows while the monthly was still bullish. He reads this as a liquidity sweep before continuation higher. [05:05], [08:26]–[09:00]
- **Accumulation area** — A consolidation before a big leg (extension). Price later tends to draw back into it before continuing. Use it as a POI. [05:05]–[05:38], [10:06], [11:14]
- **Break of structure via a big extension** — On the 4H, after a downtrend, "big extension up" counts as a BOS to the upside. He notes the trend line visually, even though he does not use trend lines. [05:05]–[05:38]
- **Retracements chop, extensions are clean** — Moves against the higher-timeframe volume direction chop; moves with it are big legs. Gold: chop down, then a big rally. Use this to identify which way the "volume wants to go". [14:34]–[15:06]
- **Nested accumulation** — A smaller-timeframe accumulation inside a larger accumulation that caused a leg. This is a high-confluence POI. [11:47]–[12:21]
- **Weekly open** — Marked as a reference; price chopped around it before the sweep. [06:12]
- **Charts over fundamentals** — Follow what the chart says, not macro sentiment (Michael Burry example). [10:39]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- News filters, with his weekly schedule:
  - Bank holiday for a currency: do not trade those pairs (GBP holiday, so no GU or GJ, because there is no volume). [01:09]
  - Heavy high-impact US news at 10:00am ET: probably no trade, or only after the release if something shows. [01:09]
  - High-impact news an hour before the open (NFP-week Wed GDP, Thu unemployment claims): trade after the 9:30 open, because the news gives volume and direction. [01:45]–[02:17]
  - NFP Friday (NFP and unemployment an hour before the open, plus ISM PMI 30 minutes into the open): do not trade at all. [02:17]
- Only take trades in line with monthly, weekly and daily sentiment, "because those are the extensions that are going to happen". [14:34]
- Do not trade counter-trend retracement moves; for example, on S&P he would not trade the move up into the area, he would wait for the move down, and he is "not willing to short yet" without high-timeframe confirmation. [11:47], [12:52]
- Entry sequence (gold long): HTF bias (monthly sweep plus monthly bullish), then a 4H big extension BOS up, then the FVG fill back into the accumulation, then a 1H "big extension down, quick and easy rally up" pattern, then a NY session sweep of the lows and a breakout from the consolidation, then entry on the 1-minute candle close up through the FVG. [05:05]–[06:44]
- "Is that enough to take a trade? No, not yet. We wait for the dip down and then another extension up. We get a break of structure to the upside. We get a sweep of liquidity." Waiting for the accumulation tap alone is not enough; the LTF sweep and BOS are required. [14:01]
- Management: close half the position at TP1, more at TP2, let TP3 run. [03:59], [07:19]
- Targets: high-timeframe areas such as monthly highs. He does not intend to hold for months. [14:01]–[14:34]

## Process / order of operations
1. Weekly: review the economic calendar and decide which days and pairs to trade or skip. [01:09]–[02:17]
2. Monthly and weekly: identify the trend and any monthly sweep; decide the big-picture direction. [05:05], [08:26]
3. Daily and 4H: look for a sweep with an instant reaction and a big extension BOS in the bias direction; mark the accumulation and FVG prior to the leg. [05:05]–[05:38]
4. Wait for price to retrace (chop) back into the accumulation or FVG. [05:38], [09:00]
5. 1H, then LTF: in the session, wait for a liquidity sweep of the local lows and a breakout from the consolidation (BOS). [06:12]
6. Enter on the 1-minute close through the FVG; target HTF liquidity; take partials. [06:44], [07:19]
