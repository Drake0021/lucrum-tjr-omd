## Summary
TJR's PM-session strategy (used while in Hawaii, where the 9:30 ET open is 3:30 a.m.): the same model as AM, shifted down a timeframe tier. 15m = trend/bias (replaces 4H), 5m = draws on liquidity / sweeps (replaces 1H), 1m = execution (BOS + third confluence). Prime time is ~1:30–3:00 PM ET (core macro 2–3 PM). He uses a single take-profit in PM because price is slow. Observations: AM often makes the big volatile move, cools off, and PM continues it; PM likes to sweep the small "gritty, grindy" lows made during the AM partial pullback. Results in Hawaii: NQ 1W/1L, ES 2W — one loss total. Examples on ES/NQ, early May 2024.

## Concepts & definitions
- **PM session timeframe mapping** — "we're treating [15m and 5m] like our 4-hour and our hourly and we're executing on the one minute." 15m = overall bias/direction; 5m = draws on liquidity; 1m = execution. [05:53][07:01]
- **PM prime time** — "macro / kill zone ... from 2 to 3 pm", extendable to 1:30; "from 1:30 to 3:00 PM Eastern is really our prime time." [04:43][05:20]
- **Core model (restated)** — liquidity sweep (in trend direction) → break of structure → third confluence fill (FVG, OB, breaker, equilibrium). [07:34]
- **Unicorn** — 5m breaker in line with 5m FVG ("unicorn, whatever you want to call it"). [20:42]
- **PM sweeps gritty lows** — PM session tends to sweep the small grindy lows built during AM's partial move; don't mark them as formal liquidity but keep in mind. [15:25][15:59][16:31]
- **AM→PM continuation** — AM makes big volatile move, cools off into PM, PM typically continues the move (observation over one week, "don't take that as the golden rule"). [13:41][14:17]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Identify 15m trend. [22:25]
- Look for low-TF liquidity sweep on 5m/15m — "don't be going on the one minute." [22:25]
- Find third confluence on 15m/5m (FVG, OB, breaker; overlapping = better). [08:41][09:48][22:25]
- Execute on 1m: wait for 1m BOS, then a 1m third confluence (OB/breaker/FVG/equilibrium) for entry. [10:20][10:51][23:05]
- Stop: below equilibrium of the 1m leg (first example) or below the order block / whole move down (second). [11:23][17:40]
- Targets: nearby liquidity (hourly high, start/end of imbalances); check RR — reject targets too close to entry. [17:40][18:15]
- PM: one take-profit only (slow price; also personal convenience). His example was 1:1. [11:23][11:58]
- An FVG that got hit but invalidated is not used as entry. [17:04]
- Too-thin "sliver" FVG — he wouldn't take a trade off it. [21:16]
- Equilibrium entry not hit → no trade (unless entering purely on BOS, which he calls dumb). [21:16][21:49]
- He prefers AM session (cleaner moves until ~11:00). Would trade PM again if in PST/Hawaii or if no AM trade. [11:58][23:05]

## Process / order of operations
1. Identify 15-minute trend. [22:25]
2. Low TF liquidity sweep on 5m/15m (not 1m). [22:25]
3. Third confluence within 15m/5m. [23:05]
4. Execute on 1m (BOS + entry confluence). [23:05]

## Confluences mentioned
- 15m/5m FVG, OB, breaker stacked in same area — "scrumptious", higher confluence. [08:41][09:48]
- Equilibrium (50% of leg) for stop placement/entry. [11:23][21:16]
- 4H bias secondary in PM — "regardless the 15 minute is what we're looking at." [09:15]
