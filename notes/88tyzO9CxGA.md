## Summary
A July 2026 psychology lesson built on Mark Douglas's *Trading in the Zone*. He covers 10 lessons and the "five fundamental truths": anything can happen; think in probabilities; accept risk before entry; detach self-worth from single trades; consistency comes from mindset, not strategy; the market owes you nothing; eliminate the need to be right; every trade is unique; follow the process without hesitation (take every valid setup, manage risk identically). There is almost no method content. He sums up his strategy in one line as "liquidity sweep, break of structure, fair value gap, break of structure, and then target other draws on liquidity". He mentions a recent −$135,000 loss on his last trade and says he has had no red month in the current year.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Edge = probability** — a strategy gives "a higher probability of being right than being wrong ... and when we win, we are making more than when we lose" [05:00–05:35].
- **His strategy, one-line form** — "liquidity sweep, break of structure, fair value gap, break of structure, and then boom, target other draws on liquidity" (used as an example of a rule set that does not guarantee 100%) [03:19].
- **Five fundamental truths** (Douglas): (1) anything can happen; (2) every moment in the market is unique; (3) a strategy indicates a higher probability of one outcome over another; (4) wins and losses are randomly distributed for any strategy; (5) you don't need to know what will happen next to make money [33:29–35:08].
- **Risk on funded accounts** — the money really at risk is the evaluation fee, not the simulated balance [08:59–09:32].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Take every valid setup; skipping valid setups after a loss forfeits the strategy's edge [16:14–17:20; 32:22].
- Manage risk identically on every trade [32:22].
- Accept the maximum loss mentally and financially *before* entering; treat the risked money as already gone [07:19–08:27; 12:52].
- Don't move stops, revenge trade or hold losers to "be right" [29:05–30:12].
- Don't buy funded-account evaluations you can't afford to lose; don't buy resets on tilt [09:32–12:20].
- Backtest and journal (TradeZella) to know your win rate and RR, so you have confidence to take every trade [20:08–20:42].
- Example math: 70% win rate at 1:1.5 RR, executed "word for word, bar for bar", is net positive over time [22:55–23:27].
- Don't blame "bad price action": a red month means you should review what you did [27:57–28:33].
