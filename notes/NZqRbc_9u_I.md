## Summary
Beginner roadmap (Feb 2025, recorded on MLK day): get a flexible job (he did DoorDash/Uber Eats) to build capital, trade around the 9:30 ET NY open (or London session if a 9-5 blocks NY), learn from free resources (TradingView free plan, his YouTube playlists and free course), practice on a TradingView paper account until three consecutive green months, then leverage capital via prop-firm challenges (FTMO example) before a live account. Almost no method content; a few statements on charting philosophy (price action only, no indicators, fractal price) and an illustrative risk example (1%/~1.25% risk, 100-tick stop/target) that he labels "a horrible idea" as a trade.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Price-action trader** — "I'm not a pattern trader ... I trade based off of price action"; no indicators, no volume, "literally just reading the Japanese candlesticks". [07:06]
- **Fractal price** — "price moves the same way on every single time frame"; liquidity-seeking happens on the 1H and the 1m alike. [07:06–07:41]
- **Tools** — he mainly uses the TradingView horizontal ray and rectangle tools to mark confluences; no indicators, alerts or multiple screens needed. [05:28; 08:14]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Session: he trades the NYSE open at 9:30 a.m. ET; as a student with classes he traded London session instead. [01:38–02:44]
- Go-live gate: stay on demo until **three consecutive green months** (e.g., +10%, +5%, +7%); any losing month resets the count. [16:35–18:51]
- Don't attempt prop challenges unless already profitable on demo. [25:29–26:02]
- Expect to be unprofitable at first on live due to new emotions; accept sacrificing some money to get past the psychological barrier. [19:24–20:29]

## Process / order of operations
1. Get a job/side hustle that leaves the NY open free (or trade London). [01:38–03:47]
2. Learn with free resources (TradingView free, his YouTube: Boot Camp, Trading Transformation, concepts, trade recaps; free course). [04:20–11:02]
3. Demo trade on TradingView paper trading. [11:35–13:13]
4. Three straight green months → prop-firm challenge or live account. [18:17–18:51]
5. Reinvest payouts into a live account. [28:19–28:52]

## Confluences mentioned
- Fair value gap — used only as a demo-order illustration ("this is a horrible idea" as an actual trade). [14:20]

## Trade examples
- None real. Demo order ticket demonstration on S&P (market closed): buy, risk ~$1,000–1,250 on a $100k demo (1–1.25%), 100-tick TP and 100-tick SL (1:1). Explicitly not a recommended trade. [14:20–14:57]

## Risk & psychology rules
- Profitable traders "are not going to be risking 5% in a day". [23:16–23:48]
- FTMO example rules: max daily loss 5%, max total loss 10%, phase-1 target 10%, phase-2 5%. [23:16–24:23]
- Treat trading like a video game; demo makes money "just numbers on a screen" to remove emotion. [15:29–16:02]
- Don't blow payouts on watches/cars; reinvest into a live account. [28:19]
- Prove profitability to a family member, not just yourself, to check ego. [16:35–17:11]

## Changes vs. earlier teaching / contradictions
- Feb 2025 (TT era); no model content. Confirms NY-open focus (9:30 ET). The 1:1 100-tick example is an order-ticket demo, not a stop rule — do not use for stop size. Risk statement "not risking 5% in a day" is consistent with the 1%–3%/day framework (§6). No news, SMT, TP-TF, OB, contracts, or daily trade limits.
