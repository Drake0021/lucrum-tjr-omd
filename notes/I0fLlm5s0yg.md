- **Set lot size** — his usual stop is ~500 "pips" at his set lot size, which equals 1% risk [12:49; 15:07].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Bias: with mixed news (50/50) he leaned short "just because of the way that price has been rallying and taking out all these areas" (the overhead 15m OB and highs) [05:49].
- He was glad structure didn't break before the Chicago PMI release (~9:45 ET) [04:40–05:16].
- Entry: wait for a candle *close* below a marked level (red line) → sell on the close [08:44–09:49]. He noted beforehand that "this isn't even that high confluence of an area" [08:44].

- Stop: very tight, well under half of his normal ~500-pip set-lot stop [12:49; 15:07].
- After a loss, don't look for another trade just to recover [13:28–14:01].

## Risk & psychology rules
- 1% risk ≈ a 500-pip stop at the set lot size; a 1% loss is "usually like 2.5 or ... two bands" (~$2–2.5K) [12:49; 15:07].
- "Be okay with taking a loss ... losing is a part of the game." You will never have a 100% win-rate year or quarter [13:28–14:36].
- Edge = strategy + risk management + psychology [14:36].
- Think in weeks and months, not days. A beginner's goal is ≥10%/month (≈120%/yr) [15:43–16:50].
- Losses = business expenses / paying for a lesson [17:22–17:55; 20:07–20:41].
- If you cannot accept ending a day negative, trading isn't for you [19:34].
- No revenge or replacement trade after the stop-out [13:28–14:01].
