## Summary
Intro / "contract" video for the free YouTube Boot Camp, filmed on arrival in Puerto Rico. TJR explicitly says no trading content is taught here; lesson one is "take action". He asks viewers to watch every daily upload in order, do all homework, journal, and treat it seriously. He sets expectations (profitability/wealth takes ~1-2 years, not days), tells his origin story (signal groups, bought courses, scammed, rock bottom), and says his edge came from screen time on clean charts. The only method-relevant content is his early "London fake-out" (liquidity sweep) trade on GJ.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Market experience / screen time** — the thing that cannot be taught or sped up; he says his biggest growth came from removing every indicator from his charts and just watching the market ("putting time between myself and the market"). [12:16]
- **"London fake-out" (= liquidity sweep)** — his own early name for a liquidity sweep: at the London session open, GBPJPY (GJ) would "fake out" by taking out a high and then reverse in the opposite direction. He says he called sweeps before he knew the term, and did not learn them from ICT. [37:05]
