## Summary
Jan 2023 (pre-Boot Camp) "simplified" daily bias lesson on the S&P (SPX/ES, chart in London time): the day's bias is simply the **1-hour market structure** (last 1H BOS direction) read on the candle before the 9:30 ET open; the 15m is secondary and ignored if it disagrees; the 4H is ignored because trades last ≤ ~30–60 minutes. Execution: mark 15m (and 5m) highs/lows in the opposite direction as liquidity, wait after the open for that liquidity to be swept ("fake-out"), then enter on the **1-minute BOS candle close** in the bias direction, stop beyond the sweep, TPs at prior structure "to the left". Four replay days shown, all winners. He does not trade CPI/PPI/NFP/FOMC/Powell days, where the 1H bias fails and 4H structure gets played off instead.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Daily bias** — "literally just reading the one hour time frame and seeing what direction it's going": bullish if the 1H last broke structure up / is in an uptrend, bearish if down. Read at the 1H candle prior to market open. [01:13] [04:01] [12:00]
- **1H vs 15m conflict** — "who cares what the 15 minute is doing because the one hour holds more power ... higher time frame". [09:35] [11:15]
- **Why not 4H** — "we're not taking four hour long trades ... our trades are in and out within 30 minutes", so the 1H is all you need. [11:15]
- **Liquidity sweep vs BOS** — a candle that closes below a low is a BOS, not a liquidity sweep. [04:36]
- **Fake-out** — move against bias at the open that takes liquidity before the real move. [02:19] [05:43]
- **Swing to break** — after the sweep, track the newest high; if a new high forms, "just use the new one that gets made" even if it's higher. [07:53] [08:27]
- Market open marked at 14:30 London time (= 9:30 ET); 1H candle "that starts Market open" is the 14:00 London candle (inferred: 9:00 ET 1H bar). [10:08]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Bias = 1H structure direction before the open. Only trade in that direction. [01:13] [07:20]
- Mark liquidity opposite to bias: bearish → 15m (and 5m) highs / relative equal highs (buy-side); bullish → 15m lows (sell-side). [01:46] [05:08] [06:48]
- Wait for that liquidity to be swept after the open; ignore sweeps/moves in the bias direction before that ("we could give a crap"). [02:19] [07:20]
- Entry: 1-minute BOS (candle close beyond the most recent swing low for shorts / high for longs) after the sweep; enter on that candle's close. [02:19] [03:26] [08:27]
- Stop: beyond the liquidity sweep extreme. [08:27]
- TPs: previous structure to the left — shorts at lows, longs at highs (incl. end of an FVG); multiple TPs (TP1–TP4). "Take profits are the least of your worries." [03:26] [08:27] [11:57]
- News: no trading on CPI, PPI, NFP, FOMC, or Powell-speech days; on those days the 1H bias "gets violated" and the 4H structure is played off instead. [13:03] [13:37]

## Process / order of operations
1. Before the open: read 1H structure → bias. [01:13]
2. (Glance at 15m; ignore if conflicting.) [11:15]
3. Mark 15m/5m liquidity on the opposite side. [01:46]
4. From 9:30 ET: wait for the sweep of that liquidity. [02:19]
5. 1m: wait for BOS in bias direction; enter on close. [03:26]
6. Stop beyond sweep; TPs at prior structure. [08:27]

## Changes vs. earlier teaching / contradictions
- Timeline: Jan 2023, pre-Boot Camp 1. **Contradicts BC1 §3 (daily bias = daily structure)**: here daily bias = **1H** structure; 4H explicitly ignored. Later Trading Transformation (2024–25) uses 4H trend + 1H; this is an earlier, simpler 1H-only version.
- Entry = sweep + 1m BOS close (BC1 model 1, later retired for the third confirmation); no FVG/OB/EQ retrace required.
- Liquidity = 15m/5m highs/lows — relevant to **OPEN §12 item 5** (which liquidity counts for the ES sweep): 15m highs/lows primary, 5m secondary, relative equal highs.
- **News-day behavior:** does NOT trade CPI, PPI, NFP, FOMC, Powell speeches. [13:03]
- Entry time: sweeps typically within ~30 min of 9:30 ET. Trade duration ≤30 min. No stop size, contracts, daily limits, SMT.
