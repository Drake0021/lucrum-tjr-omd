## Summary
First daily-bias lesson. TJR's daily bias comes from top-down analysis: weekly trend (just eyeball/line chart, low importance for a day trader), then the daily market structure (most recent daily break of structure = the bias), then the 4H and 1H for confirmation and for take-profit tentativeness. The core idea: "imagine you're trying to take a trade on the daily/4H/1H" — use the building blocks (liquidity, FVG, OB, equilibrium) on HTF to find where the daily-bias trade would set up, then drop to 15m/5m for execution (sweep, BOS, OB/FVG) during the New York session. Trading against the daily bias gives short-lived or losing trades. Includes live ES/S&P analysis of that day (bearish daily, bullish 4H) and a gold reference.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Daily bias** — where price wants to go for the day; used to choose trade direction so a short-term (several-hour) execution captures the day's move. [00:02], [07:55]
- **Weekly bias** — "literally just look at it" / line chart: recent price action going up = bullish. Useful context and for long-term targets but TJR (day trader) doesn't care much; swing traders should. [00:35], [03:26], [11:32]
- **Daily bias from daily market structure** — "I like to use the daily current market structure": the latest daily break of structure sets bias. Example: daily BOS to the downside → bearish ("prior to this candle ... I would have been bullish, not anymore"). Uptrend or downtrend on daily = the answer. [01:43], [02:17], [11:32]
- **4H trend as secondary filter** — he also checks 4H trend. Ideal: daily and 4H agree (e.g., 4H closes below a low / breaks structure the same way as daily) → go to 1H, find high-confluence areas, execute. [02:52]
- **Daily BOS + 4H counter-structure = daily retracement** — if daily broke structure up and the 4H breaks down, that's a retracement on the daily — "prime time getting ready for a setup"; look for the retrace into HTF building blocks (equilibrium, FVG, OB). [08:59]
- **"Imagine a trade on the HTF"** — to find daily bias, imagine taking a swing trade on the daily/4H/1H in the daily-trend direction: where would it set up (FVG, daily/4H OB, 1H BOS)? Once price is within those ranges, scale down to execute. [12:06], [12:41], [14:14], [17:04]
- **New York session drives the daily candle (S&P)** — majority of the daily move on the S&P happens in the NY session (NYSE open); everything else is chop. So your trade likely captures the majority of the daily candle if bias is right. [05:08], [07:55], [08:27]
- **Weekly retracement context** — if the weekly is in an uptrend and the daily is in a downtrend, the weekly is retracing; look for weekly equilibrium, FVG, liquidity, OB as larger targets. [18:12]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Only take trades in the direction of the daily bias; opposite-direction trades are short-lived or losers. [00:02]
- IF daily bearish (BOS down) THEN look only for sells / bearish confirmation on 1H and lower; IF daily bullish, look only for bullish confirmation. [02:17], [08:27], [12:06]
- IF daily and 4H conflict (daily bearish, 4H bullish) THEN either avoid trading the S&P or require clear bearish confirmation, AND be tentative with take-profits — cannot target far HTF levels until the 4H has also broken structure in your direction. [04:01], [04:34]
- Don't trade the S&P on a London-session setup ("who's trading the S&P during London session") — wait for NY. [14:48]
- When an LTF (15m) sweep/BOS appears against the daily bias, ignore it; wait for price to reach the HTF building-block levels and then give confirmation in bias direction. [17:04]
- Entry on the 5m: once price is in the HTF zone (FVG/OB/equilibrium), wait for a 5-minute break of structure — "not waiting for just a down candle". [16:30]
- Targets for an HTF-aligned trade: 4H retracement levels (4H imbalance, 4H equilibrium of the swing, the leg that caused the move/4H OB), daily draws on liquidity; can take HTF take-profits off a 5m execution. [15:22], [17:36]
- When you're stopped out because the market broke structure the other way, that's acceptable — you followed plan; bias was simply wrong. [05:42]
- Didn't trade that day because Powell was speaking. [04:01]

## Process / order of operations
1. Weekly: eyeball trend/line chart (context only). [00:35]
2. Daily: identify current market structure (last BOS) → bias. [01:43], [11:32]
3. 4H: check trend; agreement = ideal; disagreement = daily retracement or tentative targets. [02:52], [04:34]
4. Identify HTF draws on liquidity and building blocks (daily/4H FVG, OB, equilibrium) where a bias-direction trade would set up. [02:52], [12:41]
5. 1H: look for confirmation (BOS) in bias direction. [09:32], [13:42]
6. Scale to 15m/5m: liquidity sweep, BOS, OB/FVG, equilibrium; enter on 5m BOS confirmation. [10:45], [13:42], [16:30]
7. Trade during NY session (NYSE open) for S&P. [08:27]
8. Target 4H/daily levels, tentatively if 4H not yet aligned. [15:22]
