## Summary
Recap of an ES long on a high-impact news day (GDP + non-farm employment data, NFP week). Bias came from bullish news, hourly BOS up in pre-market, and 5m "chop then leg up" accumulation areas. Entry was a 1m BOS after price drew into the area. TP1 was 1:1 (50% off, stop to BE), TP2 was hourly liquidity areas, and the runner stopped at BE. The lesson is varying position size with conditions. He took half size on a high-impact news day, plans 75% size for a lesser news day, may size up on clean no-news days, and uses 0.5% instead of 1% when the technicals are iffy. He recommends traders formalize this as rules.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Area of accumulation (5m)** — price "chopped up within here and then we got a leg on up". The chop zone before the leg is the area he marks and waits for price to draw back into. [01:43]–[02:16]
- **Hourly areas of liquidity as targets** — the price range swept by the prior big drop (from the swept high up through the range) is where short orders were still able to be filled. He targets the start of that range, not the extreme high above it. [02:16]–[02:50]
- **Conditional risk / probability-based sizing** — lot size is based on "confidence and probability within the market". News lowers probability. [03:24], [06:10]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Bias: bullish news (GDP, non-farm employment) + hourly BOS up during pre-market (broke highs) = bullish day. [01:43]
- Setup: on the 5m, mark the chop/accumulation before a leg up. Wait for price to draw into it. Enter on a 1m BOS up. Stop under the low. [01:43]–[02:16]
- TP1 = simple 1:1 RR. Close 50% of the open position there and move the stop to BE. [02:16]
- TP2 = hourly liquidity areas that caused the big drop (the bottom of the range where short orders were filled). Do not put TP2 at the far high above, because liquidity from that range has already been swept. [02:16]–[02:50]
- News-day sizing: high-impact news day → about 50% of normal size, even with a perfect setup. [03:24], [04:29]
- Lesser news (e.g., unemployment claims / PCE an hour before the open, so the move is likely made before the open) → about 75% of normal size. [04:29]–[05:05]
- No news + good setup + everything aligned with bias → normal size or "a little bit more heavy". [05:38], [06:43]
- High-impact news → "maybe I don't even trade that day". If he trades, it is at 50% risk. [06:43]
- No news but iffy technicals → 0.5% instead of 1%. [07:52]
- On news days, borderline setups are "even more of a reason" to skip. [07:16]
- He usually does not trade until the market opens (9:30 ET open inferred). [06:43]
- Suggested method: compute the contract size for normal 1% risk with the actual stop (e.g., a 7-point stop), then cut the contracts by the reduction factor (e.g., half). [06:10]–[06:43]

## Process / order of operations
1. Check the news calendar and the direction of the news (bullish/bearish for USD/SPX).
2. Hourly: confirm bias via BOS in pre-market.
3. 5m: mark the accumulation/chop before legs in the bias direction.
4. Wait for price to draw in. Enter on a 1m BOS. Stop under the low.
5. Size according to conditions (news, confidence).
6. TP1 1:1 → 50% off, BE. TP2 = HTF liquidity range. Runner to BE.

## Risk & psychology rules
- Trading full size on a high-impact news day during NFP week is "a big mistake". [04:29]
- Make the risk plan more structured than his own eyeballed lot sizes, so you follow your rules and keep emotions out. [06:10]
- Sizing tiers he mentions: 50% (high-impact news), 75% (minor/pre-open news), 100%+ (no news + A setup), 0.5% vs 1% (iffy technicals). [04:29]–[07:52]
