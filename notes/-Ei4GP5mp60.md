## Summary
TJR explains why stop losses matter (prevent big losses; force risk management; mark the price where the trade idea is invalidated) and gives his three stop placement levels, illustrated with drawings (no live chart). Context: liquidity sweep → LTF BOS → third confluence (e.g., FVG/OB) → 1m BOS entry. Stop options: (1) beyond the liquidity sweep — the default/best; (2) beyond the swing high/low made inside the third confluence (tighter, used when RR to TP1 is poor with option 1); (3) beyond the confluence itself (gives breathing room when RR allows). Choice depends on RR to first TP. ~8 minutes of intro banter (Kanye quote).

## Concepts & definitions
- **Stop loss purpose** — Avoid big losses; forces risk management; "marking a specific price where our trade idea is invalidated." [04:14][05:33][06:50]
- **Stop level 1: beyond the liquidity sweep** — If price goes beyond the sweep extreme, "it's not a liquidity sweep," so the idea is fully invalid. "First area that I'm always going to be looking for." [09:39][10:12][10:47]
- **Stop level 2: beyond the high/low made within the third confluence** — e.g., a 5m high formed inside the FVG before the LTF BOS; riskier because price could take it and still go your way (bias still correct). [11:21][11:56]
- **Stop level 3: beyond the confluence itself** — e.g., above the whole FVG; gives breathing room for price to push deeper into the confluence; if price fully passes the confluence, the idea is "bunk." [13:38][14:11][14:45]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Default stop beyond the liquidity sweep. [10:12]
- If stop beyond the sweep gives bad RR to first TP, consider stop beyond the high/low inside the confluence (e.g., to achieve ~1:1) — or sit the trade out. [10:47][11:56][12:29]
- If RR is very good, you can widen to beyond the full confluence for breathing room. [13:38][14:11]
- Mirror for longs: below the sweep lows (best), below the higher low within the FVG, or below the FVG. [15:17][15:52]
- Ranking stated at end: best = beyond sweep; second = beyond the confluence; third = the higher low within the confluence. (Note: at [14:11] he lists them in order sweep → high within confluence → beyond confluence; the final ranking puts "beyond confluence" second.) [14:11][16:25]
- Risk management lesson deferred to later. [04:58]
