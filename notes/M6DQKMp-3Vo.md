## Summary
This is the strategy video that underpins Boot Camp 2.0: the "accumulation / change of trend / prominent highs-lows" model (early Sep 2023, recorded on the Sunday before Labor Day). It is likely the dedicated strategy video the framework flags as missing (§2.14 / OPEN §12.2). The building blocks are unchanged (sweep, OB, FVG, EQ). What is new: (1) find the trend and bias by eye from **prominent highs and lows** (rounded-out swings where price consolidated), helped by a **line chart**, on the daily/weekly; (2) a **change of trend** = breaking (close beyond) a prominent high/low, so price leaves the trend rather than making just any BOS; (3) **areas of accumulation** = consolidation just before a big leg, boxed as the zone price will revisit. Process: daily trend → mark 4H/1H accumulation areas in the trend direction → when price returns, wait for a lower-TF change of trend (5m, after the HTF zone hits) → enter on building blocks (EQ/FVG/OB reaction, or a 1m BOS) → stop under the structure → TPs at accumulation areas, liquidity and highs. Examples are on ES (15m/5m), gold, GBPUSD and GBPJPY.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Prominent high/low** — chosen "kind of based on the eye test"; "opinion based". It is a **rounded-out** swing where price "spent some time in consolidation rounding out — filling orders to go lower/higher", as opposed to the small internal candle highs/lows inside a leg. Example: "the high prior to this big extension down" is prominent; the smaller highs within the leg are not. [04:37]–[07:51]
- **Line chart helper** — "just toss it on a line chart" to see prominent highs/lows and the trend; he prefers marking on candles "to keep it more exact". **Caveat:** the line chart can show a "break" that on candles is "just a wick", so confirm with a candle **close** above the prominent high. [08:24]–[09:32]; [35:18]–[35:50]
- **Change of trend** — the market goes from lower highs/lower lows to making a high above a prominent high and a low above the prior low: "this right here is our change in trend, because we started making higher highs and higher lows; we broke out of this prominent high". [08:24]–[08:58]
- **Change of trend vs BOS** — "instead of break of structure I'm really looking for change of trend". A 5m BOS against you that does not break the overall trend (no prominent level broken) is ignored: "it broke 5-minute structure to the downside... did it change the trend though?" He doesn't know ICT's CHoCH vs BOS distinction. [41:57]–[43:03]
- **Area of accumulation** — "really just areas of consolidation prior to a big leg up" (or down); "this is where orders were filled... how can we see that? with that big leg up afterwards." The same zone can be called "consolidation, accumulation, order block — whatever." Box it and assume price revisits it. It occurs on every timeframe and nests (a 5m accumulation inside a 1H/4H one). [13:26]–[13:58]; [22:19]; [24:01]; [27:50]
- **Accumulation boundaries** — box the consolidation/chop range before the breakout leg; for a wick-driven sweep, "we have to encapsulate this price action" (include the sweep wick), and scale down to find where it actually accumulated. A whole choppy range can be one area if "this entire price range caused this leg down". [30:03]; [36:25]–[36:58]
- **Order block (restated)** — "this leg down caused this leg up": the opposite leg that caused the breakout leg is "just our order blocks... our building blocks". [38:02]; [40:18]–[40:51]
- **HTF magnets** — "finding your magnets": where orders were accumulated in the past = where price is drawn. He wants to "move away from using the smart money stuff on high time frame stuff" (marking every sweep/OB/FVG/CHoCH across TFs) and use trend + accumulation zones for bias. SMC building blocks remain for execution. [47:31]–[50:21]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- **Bias (weekly direction):** on the daily (line chart or candles), mark prominent highs/lows. A push past a prominent high with a higher low = bullish for the week, "push higher and higher until we see a prominent low get pushed past on the daily". Check the weekly sentiment agrees. [09:32]–[11:11]
- **Zones:** on the 4H and 1H, mark areas of accumulation **in line with the daily trend** ("our 15m, 5m, 1m — that's execution points"). Don't take counter-trend zones: "not necessarily wanting to go long on this, why? because... it's going down." [34:07]–[34:40]
- **Best condition:** "when we see price legging up on the lower time frames and our daily line chart is saying down — that's glorious" (retracement into an HTF accumulation against the LTF trend). [29:29]
- **No blind entry at the zone:** "we came into this area, we don't necessarily want to act yet... it would be stupid for us to just blindly press sell"; wait for confluence. [30:37]
- **Execution sequence:** price enters the HTF accumulation → scale down (15m → 5m; "I prefer executing on the 5 rather than the 15") → mark prominent highs/lows of the LTF counter-trend → wait for a change of trend (a close beyond a prominent LTF high/low) → enter on building blocks: [38:02]–[38:36]
  - a 5m reaction off the OB/EQ ("wait for one or two things, just that 5-minute reaction off of it") [18:28]; or
  - a 1m BOS after the EQ/OB tap ("just a simple 1-minute break of structure... you either get filled on this candle") [19:33]; or
  - an FVG fill → 1m prominent-high close above → enter at the next FVG [25:39]–[26:12]; or
  - EQ: "price push into equilibrium, price break out of equilibrium, close above it" → enter [40:51]–[41:22]; or
  - an FVG hit followed by expansion → enter [41:57].
- If the first building block doesn't hold (price runs through the FVG and sweeps liquidity): "who gives a [ __ ], we're still waiting"; a liquidity sweep is itself a building block. Re-mark the trend and wait for the next change of trend. [16:45]–[17:50]
- **Stops:** below the structure: "stop all the way down here [below the sweep low] or stop underneath here [below the 5m structure low], depending on your risk tolerance"; "stops can literally be right under here" (under the breakout FVG/low); shorts: "stops above here". [20:07]; [26:12]; [32:56]; [41:22]
- **Targets:** TP1 = nearest highs ("target these highs is your first take profit"), then areas of accumulation that caused the opposite move, and liquidity ("areas of liquidity"); scale out across TP1–TP3 (example TP3 ≈ 1:5.9). [18:28]–[19:01]; [26:45]–[27:18]; [41:22]
- **Session:** "ideally you were taking these trades in confluence with session opens when there's volume and volatility... London, Asian or New York session open... within those two hours." Avoid entries at the end of the US session. [46:23]; [23:27]
- **Holidays/weekends:** "don't trade tomorrow, it's Labor Day." [27:18]; [44:10]
- **Patience / instruments:** "you won't see a trade every single day with this... that's why I look at one, two, three, four pairs." [39:10]

## Process / order of operations
1. Daily (line chart or candles): mark prominent highs/lows → trend → weekly direction; check the weekly agrees. [04:02]–[11:11]
2. 4H/1H: box areas of accumulation (consolidation before big legs) in the daily-trend direction; also FVGs/EQ/liquidity as magnets. [12:19]; [27:50]–[28:23]; [34:07]
3. Wait for price to retrace into the HTF accumulation (the LTF trend runs against the daily). [29:29]–[30:37]
4. LTF (15m → 5m → 1m): mark the LTF prominent highs/lows; wait for a change of trend (a close beyond the prominent level) after the zone hits. [32:20]; [38:36]–[39:44]
5. Mark the LTF accumulation/OB/FVG/EQ that caused the change of trend; enter on a reaction, a 1m BOS or an EQ close-back. [19:33]; [40:18]–[41:22]
6. Stop under the swept low / structure low; TP1 at the nearest highs; further TPs at HTF accumulation/liquidity. [20:07]; [41:22]
7. Trade only around session opens (first ~2 hours); skip holidays. [46:23]

## Changes vs. earlier teaching / contradictions
- **Timeline:** Sep 2023, the bridge from Boot Camp 1 to Boot Camp 2.0. It introduces the BC2 core (change of trend, prominent highs/lows, area of accumulation) that the framework attributes to BC2.
- **Answers OPEN §12.1 (prominent swing):** no numeric rule. Prominent = a "rounded-out" swing with consolidation (where orders filled), typically the high/low before a big extension, *not* small internal candle swings. Use the line chart to see them and confirm the break with a candle close.
- **Answers OPEN §12.2 (accumulation boundaries), partially:** the box covers the consolidation/chop range right before the breakout leg, including a sweep wick if that is where orders filled. The opposite leg that caused the breakout = OB = accumulation (interchangeable names).
- **BOS vs change of trend:** a minor-swing BOS that doesn't break a prominent level is ignored, which confirms the framework's §11.19 resolution (a close beyond a **prominent** swing).
- **EQ entry:** the close back beyond EQ is required ("break out of equilibrium, close above it"), which matches §11.2.
- **Session window:** trade the first ~2 hours of a session open. Slightly wider than "first 90 min" (OPEN §12.7).
- **Holidays:** no trading on Labor Day (bank holiday), consistent with §1.
- **HTF use of SMC:** he moves away from marking every HTF sweep/OB/FVG for bias and uses trend + accumulation instead. Bias ≠ news here.
- **Instrument:** "shifted the majority of my attention over to forex", while still trading S&P (Sep 2023). By Nov 2023 – Jan 2024 he says he trades only S&P and NASDAQ (7zAy8xbzoNo, YGSXw_B-UgM).
- Order blocks are still used (equated with accumulation). No SMT, no IFVG (only a vague "breaking fair value gap" mention).
