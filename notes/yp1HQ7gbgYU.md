## Summary
TJR restates his full systematic futures model as six steps and expands step 3: besides a HTF liquidity sweep, price reaching a HTF confluence (FVG, order block, breaker block, equilibrium) also qualifies as the trigger to scale down. Which HTF's confluences to use depends on 4H/1H alignment: if the 4H and 1H trends disagree, use 4H confluences and execute 15m BOS → 15m third confluence → 5m BOS or 15m candle; if they agree, use 1H confluences and execute 5m BOS → 5m third confluence → 1m BOS or 5m candle. Goal: more setups beyond just pre-market/open liquidity sweeps. ~8 minutes of intro is off-topic (hiatus explanation, music). No live chart examples.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Six-step model** [08:34][09:06][09:40]:
  1. 4H bias/trend → bias for the day.
  2. 1H trend → determines which lower TF to scale into (15m vs 5m).
  3. HTF confluence gets hit (originally: HTF liquidity sweep on 4H or 1H; now also HTF FVG/OB/breaker/EQ).
  4. Scale into LTF; wait for LTF break of structure.
  5. Wait for LTF third confluence (FVG, order block, breaker block, equilibrium).
  6. Wait for either a smaller-TF BOS or an up/down candle on the current LTF out of the third confluence → execute.
- **1H's role** — "helps us determine whether we're scaling into the 5 minute or the 15 minute." [10:13]
- **HTF confluences** — liquidity sweeps, order blocks, FVGs, breaker blocks, equilibrium on the 4H/1H. [13:03][14:46]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- 4H uptrend + 1H downtrend (opposed) → bias bullish (follow 4H); trigger = 4H or 1H liquidity sweep OR price into 4H OB/breaker/FVG/EQ; then 15m BOS → 15m third confluence → 5m BOS or 15m up-candle → long. [10:48][11:23][11:56][12:30][13:03]
- 4H uptrend + 1H uptrend (aligned) → trigger = 1H FVG/OB/breaker (or HTF sweep); then 5m BOS → 5m third confluence → 1m BOS or 5m up-candle → long. [13:38][14:11]
- 4H downtrend + 1H uptrend → shorts; 4H FVG etc. hit → 15m BOS + 15m third confluence → lower. [15:20][15:55]
- 4H downtrend + 1H downtrend → 1H FVG/OB/breaker or sweeps → 5m BOS down → third confluence → 1m BOS down or 5m down-candle → short. [15:55][16:30]
- HTF liquidity sweeps remain "extremely valid"; this is additive. [13:03]

- vs kb/framework_v1.md §1/§3: BC uses the daily as the intraday bias, with 4H/1H not contradicting it.
