## Summary
A psychology and mindset day. TJR gives his one-page "trading psychology book": learn a strategy, never change it, good things take time, manage risk, don't overtrade, don't over-leverage, have one mentor, don't overthink, execute. He mocks the habit of mixing tools from several sources (e.g. other people's order-block marking, support/resistance, Bollinger bands). He also mocks over-analysis across timeframes. He repeats his two-candle definition of a high and a low. He says the reason to trade should be enjoying being right about where price goes, not money.

## Concepts & definitions
- **High** — "a two Candlestick pattern a move up then a move down a green candle then a red candle." [14:26–15:00]
- **Low** — "a two Candlestick pattern a move down then a move up a red candle then a green candle." [15:00]
- A candle is only a high if a down candle follows it. He cites a Mastermind question where a candle without a following down candle was wrongly taken as the high (chart example, inferred). [16:06–16:41]
- **TJR's psychology "book"** — (1) Learn a strategy and don't ever change it. (2) Good things take time. (3) Manage risk ("live to trade another day"). (4) Don't overtrade. (5) Don't over-leverage. (6) Don't have multiple mentors. (7) Don't overthink. (8) Execute. [05:42–07:26; 11:27; 17:15–17:50]
- **His method as a "step-by-step playbook"** — "this happens then this happens then this happens and you press buy or sell", then hands off until SL or TP is hit. [10:52–11:27]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- After entry, do nothing until the stop loss or take profit is hit ("take your hands you shove them into your ass"). [10:52–11:27]
- Don't add tools from other sources into his strategy (other OB marking, support/resistance, Bollinger bands). He does not use S/R. [09:10–10:17]
- Don't overtrade; don't over-leverage. [06:51–07:26; 17:50–18:24]
