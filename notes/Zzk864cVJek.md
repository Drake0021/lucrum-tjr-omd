## Summary
Lesson on break of structure (BOS), which TJR classifies as a "confirmation confluence": it confirms that orders were filled at a liquidity sweep. In an uptrend you monitor only the most recent higher low; BOS to the downside requires a candle BODY close below that most recent low (a wick or a body exactly equal does not count). Mirror for downtrends (close above most recent high). Lower highs alone do not break bullish structure. He shows how BOS on the 5-min after a HTF liquidity sweep confirms the reversal and states his three-part strategy.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Market structure** — uptrend = higher highs and higher lows (bullish market structure); downtrend = lower highs and lower lows (bearish). [00:32] [01:06]
- **Break of structure (BOS) / market structure shift** — when the current structure breaks: in an uptrend, price breaks below the most recent higher low (making a lower low, then lower high); in a downtrend, breaks above the most recent lower high. [01:42] [02:18]
- **Candle-close requirement** — BOS needs the candle BODY to close beyond the extreme of the most recent swing point. A body equal to the low does not count; a wick through does not count, however far it goes. [04:29] [06:07] [08:20]
- **Most recent swing** — only the most recent low (in uptrend) / most recent high (in downtrend) is monitored; a swing low is confirmed by "a move down then a move up". [02:50] [08:55]
- **Lower high does not break bullish structure** — in an uptrend only the lows are monitored; a lower high, or even a deep wick to a lower low, leaves you in an uptrend until a body closes below the most recent low. [07:14] [07:46] [08:55]
- **Confirmation confluence** — BOS "confirms that orders have been filled" (above/below liquidity). [11:40]
- **Strategy** — "We look for potential for orders to be filled, confirmation that orders are filled, and then a continuation of the current trend that is now being created." [11:40]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Do not act on a wick through a swing; wait for a full candle close beyond the most recent swing high/low. [06:07]
- Use BOS together with liquidity sweeps: after HTF draw on liquidity is swept, drop to the LTF (5-minute) and wait for a BOS against the prior move as confirmation. [09:28] [10:01]
- BOS alone isn't sufficient — "other confluences come into play". [10:34]

## Process / order of operations
1. Identify HTF draws on liquidity; watch for price to sweep them. [10:01]
2. Drop to the 5-minute; identify the current LTF structure and its most recent swing low/high. [10:01]
3. Confirmation = candle close through that most recent swing (BOS). [10:34]
4. Then look for continuation (FVG next lesson). [11:06]
