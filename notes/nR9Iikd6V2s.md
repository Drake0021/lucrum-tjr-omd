## Summary
Defines break of structure (BOS) using the Day 6 swing definitions. In an uptrend, watch the most recent swing low; a candle **body close** below it is a bearish BOS and the trend is now down. In a downtrend, a close above the most recent swing high is a bullish BOS and the trend is now up. Wicks don't count, and a close exactly equal to the level is not a break. Examples on an unnamed chart and on NASDAQ. He stresses that BOS alone will never make you profitable; it is just a tool for later.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Swing high/low recap** — Up→down pair = high (highest point of the two). Down→up pair = low (lowest of the two). Two consecutive same-colour candles define nothing. If an earlier candle's wick was lower but it isn't in a down→up pair, ignore it. [02:13–03:20]
- **Break of structure (BOS)** — Uptrend: "if we see a low get closed underneath that's a break of structure." Downtrend: "when we see a high closed above break of structure." [04:30–05:03]
- **Close means body close, not wick** — "if we see a candle close underneath that not a wick ... if the close ... closes underneath the lowest point then it's a break of structure." Bullish: "this line right here on the Candlestick has to be above this high." [05:38–06:47]
- **Which swing** — Only the **most recent** low (in an uptrend) or most recent high (in a downtrend). [05:03; 09:43; 10:51]
- **Equal close ≠ break** — "this candle is equal okay so we didn't necessarily get a break with this candle but then boom this candlestick closed above this high". A close equal to the high is not a BOS; the next candle closing above was. [08:34]
- **BOS = trend change** — After a bearish BOS out of an uptrend "we're now in a downtrend", and vice versa. [10:51–11:24]
- **Sideways inside a trend** — A lower high can appear inside an uptrend ("sometimes the market moves sideways"). As long as the most recent low is not closed below, the uptrend stands. [07:25–08:00]
- **BOS is detectable before the new LL/LH forms** — The close below the low is the signal; the lower low/lower high follow. [04:30]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Bearish BOS: in an uptrend, close(candle) < most recent swing low price (strictly less). [05:03–06:12; 08:34]
- Bullish BOS: in a downtrend, close(candle) > most recent swing high price (strictly greater; equal does not count). [06:47; 08:34]
- Wick-only penetration is not a BOS. [05:38]
- After a BOS, flip the trend state. [11:24]
- BOS alone is not a trade signal ("you guys cannot ... turn profitable just by identifying break of structure"). [09:09]

## Process / order of operations
1. Identify the trend (HH/HL or LH/LL).
2. Uptrend → track the most recent swing low; downtrend → track the most recent swing high.
3. Wait for a candle body close beyond it → BOS → new trend direction. [09:43–11:58]
