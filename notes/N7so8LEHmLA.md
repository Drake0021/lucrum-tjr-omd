## Summary
Psychology day on goal setting, using a method TJR learned from a basketball program. Write three long-term (~10-year) end goals, then 10 short-term goals/steps for each (30 total), complete the steps daily, and read and say the goals out loud (at least 5x daily) in "I am"/"I will" form. He suggests trading-related short-term goals such as following your trading plan, using risk management, and journaling/reviewing trades every day. The notebook is also to become the trading journal used later in the boot camp. No chart content.

## Concepts & definitions
- **"X factor" / practice player** — analogy for traders who win on demo but cannot execute live; psychology is the missing piece. [01:41]
- **Long-term end goals** — three goals ~10 years out that you don't know how to reach (e.g., seven-figure account, six figures/month profit, a $10M house). [02:47]-[04:57]
- **Short-term goals/steps** — 10 per long-term goal; daily actions or near-term milestones (e.g., profitable on the week, profitable on the month) that lay out the path. [05:29]-[06:36], [09:41]-[10:14]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Suggested trading short-term goals (stated as examples): follow your trading plan every day; use risk management every day; journal your trades and review what you did right/wrong every day. [06:36]-[07:10]
- Getting funded comes from completing daily steps, not from going "full port" (all-in sizing) without a plan. [08:16]-[08:50]

## Process / order of operations
1. Get a notebook (this becomes your trading journal). [02:47], [23:00]-[23:32]
2. Write 3 long-term end goals (at least one trading-related). [03:19], [06:36]-[07:10]
3. Write 10 short-term goals/steps for each (30 total). [06:03]
4. Complete/take steps toward them every day. [09:41]
5. Read and vocalize all goals out loud at least 5 times daily, phrased "I am ..." or "I will ...", as if already achieved. [15:43]-[17:24]
6. (His practice) Rewrite all goals on a new page every morning. [19:37]-[20:10]

## Risk & psychology rules
- Daily affirmations (e.g., "I am a disciplined trader", "I am patient within the markets") make it harder to over-leverage or over-trade at the open. [18:33]-[20:44]
- Being embarrassed to say your goals out loud means you don't want it enough. [22:25]
