## Summary
A talk (after a joke intro, 00:00–03:00) about not over-complicating trading: follow the trading plan, use one strategy built on the four building blocks (liquidity sweep, break of structure, order block, fair value gap), form your own daily bias rather than borrowing others', and accept that the missing piece is market experience/time (minimum ~1 year for most). Learn about the strategy and market rather than adding components. Journal decisions, including no-trade decisions, and review them at day end.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Trading plan purpose** — defines how many times, when, why, and how you trade, so the only way to lose is the market going against you. [03:36]
- **Four building blocks** — liquidity sweep, break of structure, order block, fair value gap; be an expert at each, and at finding daily bias with that one strategy. [17:24], [18:32]
- **Daily bias vs execution** — "understanding where the market wants to go on the day is light work"; the hard part is finding the right execution areas with good R:R. [05:48], [06:20]
- **Late-session OB reactions** — an order block may not react later into the session due to low volume/volatility; a lesson learned from market experience ("don't take trades way later into market open"). [06:20], [06:52]
- **His original Forex strategy** — "30 minute boxes in session, session split up" (mentioned, not explained). [16:17]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Follow the trading plan step by step; don't stray because "it's not working" early. [03:36], [04:10]
- Use ONE strategy and ONE bias — your own. Don't take daily bias from YouTubers, signal groups, mentors or him once you can derive it. [09:08], [12:29]
- Don't add components to the strategy; learn more about it instead. Each addition is another skill to master. [16:49], [17:24]
- Avoid trades late into the session (low volume/volatility) — stated as an example of an experience-based lesson. [06:52]
- Example no-trade reason to journal: daily bullish, 4H bullish, but 1H bearish → he couldn't take a trade. [14:09]
- Also: "against his daily bias but the hourly is countering it" → no trade. [13:35]

## Process / order of operations
1. Find daily bias yourself with the one strategy. [09:08]
2. Look for execution with good R:R at building blocks. [06:20]
3. Write down trade AND no-trade decisions with reasons. [14:09]
4. End of day: check where the market went; was I right/wrong; what didn't I account for. [14:09], [14:41]
5. Study the journal daily so recurring setups trigger memory of past losses. [15:13]
6. Chart every building block on historical price and note how price reacted. [20:10]
