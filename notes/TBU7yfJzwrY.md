## Summary
The first ~5 minutes are banter (Rolls-Royce configurator, TikTok sounds, a meme-coin joke; he mentions hitting a 1:5 RR on USDJPY). TJR then restates the basic strategy: 4H trend = bias; 1H picks 5m or 15m; a 1H/4H liquidity sweep in line with the bias; an exec-TF BOS; a third-confluence entry with a reaction candle. He adds one refinement: **once price is inside the exec-TF third confluence, drop one timeframe** (5m → 1m, 15m → 5m) and enter on a BOS there. That way you don't wait for the full exec-TF candle to close. SL/TP is promised for the next video.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Restated steps** — (1) 4H trend = bias. (2) 1H trend determines the exec TF: in line with 4H → 5m, not in line → 15m. (3) A HTF liquidity sweep of either 1H or 4H highs/lows in line with the bias (bullish → lows swept; bearish → highs swept). Mark them all and do nothing until one is hit. (4) Scale to the exec TF → BOS. (5) Third-confluence entry; the reaction is "just like a candle up or a candle down." [05:14–07:47]
- **LTF refinement entry** — "When price enters into this fair value gap ... we can scale down into the one minute time frame and just wait for a one minute break of structure, enter off that instead of having to wait for a full five minute candle to close." Rationale: a 1m BOS inside the 5m confluence "is more than likely going to cause a 5 minute bullish candle." [09:32–10:08]
- **15m path** — With 4H bearish and 1H bullish: sweep of a 1H high → 15m BOS down → price enters a 15m OB → scale to the 5m and wait for a 5m BOS down "without having to wait for a full bearish 15 minute candle." [10:08–10:42]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Entry options at the third confluence: (a) the exec-TF reaction candle closes out of the zone (base), or (b) **better**: a BOS one TF lower (5m exec → 1m BOS; 15m exec → 5m BOS) while price is in the zone. [08:20; 09:32–10:42]
- SL/TP not given (next video). [07:47; 11:16]

## Process / order of operations
1. 4H trend → bias.
2. 1H trend → exec TF (5m/15m).
3. Mark 1H/4H highs/lows in the bias direction; wait for a sweep.
4. Exec-TF BOS.
5. Price enters an exec-TF OB/FVG/breaker/EQ.
6. Drop one TF → BOS in the bias direction → enter (or a reaction-candle close on the exec TF).
