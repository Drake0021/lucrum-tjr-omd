## Concepts & definitions
- **News direction by pair construction** — For pairs where USD is the quote currency (XAUUSD, GBPUSD and, as he frames it, S&P), USD-bearish news is bullish for the pair. For GBP-base pairs (GBPUSD, GBPJPY), GBP-bearish news (bearish GBP CPI) is bearish for the pair. "Anything against the dollar, it's going to be opposite." His wording is muddled; the practical point is to read news direction relative to base and quote. [01:09]–[02:15]
- **HTF building block** — He wants price to reach a higher-timeframe building block such as an order block before taking a trade. A "small fair value gap play" is not enough. [03:56]
- **Draw on liquidity** — On S&P, a monthly high. [02:49]
- **Sweep-and-rally** — The 1H swept liquidity below and then rallied, which made the 1H "real bullish". [03:22]
- **30-minute high (forex strategy)** — In his forex strategy price should come up into the 30-minute high or order block before the short. Without that, no trade. [14:45]

1. Close out or review the prior trade. Clear all chart drawings. [00:00]
