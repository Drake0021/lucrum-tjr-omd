## Summary
A Sunday weekly outlook (mid-December 2023, holiday markets) for ES, NQ and Bitcoin. ES and NQ are in monthly and weekly uptrends (monthly BOS up → retrace into an accumulation area → weekly BOS → extension), with all-time highs as the HTF draw. The lower timeframes are consolidating, so he will "play what the market gives us": either a drop into the daily imbalance or a run to the interim daily high. Most of the video is Bitcoin/altcoin investing: wait for a monthly BOS up, then dollar-cost-average during weekly chop/consolidation, and never close crypto investments for a loss.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Monthly → weekly sequence** — "we broke monthly structure to the upside... a little retrace into this little accumulation area and then we reacted off of it with a weekly break of structure; now... we're extending on the monthly." Same model fractally on HTF. [03:45]–[04:19]
- **Long-term vs interim draws** — long-term draw = all-time highs; "super interim, shorter-term draw on liquidity" = a daily high. Downside daily-retrace draws = daily imbalances. [04:19]–[07:09]
- **Marginal BOS** — NQ "we closed above this, but just by like an inch piece, so we're technically bullish on the 4-hour time frame." A close by any margin counts. [08:53]
- **Consolidation → pop ("chop, chop, pop")** — after consolidation price usually expands in the trend direction; in a downtrend the pop is down. [15:18]–[15:51]
- **Crypto investing method** — wait for a monthly BOS up (e.g., Bitcoin, with an FVG retest), then scale in/DCA during weekly chop; take EQ of the chop range as the average entry (illustrative). [11:49]–[12:21]; [17:33]–[18:41]
