## Summary
A pure beginner lesson on Japanese candlestick anatomy (open, close, high, low; body vs. wick) and on what a timeframe means. It is demonstrated on a live Bitcoin chart (1H, 5m, 15m). Highs/lows and trends were planned but pushed to the next video. There is no strategy content. He uses blue (up) and black (down) candles.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Chart types** — Japanese candlestick (the standard one he uses), line and Heikin-Ashi exist. [03:21]
- **Body** — The solid part. It shows the open and the close. Up candle: open at the bottom of the body, close at the top. Down candle: the reverse. [06:12–08:31; 10:15]
- **Wick** — Shows the highest and lowest prices reached during the candle's period, "like a snail trail": price was there but did not close there. [08:31–10:15]
- **Within a candle you can't know whether the high or low came first** — "who knows which came first the high or the low." [09:41]
- **Timeframe** — The period each candle represents, from its open until it closes (1m, 5m, 15m, 1H...). Lower timeframes show the same price action in smaller chunks. A run of seven 1H down candles appears as up/down swings on the 5m. [13:42–15:24; 16:37–17:09]
- **Fractal nesting** — Monthly → weekly → daily → 4H → 1H → 15m → 5m → 1m: each contains the smaller chunks. [19:33–20:04]
