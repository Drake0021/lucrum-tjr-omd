## Summary
This launches Boot Camp 2.0, which combines trade recaps with tips and psychology, aimed at traders who finished 1.0. TJR recaps two trades. The first is an ES short with daily, 4H and 1H all bearish. He entered with 50% size on a 1-minute BOS after price tagged a 15m/1H order block and FVG, without waiting for the third confluence. It lost about $13k. The second is a GJ long that met every step of his forex strategy: a sweep of the London lows, a 5m BOS up and an order block retest, with Wyckoff-style spring and breakout context. It hit TP1, and the day was about -$1,954 realised with about $16k floating. He then teaches which losses are acceptable: following every rule with aligned biases and still losing is fine. He also covers holding trades without emotional interference and his old habit of deleting MT4 and TradingView after placing a trade. He says strategy matters less than psychology.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Order block (15m / 1H)** — "Leg up before the extension down." This is the last up-move before the bearish displacement, and price pushing back into it is the short location. [04:52]–[05:26]
- **Three confluences for entry** — The order block or POI tag, then a FVG or OB, then a rejection or confirmation off it. Entering on the BOS alone, without the FVG/OB and rejection, is not waiting for the "third confluence". He frames this as what he should have waited for. The exact list of the three is somewhat vague. [07:09], [07:44], [14:37]
- **Partial-size entry when confluence is missing** — He entered with only 50% of his position because he did not wait for a FVG or OB and a rejection. [06:35]–[07:09]
- **"Market experience" trade** — A discretionary trade anticipating price from experience rather than a full checklist. Example: after a big London sell-off, expect relief at the session switch. He says these sometimes fail. [07:09], [09:53]
- **Hourly equilibrium / discount** — His ES short entry was past hourly equilibrium in a discount. He considered the location good, but it is notable for a short (inferred: he likely meant premium for a short, or was referring to a different leg). [15:10]
- **Wyckoff accumulation (spring)** — Consolidation, then a liquidity sweep (spring) below the range, then price springs back into the range, breaks out, retests the old ceiling as a floor, and continues. He calls this "extra confluence or extra confidence" to stay in a trade. It is not ICT. [20:43]–[22:21]
- **Avoid entries inside consolidation** — After a huge London move, price in New York ranging in "support and resistance territory" is not an area to enter. Wait for the sweep and displacement out. [20:09]–[20:43]
- **Equilibrium target of a daily order block** — The 50% of a "massive daily order block" (the expansion up before the BOS down) is used as a target for the runner. [12:55]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Bias: IF daily, 4H and 1H are all bearish before the market open, THEN bias is bearish for the day; look for shorts at the 15m/1H order block or FVG. [04:52]–[05:26]
- Entry: wait for all three confluences (POI tap, FVG or order block, rejection off it) before entering. Entering on a bare 1m BOS is a mistake. [07:09], [07:44]
- If you enter early without full confluence, use 50% size. Described as what he did, not a recommended rule. [06:35]
- Forex strategy long (GJ): price sweeps the London session lows, then a 5m BOS up, then a pullback into the order block (the "back end of the London lows"), then closes higher. Enter with the stop beneath the swept London lows. [08:16]–[09:21]
- Stop placement in the forex strategy: he advises 4 pips beneath the London lows. [19:35]
- Filter: avoid pound pairs after a big high-impact-news sell-off in London. He breaks this rule here. [04:15], [08:50]
- Filter: GJ was not in line with 4H or 1H bias, only with taking daily liquidity. That made him "iffy". [09:21]
- Filter: do not enter inside a consolidation or range after a large session move. Wait for the sweep and displacement (a "big candle down" and BOS back up). [20:09]–[20:43]
- Management: hold until TP or SL. Do not close because price is near TP; he did not close 0.2 pips from TP2. [26:20]–[26:56]
- Management: at TP1, partial (per the prior pattern; he hit TP1 and kept the remainder running toward TP2 and daily OB equilibrium). [11:46], [12:55]
- News: high-impact PMI. He expected a fake news move up to sweep the hourly highs; it did not happen. [04:15], [05:26]–[06:35]

## Process / order of operations
1. Check high-impact news (Flash Manufacturing/Services PMI). [04:15]
2. Pre-market: establish daily, 4H and 1H bias (all bearish on ES). [04:52]
3. Mark the 15m/1H order block and FVG for the expected retracement. [04:52]–[05:26]
4. At the open, price pushes into the OB/FVG. Drop to 1m and look for a BOS. [05:26]
5. Ideally wait for the full three-confluence confirmation; a sweep of the hourly highs would have been even better. [06:35]–[07:09]
6. Targets: hourly low, then 4H low, 4H order block, then daily lows (swing). [06:35], [07:44]

## Risk & psychology rules
- Acceptable losses: you followed the daily bias, your trading plan and the right time, fulfilled every confluence, and still lost. "Those should be the only trades that you lose." [15:45]–[16:52]
- Unacceptable losses: you skipped a confluence, or took mixed-bias trades (for example the daily and 1H disagree while the 4H and 15m agree). Those come from greed and emotion. [16:52]–[17:24]
- Review wins too, not just losses. [19:03]
- If you cannot hold a trade to at least TP1 or SL, you are either over-risking or not built for trading. [22:54]
- Do not re-chart, add indicators or "backtest" the trade you are currently in. Know your risk and leave it. [27:29]–[28:33]
- His old fix for emotional trading: set a 1:1 RR with SL and TP, delete MetaTrader and TradingView, and review the outcome before the next day's open. [29:42]–[30:15]
- Speak or write out why you lost or won; for example, record a mock trade recap. [32:58]
- Strategy matters less than psychology. Profitable traders exist with SMC, support and resistance, and Bollinger Bands. [24:03]–[24:38]
- He did not close a trade 0.2 pips from TP2. Let it hit or not. [26:20]
