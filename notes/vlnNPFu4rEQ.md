## Summary
A talking-head psychology and discipline video, with no chart content. TJR gives six rules he wishes he had as an unprofitable trader: (1) know when to turn off the charts, (2) journal, (3) stop jumping between strategies, (4) patience and discipline, (5) have a trading plan (strategy + risk plan), (6) risk management is everything. The only near-operational content is the stop-after-a-win/loss guidance and the "not trading is better than trading" rule.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Strategy as a safety net** — "think of our strategies and our trading game plan as a safety net that protects us from bad price action." If the setup appears, take it; if not, don't [01:36–02:09].
- **Three pillars of profitability** — strategy (which "just gives you the probability"), risk management and psychology [04:19–04:52].
- **Trading plan** — made of a strategy (enter only when it presents itself) and a risk management plan (how much risk you'll put on **for the day**) [09:49–10:55].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- IF you take a **win** for the day (especially early in your trading career) → turn off the charts and study the win [00:00–01:04].
- IF you take a **loss** → do not chase it with more trades [01:04–01:36].
- No setup → no trade: "not trading is better than trading" [01:36].
- Only enter when the strategy presents itself [10:22; 13:10].
- Define in advance the amount of risk you're willing to put on **for the day** [10:22]. No numbers given.
- Never risk your whole account on one trade; even a 97% win rate gets wiped out eventually [10:55–11:30].
- Win size should be ≥ the previous loss to keep equity growing [11:30–12:03] (vague).

## Risk & psychology rules
- Stop trading for the day after a win (beginners) and don't chase after a loss [00:32–01:36].
- Journal every trade and build a large data set (a month or more) to find which setups win and lose. Sponsor mention: Tradezella [02:09–03:47].
- Don't switch strategies after a loss; losses are part of any strategy (the Stephen Curry analogy) [03:47–06:29].
- He used to raise risk/leverage and "full port" after a winning week, then lose; don't do that [04:52–05:24].
- His notebook mantra: "I will see good setups. I will take good trades. I will be patient!!!" [06:29–07:02].
- If you need trading money for rent or food, trading isn't for you; get a stable job first [08:11].
- He spent 2 years as an unprofitable trader [14:18].
