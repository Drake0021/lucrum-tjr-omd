## Summary
TJR restates the systematic model built so far (4H bias; 1H alignment selects the execution TF — 5m if aligned, 15m if not; wait for 1H/4H liquidity sweep; LTF BOS + third confluence; then drop one more TF for a BOS entry) and adds a continuation rule: if the HTF liquidity sweep and hourly BOS already happened before the open (pre-market/London), don't wait for another sweep — treat hourly confluences (OB, breaker, FVG, equilibrium) formed by that move as the "HTF sweep" equivalent and trade the continuation. Liquidity sweep remains "our best and most awesome confluence." Examples are hypothetical/illustrative (one during Asian session).

## Concepts & definitions
- **Model as of pt.4** [00:36][01:11][01:52][02:27][03:01][03:34]:
  - 4H trend → daily bias.
  - 1H trend: in line with 4H → execute on 5m; not in line → 15m.
  - Wait for HTF (1H or 4H) liquidity sweep; "we're not doing anything until that liquidity gets hit."
  - Scale into LTF (5m/15m): BOS then third confluence (e.g., OB/FVG/EQ).
  - Once price pushes into the third confluence: from 5m go to 1m, from 15m go to 5m, find a BOS and enter.
- **Continuation addition** — If an hourly sweep + hourly BOS already occurred (pre-market/London), the hourly OB/breaker/FVG/EQ created by that move are used "as our high time frame sweeps". [04:42][05:15][05:47]
- **Original strategy intent** — "trying to find the start of a move"; the addition trades "the continuation of that hourly trend." [05:47]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- If 4H and 1H bearish and hourly sweep + BOS already occurred pre-open, wait for price into an hourly FVG/OB/breaker → 5m BOS → 5m third confluence (e.g., 5m FVG) → 1m BOS → short; stop above the highs; target HTF draws on liquidity / HTF confluences. [08:05][08:38]
- 4H bullish, 1H swept lows + BOS up (not aligned overall) → hourly OB/FVG retrace → LTF BOS + FVG → 1m BOS → long; target HTF draws. [09:11][09:46][10:57][11:31]
- 4H & 1H bullish, price dips into hourly OB at open → wait for 5m BOS up (a retrace into the OB likely broke 5m structure down first) → 5m third confluence (FVG/OB/EQ) → either 5m up-move/candle or 1m BOS → long. Stop under the FVG or under the lows "to play it safe"; target previous hourly/4H highs or hourly/4H confluences. [12:37][13:10]
- Always prefer liquidity sweeps when available; use this only when price is "already so far gone" that a sweep is unlikely. [10:21]

## Process / order of operations
1. 4H bias. 2. 1H trend → choose 5m or 15m. 3. HTF sweep (or, if already happened, hourly confluence hit). 4. LTF BOS. 5. LTF third confluence. 6. Next lower TF BOS → entry. [01:11]–[03:34]
