## Summary
Short lesson (Feb 2023) on a weekly-range Forex swing strategy — about one setup per week per pair, forex/gold only ("does not apply to the S&P 500"). Mark the previous week's high and low as liquidity; when price sweeps or tests one, drop to the 1H and enter on the 1H BOS close; stop beyond the sweep (or the weekly level); single target at the previous daily high (longs) or previous daily low (shorts). If price closes well past the weekly level on 4H/daily without reacting, it is not valid liquidity — move to the next weekly range. Bar-replay examples on AUDUSD, EURUSD, gold, and a live GJ short (1:8, 30-pip stop).

## Concepts & definitions
- **Weekly range as liquidity** — previous week's high and low marked at the start of the week (Monday); "anything above here is fair game" for a sweep or "just a test into" the level. Same strategy as his S&P one "just on a higher time frame". [01:07] [01:41] [02:13]
- **Not valid liquidity** — price pushes "all the way past" the weekly level, closing beyond it on 4H and daily without reaction → invalid; look to the next weekly range. [07:24] [11:19]
- **Swing high** — "move up move down that creates a high". [04:34]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Mark previous week H/L before Monday. [01:07]
- WHEN price sweeps/tests a weekly H/L THEN scale to the **1H** and wait for a BOS (candle close beyond the recent swing) in the opposite direction; enter on the close of that candle. [03:21] [03:56] [11:19]
- Stop: above/below the sweep extreme, or at the weekly range level; if entry is very close to the weekly level, use the sweep extreme ("worst case scenario"). [02:47] [05:07]
- Target: shorts → previous daily candle's low (prior to the sweep); longs → previous daily high. "Only need one stop loss one take profit". [02:47] [11:19]
- Instruments: GJ, major USD pairs, gold. NOT the S&P 500. [10:45]
- No reaction / no setup → no trade. [11:19]
- Retest entry after BOS also acceptable ("enter on the BOS, enter whenever you want"). [08:31]

## Process / order of operations
1. Weekly: mark previous week high and low. [01:07]
2. Wait for one to be swept/tested (which gets hit first decides direction). [04:34]
3. 1H: wait for BOS against the sweep. [03:21]
4. Enter on BOS candle close; stop beyond sweep / weekly level. [03:56]
5. TP at previous daily H/L. [05:40]

## Confluences mentioned
- Weekly high/low (liquidity) — required. [01:41]
- 1H BOS — required. [11:19]
- 4H/daily close beyond the level — invalidator. [07:24]

## Trade examples
- AUDUSD (bar replay) | weekly high taken | 1H BOS down → short | stop above high or weekly level | TP previous daily low → hit. [01:41]–[03:21]
- (Pair unclear, Zoom backtest) | weekly level pierced, 1H BOS down → short at close | TP previous daily low → hit. [03:21]–[03:56]
- EURUSD | weekly low hit first, 1H BOS up → long | stop under sweep (not weekly level, too close) | TP previous daily high → hit. [04:34]–[05:40]
- Gold | previous week low, 1H BOS up → long | TP previous daily high → hit. [06:14]
- GJ (live, Discord, Feb 2023) | first weekly high blown through on 4H/daily close → invalid; next weekly range hit, 1H BOS down → short via a retracement entry (missed initial BOS) | 30-pip stop, ~1:8 | TP1 taken at previous NY session low; running to previous daily low. [06:49]–[09:38]

## Risk & psychology rules
- Expect ~1 setup per week per pair; don't force. [00:00] [10:13]
