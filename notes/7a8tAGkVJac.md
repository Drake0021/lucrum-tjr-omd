## Concepts & definitions
- **Priced-in news via prediction markets** — Polymarket shows what "the masses" expect. If ~98% expect the outcome, it is already priced in, so the release causes no significant move. A surprise vs the consensus causes a large move (the Meta earnings miss → flash crash) [05:34–08:48; 13:48–14:21].
- **Run-up into expected bullish news** — Monday/Tuesday "people were buying into the news" (the expected cut + earnings beats) [07:42–08:48].
- **News stack** — FOMC decision 2:00 PM ET; Powell speech 2:30 PM ET; Google/Meta/Microsoft earnings after the close. Mega-caps drive the S&P/NQ ("80% of S&P stocks red, the 20% green made the S&P up 1%") [08:48–11:01].
- **Post-decision consolidation = area of accumulation** — "we were pretty much creating an area of accumulation before Federal Chairman Powell got on the stage". He boxes it on both ES and NQ [14:54–15:28].
- **Two-path scenario plan** — breakout up to sweep the highs then lower, or breakout down to sweep the hourly lows then higher [15:28–16:02].
- **Fake-out vs significant sweep** — the first push up "didn't take out any of these significant draws on liquidity ... It was just like a fake out". The real sweep was of the lows: "that is what I base my trade off of because I needed a high time frame sweep of liquidity for me to take a trade" [16:02–16:34].
- **SMT as exit and re-entry signal** — a 5m bearish SMT (NQ vs ES equal highs) + an ES IFVG → exit. A 5m bullish SMT (ES swept the 5m lows, NQ did not) + BOS up → re-enter [18:46–21:40].
- **FOMC data highs** — the high of the decision candle/move is a key draw [23:18].
