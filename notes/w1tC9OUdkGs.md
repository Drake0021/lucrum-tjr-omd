## Summary
Dec 2022 vlog + lesson (first ~5 min is travel/life update). Week of Dec 12–16 2022 had CPI (Tue), FOMC (Wed), retail sales/Empire State/unemployment claims (Thu). He states his news rule — never trade CPI, FOMC or NFP; trade other red-folder days if the release doesn't move the market much — and walks through ForexFactory (folder → source, "usual effect", actual vs forecast vs previous) to read each release's implication for USD and, inversely, the S&P 500 (DXY up = S&P down). Uses the Dec 13 CPI miss (0.1% vs 0.3% forecast) and FOMC rate decision as examples. No execution model.

## Concepts & definitions
- **News source** — ForexFactory.com; S&P traders only need USD events. [05:31] [06:05]
- **USD vs S&P inverse** — good for the dollar = bad for S&P; "whenever you see red it's the inverse for the S&P"; when DXY drops, stocks, metals, crypto go up. DXY monthly uptrend since May 2021 explains the 2022 S&P downtrend. [07:50] [08:23] [18:34]
- **Usual effect** — e.g., CPI: actual > forecast good for currency (→ S&P down). Retail sales: actual > forecast good for currency. Unemployment claims: actual < forecast good for currency (higher claims → S&P up). Fed funds rate: actual > forecast good for currency. FOMC: "more hawkish than expected is good for currency". [07:12] [13:28] [15:11] [18:34] [19:42]
- **CPI m/m vs y/y, core CPI** — m/m compares to last month; y/y the year. Dec 13 2022: CPI m/m 0.1% actual vs 0.3% forecast (0.4% prev); y/y 7.1% vs 7.3% fcst (7.7% prev) → S&P rallied on a big hourly candle. [10:03] [11:47]
- **FOMC components** — economic projections (4×/yr, no data printed), statement (8×/yr), federal funds rate (4.50% vs 4.00% prev, in line with forecast → S&P fell since higher than previous), press conference (Powell). [13:28] [14:04] [14:37] [15:44]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Never trade CPI, FOMC, or NFP days. [02:44]
- Other red-folder (high-impact) news: if it "doesn't affect the market too much", trade that day (he traded Thursday after claims/retail sales/PMI). [02:12] [02:44]
- He skipped Tue (CPI) and Wed (FOMC) of that week. [02:44]
- Wake before the news release to see the reaction before trading. [02:12]
- Entering at the news candle = "pretty much a gamble"; you'd get filled halfway through. [11:12]
- Doesn't like FOMC partly because no data is visible to read. [13:28]

## Process / order of operations
1. Check ForexFactory for USD red folders. [06:05]
2. For each: open folder → source, measure, usual effect. [06:37]
3. Compare actual vs forecast (and previous) → USD good/bad → S&P inverse. [10:03]
4. Skip CPI/FOMC/NFP days; on other red-folder days, see the reaction then trade. [02:44]
