## Summary
Continuation of the hedged "full port" challenge experiment on the **PPI** release (the day after CPI), on two phase-2 $200k accounts (long on one, short on the other, GBPUSD, 35 pips ≈ 5%). The short reached +$10k, but he closed it right at the target. Spread and commission left it about $60 short, so he re-entered repeatedly, traded emotionally (added longs against the trend, "doubled down"), went to −$5k, and finally scraped the target. He presents the episode as a lesson in emotional trading. He then explains the economics (account refund on the first payout; make 1%).

## Concepts & definitions
- **Spread/commission at targets** — closing exactly at the dashboard profit number fails once spread and commission are deducted; "wait until it's... a couple hundred more in your favor." [17:29]–[18:03]; [26:10]–[26:44]
- **Emotional trading in action** — revenge re-entries, counter-trend adds, hoping for an RSI divergence or a 5m FVG to bail him out: "I'm trading like you guys right now." [19:46]; [24:22]–[25:32]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
