## Summary
Part 2 fills in the Pt.1 outline (session, trader type, timeframes, confluences, stop/TP) with options and TJR's own choices. TJR: NYSE open; a "longer-term scalper / intraday" trader holding about 30 min–2 h, runners longer. Top-down = weekly → daily → 4H → 1H (building blocks), execution on 15m/5m/1m. Confluences = sweep + BOS + FVG/OB (three for confidence). TPs go at price-action levels where price could reverse, even below 1:1. The key extra tip is to **"treat finding your daily bias like a trade"**: the HTF chain (4H sweep → BOS → FVG → 1H BOS) itself *is* the bias. He illustrates it with that day's ES/NQ short, which was stopped out ("I lost 20K today").

## Concepts & definitions
- **Confluence** — like confidence: each aligned signal raises confidence; "usually I like to wait for three." [11:09]–[12:17]
- **His simplest execution** — "liquidity sweep plus break of structure plus fair value gap / order block — that's the simplest execution points for me." [12:17]
- **Daily bias as an HTF trade** — "when you're finding your daily bias, treat it like a trade": run the same confluence chain on the HTF (4H sweep of liquidity → BOS → FVG → scale down to 1H BOS = the HTF "entry"). That gives the direction and the zone the market is reacting off; then scale down and find the real trade with the same confluences. [12:17]–[15:08]
- **Trader-type holding times** — scalper: in and out within 5–30 min, never over an hour; intraday: closes within a day; swing: days to months. TJR: "usually within a trade... right around an hour, 30 minutes, an hour, sometimes even two hours if I have runners, and sometimes even a whole day" with extended TPs. [04:27]–[05:34]
- **Timeframe sets** — scalper: 4H context → 1H + 15m bias → 5m/1m execution. Intraday (TJR): weekly → daily → 4H (what are we reacting off) → 1H (which building blocks) → execute 15m/5m/1m. Swing: monthly/weekly/daily/4H, executing on 1H. [08:25]–[10:37]
- **Execution-TF variability** — "sometimes I'm taking an execution point based off the 5-minute... I have a wide stop loss... long-term play, or I'm taking a 1-minute trade and I'm out within a couple minutes." [10:03]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Session: NYSE open (10:30 his local time). Keep trading and the day job separate. [02:14]–[02:47]
- Bias chain example (that day): daily bullish; 4H had been bearish (now about to close above); price came into a 4H FVG → 4H sweep of liquidity, BOS, FVG → scale to 1H BOS = entry for the HTF bias (short). Then execute on the LTF with the same confluences. [12:53]–[14:34]
- Stops/TPs: either fixed (points) or price-action based. TJR takes profits "where there's the potential for price to reverse"; "sometimes I don't even have a one-to-one risk reward ratio"; after TP, the stop goes to breakeven. [18:32]–[20:16]
- TP levels from his confluences: "liquidity sweeps, fair value gaps, order blocks, equilibrium." [20:16]–[20:50]

## Process / order of operations
1. Weekly bias → daily trend → 4H (what are we reacting off) → 1H building blocks. [09:29]–[10:03]
2. Treat the HTF read as a trade: sweep → BOS → FVG → lower-HTF BOS = bias + zone. [13:29]–[15:08]
3. Scale down to 15m/5m/1m and take the real trade with sweep + BOS + FVG/OB. [15:08]
4. Stop by price action; TPs at reversal levels; BE after TP1 (inferred from [20:16]).
