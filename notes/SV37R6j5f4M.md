## Concepts & definitions
- **5m order block from a sweep + BOS** — after the sweep of lows and a 5m BOS, the OB created on the 5m (gray box) is the entry zone. Entry is a dip into it plus a 1m BOS and a 5m reaction. [01:07]–[01:39]
- **Third confirmation / third confluence** — after the area hit and the BOS, wait for a comeback / retest of equilibrium, an FVG, or an OB with a reaction. Skipping it is the "mistake". [05:36]
- **Leg validity** — a leg up with too little movement is no longer treated as a valid leg. He then expects price to draw lower into the other accumulation area. [07:49]–[08:22]

## Process / order of operations
1. Hourly context (breakout of trend / liquidity sweep).
2. 15m/5m: mark the accumulation areas (leg down prior to leg up).
3. Price enters the area → 5m BOS in the bias direction.
4. Wait for the third confirmation: a retrace into EQ / FVG / OB and a reaction, then a 1m BOS.
5. Enter. Stop under the lows. TP1 50%, then the rest to further targets.
