## Summary
A motivational, talking-head video with no charts. TJR's lessons: (1) no get-rich-quick, it took him 2 years to become profitable; (2) you know what to do but don't act, so backtest and collect data; (3) success lies in discipline, which sometimes means not trading; (4) "you suck, your strategy doesn't"; (5) focus on the process (predicting price), not money; (6) act like the trader you aspire to be; (7) nothing changes if nothing changes. The trading-relevant content: his confluence list (liquidity sweeps, BOS, IFVGs, FVGs, equilibrium), the "best traders take 1–2 / 5–6 trades a month" remark, and his old morning routine (weekly → daily → 4H).

## Concepts & definitions
- **Three skills of profitability** — strategy, risk management, psychology/discipline. Strategy only gives an edge/probability [02:46; 11:34–12:07].
- **His strategy's confluences (Nov 2025)** — "liquidity sweeps, break of structure, inverse fair value gaps, fair value gaps, equilibrium". He says he dislikes indicators [11:34]. Order blocks are not listed.
- **Goal of trading** — "to have a high probability of predicting where price wants to go"; money is a side effect [14:48–15:20; 17:33].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Only trade when every confluence in the plan has appeared; if the strategy never presents itself, the correct action is to not trade [07:43–08:16].
- Don't skip or force confluences; "the second that we abandon our edge, then we're just flipping a coin" [14:48].
- Size so you can survive three losing days in a row or a losing month; never risk the whole account on one trade [15:20–15:55; 18:39].
- Seek >1:1 risk-to-reward [14:48].
