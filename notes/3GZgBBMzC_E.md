## Summary
Day 1 of TJR's January 2026 "Path to Profitability" YouTube series (one video per day through January, possibly into February). He explicitly says it is NOT a remake of the 2023 Boot Camp: it will contain updated confluence, psychology, strategy and risk-management videos plus confluences/advanced material never covered on YouTube before. The bulk of the video is a motivational rant about taking responsibility and taking action. The last third is a goal-setting method: specific, measurable yearly goal -> double it -> break into monthly and weekly targets -> daily plan of attack. No chart/strategy content.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Path to Profitability series** — a daily January 2026 series of "updated confluence videos, updated psychology videos, and updated strategy video[s]... updated risk management videos", plus "confluences that I haven't even covered on YouTube before" and "more advanced stuff". [01:41]–[02:52]
- **Specific goal** — a goal must be a trackable number with a roadmap; "lose weight / put on muscle" is too vague. Example: "$100,000 by end of 2026". [23:50]–[25:31]
- **Goal decomposition** — yearly goal / 12 = monthly; monthly / 4 = weekly ("$8,500/month", "$2,250/week" — his arithmetic is approximate: 100k/12 ≈ 8.3k). Do NOT break it down to a daily dollar target ("today I need to be making $300... that f*** you up"); instead write a daily plan of attack toward the weekly target. [26:04]–[27:11]
- **2x rule for goals** — after writing achievable goals, double them (for "more is better" goals); aim at "unrealistic" goals so you wake up with urgency. [29:22]–[31:00]
