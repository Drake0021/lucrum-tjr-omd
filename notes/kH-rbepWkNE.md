## Concepts & definitions
- **Manipulation** — a big move at or before the open that sweeps liquidity. "Typically, I want to see some form of 5-minute manipulation for me to want to take a trade or any sort of manipulation" [27:45]. Both a big leg up and then a sweep of lows can be manipulation, so he must decide which one is real [28:18–28:51].
- **Respect current price action** — when two scenarios exist, favor the current structure: "we are bullish on the 5-minute and we're also bullish on the hourly ... I kind of have to favor that bullish bias" [28:51].
- **Disrespecting order flow / IFVG** — a bearish FVG that gets closed through (inverted) = "disrespecting bearish order flow to the upside", confirmation price will target the opposite liquidity [29:26]. "If we start disrespecting 1-minute bearish gaps ... that typically for me would cause me to take a long" [05:31–06:03].
- **5m retrace inside bullish structure** — a sweep of lows after a 5m bullish BOS that keeps 5m structure bullish is "just a 5-minute retrace" [04:56; 29:26].
- **Doji not a high** — ES's BOS didn't count because the swing was a doji: "We didn't really have like a real high. This is kind of a doji candle ... I couldn't really classify it as a full bullish break of structure" [31:39].
- **HTF SMT** — NQ took 4H highs, ES did not = "potential bearish high time frame SMT" [01:44]. A 5m bearish SMT during the trade was a worry [10:40; 13:00–13:33].
