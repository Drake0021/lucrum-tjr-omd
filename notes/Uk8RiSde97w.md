## Summary
Daily bias for Wed Jan 3, 2024, recorded about 2.5–3 hours before the open. He checks ForexFactory first: JOLTS, ISM Manufacturing PMI, an FOMC member speaking and the FOMC meeting minutes, so **no trading today**. He still does the bias so the levels can be reviewed in the evening recap. ES and NQ are bearish on the 4H (NQ also closed below daily structure → weekly retrace). He marks the HTF draws below and the "confluences" above where he would want a market-open sweep of the hourly high, then a reaction off a 15m imbalance or 15m/hourly order block. He states the rule that he only trades setups marked in the daily bias and does not adapt after the open.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Weekly retrace from a daily BOS** — NQ "got that daily closure to the downside, which kind of confirms okay we're going to get a weekly retrace." Retrace targets: the previous weekly high, the 50% mark of the weekly FVG, or the bottom of the weekly FVG. [06:11]
- **Hourly order block** — "this was a previous hourly high... boom spike up, rip down", so the up-move into that high is the hourly OB. [08:23]
- **Stacked confluences** — NQ: hourly high + hourly imbalance + hourly OB + 15m OB in line with the imbalance = "four different confirmations... really really high confluence." [08:23]–[08:56]
- **ES vs NQ volatility** — "the S&P moves slower than NASDAQ"; a big S&P move down = a massive NQ move down; NQ may sweep a 4H high while ES only sweeps an hourly high. [06:43]–[07:17]
- **Bias correct, no entry** — price going straight to the draw without retracing to the marked zones is fine: "it just didn't provide us an entry, but it solidified... we knew where market was going." [07:49]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- **News filter:** JOLTS + ISM Manufacturing PMI + FOMC member + FOMC minutes = "I will not be trading due to all of this today." Still do the bias and a post-session recap to watch how price reacts to the levels. [00:32]–[01:42]; [05:04]–[05:35]
- ES (bearish): expect a "market open sweep of these highs" (the hourly high being set as the hourly candle closes bearish), then maybe a fill of a 15m imbalance or a tap of the 15m order block → short. Confluences in order: (1) hourly high, (2) 15m imbalance, (3) 15m order block. First draw = the 4H lows, then the daily lows; the hourly shows room down to an "area of liquidity". [02:14]–[03:56]
- Alternative: the 50% of the 4H imbalance above was not filled, and there is an order block higher, so news could push price up to sweep the higher highs. [02:50]; [05:35]
- NQ (bearish): "definitely just a sweep of these highs first and primarily"; zones = hourly high, hourly imbalance, hourly OB, 15m OB. Downside draws = the daily low, then the 4H lows, then a 4H imbalance. HTF upside draw = the big unfilled 4H imbalance (marked for later). [08:56]–[10:05]
- **Only pre-marked setups:** "I'm waiting for my setups that I mark out during my daily bias, and I'm not adding or adapting once market opens, because typically that's just low time frame stuff." [09:32]–[10:05]
- If price doesn't sweep the highs first: "it's fine, we're just going to the draws on liquidity... daily bias correct, execution just wasn't there." [09:32]

## Process / order of operations
1. Check ForexFactory for high-impact news → decide trade / no-trade. [00:32]
2. HTF draws (4H lows, daily lows; NQ weekly FVG 50%/bottom, prior weekly high). [02:14]; [06:11]
3. Mark the retracement confluences in the bias direction (hourly high, 15m/hourly imbalance, 15m/hourly OB). [03:23]–[03:56]; [08:23]
4. At the open: wait for a sweep of the marked high + reaction from a marked zone; otherwise no trade. [09:32]
5. Evening: market recap to compare. [05:35]
