## Summary
TJR's mid-2025 daily-bias method replaces higher-timeframe market-structure bias with "what did the previous session do?" relative to HTF draws on liquidity (1H/4H highs and lows, session highs and lows, previous-day highs and lows). There are three "daily profiles": (1) previous-session consolidation → new-session manipulation + reversal; (2) previous-session manipulation → new-session reversal; (3) previous-session manipulation + reversal → new-session continuation. Bias = the direction away from the manipulated draw, and targets = the draws on the other side. In a continuation profile you must still wait for a LTF (5m/15m) manipulation after the open, because new money must manipulate. He demonstrates live on a war-news day he didn't trade and on that week's Thursday, Wednesday (CPI) and Tuesday, on ES/NQ.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Daily bias** — derived from (a) the previous session's profile and (b) which HTF draws on liquidity were or weren't manipulated. Then "target the draws in the other direction" [08:58–10:06].
- **Previous session** — for NY, London; for London, Asia. "Applicable for any session" or instrument (forex, gold) [01:39–02:46].
- **Trade at market opens** — when volume, volatility and new money enter [02:12].
- **Daily profile 1: previous-session consolidation → new-session manipulation & reversal.** "Consolidation" doesn't have to mean sideways; it means **no manipulation of HTF draws on liquidity** during the previous session [03:19; 06:09–06:41].
- **Daily profile 2: previous-session manipulation → new-session reversal.** The previous session sweeps a draw but does not reverse; expect NY to reverse [03:53–04:25].
- **Daily profile 3: previous-session manipulation + reversal → new-session continuation.** London sweeps and reverses; expect NY to continue [05:02–05:34].
- **Manipulation** — a sweep of a HTF draw on liquidity. "I am only trying to trade off of manipulation ... We need manipulation in order to fill orders" [03:53].
- **HTF draws on liquidity** — 1H highs and lows, 4H highs and lows, session highs and lows (London, Asia), plus previous-day highs and lows [07:16–08:26].
- **One profile per session** — "Ideally we're only classifying one daily profile ... it's one per session" [20:17].
- **Continuation needs LTF manipulation** — in profile 3, new money at the NY open still must manipulate, but on the lower TFs. Mark **5m and 15m highs and lows** and wait for a sweep of them in the counter-direction before entering with the bias; never buy blindly at the open [21:22–23:38].
- **NY pre-market opens at 8:00** (ET) per this video [16:56]. (8QDDxwUV1KM says 8:30.)

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Before NY: mark London H/L, Asia H/L, 1H and 4H H/L and previous-day H/L; remove draws already taken [07:50–08:26; 16:20].
- Classify London as consolidation (no HTF draw swept), manipulation without reversal, or manipulation plus reversal [06:41].
- IF London consolidated, THEN expect NY to sweep a HTF draw at the open and reverse; bias = away from the swept side [12:57–14:02].
- IF London manipulated without reversing, THEN expect the NY reversal [04:25].
- IF London manipulated and reversed, THEN bias = continuation; wait for a 5m/15m sweep against the bias after the open, then trade with the bias. Ignore an NY sweep of London highs in the bias direction as a reversal signal [23:38–25:18].
- Targets: draws on liquidity in the other direction: 5m lows/highs, London/Asia session H/L, hourly H/L, 4H H/L, relative equal highs [14:02; 16:20; 18:39].
- Avoid trading high-impact news days: "I try and avoid trading on high impact news days" (CPI Wednesday) [16:56]. He did not trade a war-news day either [01:05].
- On news days the profile can play out in pre-market (8:30 release) [19:12].

## Process / order of operations
1. Identify the previous session (London for NY trading) [02:46].
2. Mark HTF draws on liquidity: 1H, 4H, session H/L and previous-day H/L [08:26].
3. Classify London's profile relative to those draws [08:26–08:58].
4. Derive the expected NY behavior: manipulation + reversal, reversal, or continuation, and the bias direction.
5. For continuation, mark 5m/15m opposing H/L and wait for them to be swept after the open [23:04].
6. Target the opposite-side draws [09:33].

## Changes vs. earlier teaching / contradictions
- **Timeline placement:** June 2025, the late Trading Transformation era. It **replaces the daily-structure bias (BC §3) and the 4H-trend bias (Trading Transformation)** with session-profile plus liquidity bias. It is the conceptual precursor of the Sept–Nov 2025 "2B" rule (xRuMUvW3T7Q, 8QDDxwUV1KM): when the HTF manipulation happened in the prior session, wait for a LTF (5m/15m) manipulation at the NY open [21:55–23:38].
- **News days: "I try and avoid trading on high impact news days" (CPI).** This confirms the BC news rule as of June 2025. By Sept 2025 (BMyeYQIj_vU) he traded a PPI day, so the rule may have loosened. Answers OPEN #10 partially. He also skipped a war-news day.

- **Draw hierarchy:** 1H/4H H/L, session H/L and previous-day H/L are HTF draws; 5m/15m H/L are LTF manipulation levels. This answers OPEN #5 (which liquidity counts for the ES sweep).
- **Entry time:** market open (9:30); the Tuesday continuation sweep happened "a little bit later in the day".
- **SMT:** observed only in passing. No IFVG or OB mention.
