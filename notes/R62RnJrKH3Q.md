## Summary
Day 2 of the 2026 Path to Profitability series. TJR defines trading not as making money but as the skill of predicting where price will go with high probability on a daily basis; money is a "side effect" of the skill. Approaching the chart with "I want to make money today" causes FOMO entries at the open, overleverage, too many trades and oversized risk. He prescribes demo trading while learning, journaling and analyzing both wins and losses, and judging yourself on win rate and risk-to-reward rather than daily P&L. He previews that later videos will cover journal statistics and the win rate / R:R needed to be profitable.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Day trading (definition)** — "having the ability to predict where price on any sort of asset class is going to go with a high probability on a daily basis." [06:37]–[07:10]
- **Reason-based confluences** — every confluence must have a "why" (e.g., orders getting filled or trend continuation). He contrasts this with support/resistance, where "you don't really know what's going on". [01:05]–[01:39]
- **Liquidity -> reversal logic (preview)** — taking out a sweep of liquidity / draw on liquidity = potential to fill orders -> possibility of trend reversal -> look for more confluences confirming the reversal -> then enter. [01:39]–[02:11]
- **Skill metrics** — win rate (how often you win vs lose) and risk-to-reward (amount risked vs won per trade); together they determine whether you are profitable/skilled. [20:32]–[21:07]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Do not enter at the market open out of FOMO; trades must be based on a strategy and price action. [05:31]–[06:04]
- Do not leverage the whole account on one trade. [05:31]
- After a liquidity sweep, do not enter immediately: look for additional confirming confluences that the trend is reversing, then enter. [02:11]

## Process / order of operations
1. Mark a liquidity sweep / draw on liquidity being taken. [01:39]
2. Treat it as potential order-fill -> possible reversal. [02:11]
3. Look for further confluences confirming reversal. [02:11]
4. Enter. [02:11]
