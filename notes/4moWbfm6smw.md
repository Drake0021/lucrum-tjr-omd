## Summary
Psychology day on overconfidence, prompted by a winning trade earlier that day. TJR explains why the first trade at market open is the highest-probability trade (price & time theory: new money enters, liquidity sweep, orders filled) and why trades after that are lower-probability, lower-RR retracements — hence one (max two) trades a day. He frames confidence as a narrow band between overconfidence (over-risking, over-leveraging, over-trading) and fear (missing entries, under-leveraging, hesitating), warns about demo-to-live overconfidence, says he does not trade news, and explicitly tells viewers not to trade the days Fed Chair Powell speaks (Wednesday and Thursday that week).

## Concepts & definitions
- **Price and time theory / market-open probability** — the beginning of market open is when his model is most probable, because new money comes into the market; there is usually a liquidity sweep and orders get filled to push price in its intended direction. After the first trade, volume dies out; you can't catch the top/bottom of the move, you catch retracements with lower RR and longer development. [03:48] [04:24] [04:57]
- **Overconfidence** — believing you'll win every trade / can take a trade whenever you want; leads to over-risking, over-leveraging, over-trading; a loss then flips you into fear. [07:44] [08:18] [08:51]
- **Under-confidence (fear)** — missing entries, under-leveraging, waiting too long to pull the trigger. [07:44] [08:18]
- **Revenge/"one trade to get out of the hole"** — searching for one trade to recover losses is worse than gambling; it's emotional, forces low-probability trades and forces your bias on a market that isn't primed, often as the 5th or 6th trade of the day with high leverage. [05:29] [06:02]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Take one to two trades a day max; one trade is best — the first trade at market open is highest probability. [04:24]
- After a winning trade, do not go back to the charts searching for another trade to grow profits. [03:16]
- Don't force your bias on the market when the market isn't primed for it. [06:02]
- Do not trade news; TJR states "I don't trade news". [15:46]
- Do not trade high-impact Fed (Powell) speech days — he told viewers not to trade the Wednesday and Thursday Powell was speaking, calling them likely the most volatile days. [16:19] [16:52] [18:31]
- If you are fearful in the market, that means go back to a demo account. [11:40]
- Once proven profitable, don't change what got you there (risk management, no over-leverage, no over-trading). [10:31] [11:04]

- Development path: learn concepts (not trading yet) → master yourself on demo with one trade a day, track win rate → prove profitability over a solid course of time → go live without increasing leverage. [00:33] [11:40] [12:12]
