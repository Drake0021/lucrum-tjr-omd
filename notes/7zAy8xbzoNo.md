## Summary
A no-chart outline of how to build a personal day-trading strategy, illustrated with TJR's own answers. The five components are: (1) **when**: the session; (2) **what type of trader**: scalper, intraday or swing; (3) **timeframes**: three for bias, three below for execution; (4) **confluences**: pick three you can spot and execute well; (5) **stop loss and take profit**: price-action based or fixed units. TJR's own answers (late Nov 2023): NYSE open only, S&P and NASDAQ only, intraday; bias from daily/4H/1H, execution on 15m/5m/1m; confluences = liquidity sweep → BOS → order block or FVG. Stop at the invalidation point and TPs at price-action levels, even if TP1 is below 1:1.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Strategy vs trading plan** — the strategy = "when and where are we entering and what are our confluences"; the trading plan also includes session, number of trades and risk. [04:29]–[05:00]
- **Trader types** — scalper: in and out in 1–5 minutes; intraday (TJR): in and out within 15 min to 4 h, "sometimes you leave runners for 5 hours", but "most of the time you're closing your entire position within every single day"; swing: daily/weekly/monthly. [08:21]–[09:27]
- **Timeframe ratio** — bias from three timeframes, execution on the three below. TJR: bias = daily, 4H, 1H; execution = 15m, 5m, 1m. Scalper: bias 1H/15m/5m, execute 1m. Swing: bias monthly/weekly/daily, execute 4H/1H. [10:01]–[12:12]
- **Execution-TF selection by POI timeframe** — "if daily is bullish, 4H bullish, hourly bullish, I find a 15-minute building block, I'm going to probably execute on the 5 minute or the 1 minute; if we see a 4-hour building block and then hourly confirmation, I'm probably going to execute on the 15 minute." [11:07]–[11:40]
- **Invalidation-based stop** — "underneath a point where I know if we get past that price... my whole trade idea is invalidated... likely price is going to go in the opposite direction." [17:45]–[18:17]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Session: TJR trades only the NYSE open; "nowadays I really only trade the S&P and NASDAQ", which move most in the NY session, so there is no reason to trade Asia/London. (Earlier, with GBPJPY, he traded London only.) [05:35]–[06:09]
- Bias: daily + 4H + 1H all aligned → find a building block → execute on the lower TFs. [11:07]–[11:40]
- POI timeframe → execution TF: a 15m building block → execute 5m/1m; a 4H building block + hourly confirmation → execute 15m. [11:40]
- Confluences (TJR): "liquidity sweep, break of structure, order block or fair value gap." [15:29]
- Stop: price-action based (invalidation). [17:45]–[18:17]
- TPs: price-action based; R:R varies: "sometimes it's like a 1 to 0.7 RR for my first take profit... sometimes a 1 to 3"; last week one trade hit every TP with the final at ~1:7. [18:17]–[18:51]
- Use only confluences you can both spot and execute; "ideally you find three that work for you." [13:19]–[13:50]

## Process / order of operations
1. Choose the session (when the instrument moves most and you're available). [05:00]–[07:48]
2. Choose the trader type. [08:21]
3. Choose bias TFs and execution TFs (three + three). [10:01]–[12:45]
4. Choose three confluences. [12:45]–[16:02]
5. Define stop and TP method (price action vs fixed units). [16:39]–[19:25]

## Confluences mentioned
- Liquidity sweep, BOS, order block, FVG — his core three (sweep → BOS → OB or FVG). [15:29]
- Equilibrium, "a high gets breached then BOS", support/resistance, indicators: acceptable for *others* if they work for them, but he dislikes indicators. [14:25]–[14:58]

## Risk & psychology rules
- Trading plan includes the number of trades and the risk (not detailed here). [05:00]
- Fixed-point stops/TPs suit traders who get emotional or move stops; TJR uses price action. [19:25]

## Changes vs. earlier teaching / contradictions
- **Timeline:** Nov 2023, after BC2. **Answers the 2024 TT precursor:** the "4H building block + hourly confirmation → execute 15m; 15m block → 5m/1m" mapping is an early form of the 2024 rule (1H picks the execution TF). Also confirms the instrument switch to **S&P + NASDAQ only**.
- TP1 can be < 1:1 (1:0.7) because TPs are at price-action levels. Compare the framework's "TP1 ≈ 1:1" (§5.2): TP placement is by level, not by fixed R.
- Runners can be held up to ~5 h (vs the 90-min entry window; exits can be later). Relevant to OPEN §12.12.
- Confluence trio "sweep → BOS → OB or FVG" = the BC1/BC2 model; order blocks are still in use in Nov 2023.
