## Summary
A short conceptual intro to liquidity, with no chart identification yet (that comes in part 2). Liquidity = resting orders (buy/sell orders, limits, stop losses, take profits). It exists on every timeframe and is required for the market to move, change direction, break structure, start or continue a trend. It matters because it determines where price is drawn, "like a magnet". The trader's only goal is predicting where price wants to go.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Liquidity** — "just resting orders ... buy orders ... sell orders ... buy limits ... sell limits ... stop losses ... take profits." [04:02–05:08]
- **Present on every timeframe.** [05:08–05:42]
- **Required for movement** — Needed for the market "to move slash change direction slash break structure slash start a new trend slash continue a trend ... if we can't fill orders then the market can't move." [06:15–06:49]
- **Draw / magnet** — Liquidity "determines where the market is drawn towards ... used as like a magnet." [07:21–08:30]
- **Trader's goal** — "figure out and be able to accurately predict where price wants to go." Liquidity should therefore get "all of our attention". [08:30–09:04]
- He hints that liquidity is something "you guys already know how to find" from an earlier concept (i.e. highs and lows, inferred; confirmed in pt. 2). [05:42–06:15]
