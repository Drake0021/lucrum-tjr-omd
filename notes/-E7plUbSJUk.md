## Summary
Chart day for equilibrium (EQ). TJR shows his tool settings (Gann box or Fibonacci with only 0, 0.5, 1 levels) and how to anchor it: in an uptrend from the swing low to the latest swing high, in a downtrend from the swing high to the latest swing low, re-dragging the anchor whenever a new high/low forms before EQ is reached. EQ is only a zone filter — he waits for price in discount/premium plus an FVG/imbalance or order block, then scales down for a BOS entry, targeting previous liquidity. Examples include forex pairs, a monthly gold uptrend, and a Discord daily-EQ swing short (4 TPs, 3 hit). He drops a planned EQ pt.3 and announces the next videos: trading plan, then a 3-part "putting everything together" series.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Equilibrium (summary)** — a retracement tool measuring premium and discount for smart money to enter; 50% mark of the range. [00:35] [01:10]
- **Premium / discount (uptrend)** — measure from the low to the new high; everything above 0.5 = premium, from 0.5 down to 0 (the low) = discount. Smart money buys in discount, not premium. [01:45] [02:18]
  - Caption note: he says "smart money never shorts in a premium ... they always buy and they always short in a discount" — a slip of the tongue; the intended standard meaning is buy in discount, short in premium. (inferred, consistent with Day 26 and with his downtrend explanation at [02:51])
- **Premium (downtrend)** — measure from the high down to the new low; the area above 50% is where smart money looks to short ("when price gets on sale" for shorts). [02:51]
- **Tool settings** — Gann box (or Fibonacci) with only levels 0, 0.5 and 1; remove all other levels. [03:24] [03:57] [04:30]
- **Anchoring / re-anchoring** — whenever price makes a new high in an uptrend, drag the tool from the low to that high; if price doesn't retrace to EQ and makes a new high (or new low in a downtrend), drag the anchor to the new extreme. [06:46] [07:53] [09:01] [18:02] [18:37]
- **Why fib 50%/~62% retracements work** — "because it's equilibrium". (He says "64 mark", presumably the 0.618/0.62 level — inferred.) [07:19] [07:53]
- **Works on every timeframe** — monthly to 15m. [07:19] [12:55]
- **Accumulation as OB** — a consolidation where orders were filled before a move can be considered an order block. [10:08]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Do not take a trade purely off equilibrium; combine EQ with an FVG or order block in the discount/premium zone, then wait for a BOS for confirmation. [02:51] [03:24]
- EQ + only a BOS (no FVG/OB) is "not much confirmation" to him, though it would have worked in the example. [07:19]
  - Note: Day 26 said "even just a break of structure while in a discount — that's enough reason for me to get in"; here he calls EQ + BOS alone "not much confirmation". Treat EQ + FVG/OB + BOS as his preferred standard. (inferred)
- Entry: when price is in EQ (discount for longs / premium for shorts) and in an FVG/OB, scale down to a lower timeframe and enter on the BOS; stop beyond the swing that formed the BOS ("stop under here"); targets at previous areas of liquidity. [06:13] [08:26] [10:42] [12:21]
- If no good entry on the 1H BOS (bad RR), wait for price to come back into equilibrium, then take the LTF (15m) BOS. [10:08] [10:42]
- Trade with the trend of the higher timeframe being played ("playing what the market was telling me"): e.g. daily BOS down while weekly was technically in a downtrend → short from daily premium. [15:10]
- HTF EQ play: when monthly/daily EQ is reached and there's an FVG/OB, scale to 4H (or 1H) and wait for a BOS/confirmation. [13:27] [15:42]

## Process / order of operations
1. Identify trend and latest swing (low → high for uptrend; high → low for downtrend). [18:02]
2. Draw Gann box/fib (0, 0.5, 1). Re-anchor if a new extreme forms before EQ is reached. [06:46]
3. Wait for price to retrace beyond 0.5 into discount (longs) / premium (shorts). [02:18]
4. Look for confluence inside the zone: FVG/imbalance, order block, liquidity taken. [05:40] [11:16]
5. Scale down (4H → 1H → 15m) and wait for a BOS in trade direction. [08:26] [10:42]
6. Enter on BOS candle; stop beyond the BOS swing; TPs at previous liquidity (multiple). [12:21] [16:15]
