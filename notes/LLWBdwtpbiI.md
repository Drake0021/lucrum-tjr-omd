## Summary
A no-trade NY-open stream (late March 2026), co-hosted with a student/mentee who is on a "30 days" program and takes his own trade. TJR explains that manipulation can occur in pre-market or London, so a pre-open sweep can be "our high time frame sweep for the day". He explains the "fake trend after taking HTF draws" trap and describes a 1m scalp variant of the same model. He then works through the dilemma of entering on a 1m gap during a fast sell-off versus waiting for a 5m retrace, and chooses to wait. He identified a short he would have taken on NQ (a sweep with SMT near stacked lows), but the student's interruption kept him from entering. ES then took out all the lows. He passes on later setups because "most of the objectives are taken for the day." The student hits his TP at London lows.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Manipulation can be pre-market or London** — "market can have manipulation during premarket. We can even take trades off of London session manipulation. So, this in my opinion looks like our high time frame sweep for the day" [00:33–01:08]. Model: manipulation first, then "continuation of order flow" [00:01].
- **More bearish index** — he calls NQ "our more bearish index" when it confirms a bullish BOS (i.e. the confirmation that matters is the weaker index flipping) [01:08].
- **15m example of the model** — sweep, 15m BOS down, retrace that respected a gap, then a fill of equilibrium and a 5m BOS up = "long long long" [01:08–01:43].
- **Fake trend after HTF draws (trap)** — "we'll break structure to the downside. And then in that move down, we're also taking out high time frame draws on liquidity ... we'll convince people that we're continuing the new trend ... But that move down [was a] retrace for us to move higher" [02:48–03:58]. So after a BOS that also takes HTF draws, expect a 5m retrace, and require "hella confirmations" at the retrace [02:48; 03:58].
- **Low-resistance draws / stacked lows** — clustered equal lows (London, Asia, hourly, previous-day equal lows) are "free money"/"good draws" [00:33; 18:20; 21:48].
- **1m scalp variant (same strategy on lower TFs)** — after the HTF sweep, while the 5m is still bullish, a 1m IFVG signals the coming 5m retrace. Short the 1m fill/EQ and target the 5m confluence (gap) the retrace will reach, not the main draw. TP1/TP2/final under it [07:57–10:14]. He presents this as what "some people do"; "I've done this before too."
- **Completing an SMT** — if there was a bearish SMT off the highs, the lows must be taken out "to complete the SMT" [18:20].
- **"Market experience"** — near stacked draws after a big sell-off, price often doesn't give a full 5m retrace; it "grab[s] quick orders" (a sweep + SMT) and then sends [28:33–29:39].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Don't trade the first minutes: "we're still in the manipulation time frame. It's still 7 minutes into market open. It's a little bit early to be placing trades" [01:43–02:16].
- Long requires a 5m retrace into a 5m bullish confluence plus confirmations. None had appeared, so no long [07:57].
- Want both ES and NQ to invert the gap (change in order flow on both) [11:57–12:29].
- He expects more manipulation after the inversion: "once we inverse this gap, we're probably going to get a move up ... 1-minute break to the upside cuz it's going to want to get people into the longs." So after the inversion he waits for a 5m retrace up and a change in order flow back down, and then targets the lows [11:57–12:29; 14:29].
- Short opportunity rule: if NQ fails to take session highs (that ES took) and breaks structure down or inverts 5m gaps → "very obvious short", targets London lows, Asia lows, hourly lows [04:31].
- **Gamble vs wait:** entering on a 1m gap mid sell-off ("10 straight minutes of down candles", 183 ticks) risks a "deep heavy retrace". "Waiting is the safer route ... you give yourself the opportunity to miss the trade, but it's a lot safer" [15:04–21:13].
- Micros option: dropping to micros (MES/MNQ) allows "a much wider stop" while still capturing the lows. He suggests it to the student as the "play it safe" option [19:29–20:02].
- A retrace that only sweeps nearby highs and rotates down is "good enough" for him [20:34].
- 9:56 ET = "pretty good" timing for the 5m retrace [21:48].
- Stand-down: after ES takes out all the lows with the 4H still bullish, a new short risks a bullish SMT toward NQ's untouched highs. "We took out draws to the upside and to the downside. Most of the objectives are taken for the day" → no trade [29:39–30:47].

## Process / order of operations
1. Identify the day's manipulation (pre-market/London sweep of an hourly low = the HTF sweep) [00:33–01:08].
2. Confirm the BOS on the more bearish/bullish index (both indexes) [01:08].
3. Wait out the first ~10 minutes (manipulation window) [02:16].
4. Wait for the 5m retrace into a confluence and an LTF change in order flow (IFVG/BOS) on both indexes [07:57; 12:29].
5. Target stacked session/hourly lows or highs [04:31; 18:20].
6. If both sides' draws are taken, stop for the day [30:47].
