## Summary
A psychology and discipline video listing seven non-strategy rules: (1) never trade without a plan, and follow it; (2) size up after consistency, not after profit, which means multiple quarters of stable results and stepping back down if emotions creep in; (3) use one strategy only; (4) don't trade when feeling off (travel, emotional events); (5) no FOMO, because a daily bias that played out without you is still a win; (6) journal every trade with a pre-trade and post-trade recap; (7) how you live is how you trade. The only bot-relevant items are the sizing rule, no-trade days and the trade-frequency attitude.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Trading plan** — trading plan + risk management plan + psychology plan. Trading against it destroys probability [01:05–03:15].
- **Size after consistency** — keep risking the same amount even after the account grows. For example, a $10k account up $1k (+10%) over 2–3 weeks: keep the risk based on $10k. Consider sizing up only after about two quarters (6 months) of consistent profitable trading and account growth of around 30–40%, when risking too little becomes a disservice. If larger size triggers emotional mistakes, revert to the original risk [04:56–07:43].
- **A daily bias that played out = a win** — predicting the market correctly counts even without a trade [16:58–17:31].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- If the confluences don't line up, don't enter; if the bias plays out without you, don't chase [17:31–18:04].
- "A day out of the market is better than a day in the market statistically" without a setup [17:31].
- If the plan gives no trade, "just leave, bro. Be done." [19:11]
- Don't trade while traveling, on a plane, or when not "completely locked in" at the desk. Trades he placed on a plane were stopped out [10:57–11:30].
- Don't trade in an emotionally unstable state (breakups, bereavement); take days off [11:30–12:36].

## Risk & psychology rules
- Follow your written trading, risk and psychology plan every day [01:05–03:15].
- Don't increase risk after a big win day. The cautionary tale: $10k to $112k by tripling size after wins, then all of it lost the next day [03:49–04:56].
- Fixed risk amount; reassess size only after about 6 months of consistency and +30–40% account growth; drop back if emotions appear [05:30–07:10].
- One strategy that makes sense to you and fits your schedule; don't judge it on one day or one loss [08:15–10:25].
- No trading when feeling off, traveling or emotionally compromised [10:57–13:08].
- No FOMO or chasing; be comfortable with zero-trade days and weeks. "The best traders in the entire world are not taking trades on a daily basis"; taking multiple trades every day "is not okay at all" [18:37].
- Journal: long/short, risk, confluences used, a post-trade recap of what went right and wrong; track the data to raise your win rate [19:11–20:50].
- Discipline and consistency in life carry over to trading; "get 1% better every day" [21:23–24:45].

## Changes vs. earlier teaching / contradictions
- **Timeline placement:** July 2025 (Trading Transformation era), a psychology video. It confirms the BC psychology rules (§9): no FOMO, journaling, don't trade emotional.
- **Trade frequency:** "taking multiple trades every single day ... is not okay" and one trade a week is fine. This supports a low frequency (≤1–2/day) and is consistent with BC §11 #8 [18:37].
- **Sizing:** a fixed risk amount, not re-computed from equity; resize only after about 6 months. This adds to §6 (it is a new, explicit rule) and is relevant to the "fixed contract sizing" in 2026 [05:30–06:37].
