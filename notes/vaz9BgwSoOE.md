## Concepts & definitions
- **4H BOS verification** — he checks the swing high (4717) vs the 4H candle close (4719): "so we did get a closure above those highs." The BOS requires a body close above the high. [01:40]–[02:15]
- **Sweep + HTF BOS = bias flip for a retrace** — "we went down and took out this 4-hour low, but then we came through and made a 4-hour break of structure to the upside... that's like, all right, we're getting a bit of bullish confirmation." [01:07]–[02:15]
- **Daily retrace** — after three bearish daily candles, the 4H sweep+BOS suggests "a daily retrace"; targets = 4H imbalances, intermediate hourly highs, then the daily EQ (from the daily highs to the lows), which sits just above the previous daily high. [03:24]–[05:08]
- **ES moves slower than NQ** — NQ "very well could just give us this big black candle to the downside and then make the same movement, if not more, to the upside." [06:49]–[07:22]
