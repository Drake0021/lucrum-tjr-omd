## Summary
A short interactive recap of his ES short on Thursday June 6, 2024 (+$6,000 in under an hour). Bias: price was "significantly overbought" after Wednesday and had swept daily highs, so he expects a move down to the EQ of the range (range lows to range highs) → bearish. Execution: at the NY open ES swept 1H highs; SMT (ES made a higher high, NQ a lower high); on the 1m, a bullish FVG was invalidated (IFVG = confirmation), then price filled a bearish FVG with a reaction (third confluence) → short. Stop above the high; one contract off at 15m lows (1:2); the rest ran to the HTF lows, which NQ had already taken. It is a complete trade example of the 2024 model.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Overbought → EQ** — after a strong up-move, expect price to seek the EQ of the range (range lows → range highs), ignoring older price action outside the range. [00:35]–[01:09]
- **Daily highs swept** — "more of a confluence for at least some profits to be taken off of these daily highs". [00:35]
- **Confirmation vs third confluence** — on the 1m he needs "a confirmation" (BOS or IFVG) and "a third confluence" (e.g. an FVG fill with a reaction). [02:51]
- **SMT reading** — ES high then higher high; NQ high then lower high → "NQ, because it has SMT divergence, is pretty much telling us hey we are going to go lower". He then executes on the S&P (ES), the index that made the higher high. [02:16]
