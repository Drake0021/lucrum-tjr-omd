## Summary
After a ~9-minute comedy skit (no trading content, [00:04]–[08:45]), TJR gives his time windows for trading NY session (ES/NQ, inferred). AM "kill zone"/macro: 9:50–10:10 ET, because the 1H and 4H candles close at 10:00; avoid pre-market (8:30–9:30) and generally don't execute before 9:50 ("never take a trade before 10 am" — he says "10 pm", a slip). From 9:30 to ~9:50 price usually runs toward a draw on liquidity or consolidates. PM window: 13:50–14:10 ET. Windows are guidelines, not strict rules. Multiple replay examples show sweep → BOS → FVG/OB fill entries in those windows.

## Concepts & definitions
- **AM kill zone / "macro"** — 9:50–10:10 ET; "Elite setups"; time to look for liquidity sweep or entry. [10:30][11:06]
- **Why 9:50–10:10** — The 4H candle (and 1H) closes at 10:00; "a bunch of candle closures that can cause a lot of movements." [11:06]
- **Pre-market** — 8:30 ET (pre-market open, news) to 9:30; "almost always in your best interest to not trade during pre-market." [09:58]
- **9:30–9:50 behavior** — For the first 20–30 minutes price is usually going toward the draw on liquidity / a confluence, or consolidating until the 1H/4H close. [14:01][21:42]
- **PM kill zone** — 13:50–14:10 ET (PM session technically starts 13:00; lunch hour before). [14:33][15:13]
- **Third confluence** — In his sequence: liquidity sweep → BOS → "third confluence" (FVG/OB) filled for entry. [13:26][16:56]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Do not trade pre-market (8:30–9:30 ET). [09:58]
- "Never really try and look to execute before 9:50"; he still holds "never take a trade before 10 [am]" as a preference. [09:21][11:40]
- Look for sweep/entry 9:50–10:10 ET. [11:06]
- PM session: look for sweep/entry 13:50–14:10 ET. [15:13]
- Windows are not strict ("not something that's supposed to be super strict like I can only enter within these time frames"); setups may come just after (e.g., sweep at ~10:25). [11:40][15:47]
- He does not follow ICT kill zones/macros "to a T". [21:08]
- PM moves often continue the trend started in AM. [18:12]

## Process / order of operations
1. 8:30 news/pre-market: observe, don't trade; note sweeps/BOS in pre-market. [09:58][18:12]
2. 9:30 open: let price run toward draw on liquidity/consolidate. [14:01]
3. 9:50–10:10: look for liquidity sweep → BOS → FVG/OB fill entry. [13:26]
4. PM: 13:50–14:10 same sequence. [15:47]

## Confluences mentioned
- Time of day (9:50–10:10; 13:50–14:10) — used as filter/expectation, not strict. [11:06][16:19]
- Liquidity sweep, BOS, FVG ("imbalance"), order block — the entry sequence. [13:26][16:56][20:35]
