## Summary
TJR presents his Sept 2025 strategy as a checklist. Mark key levels (1H/4H H/L, session H/L, and "data highs/lows" from news candles); wait for one to be hit at or after the 9:30 open; on the 5m, look for a confirmation confluence (BOS, IFVG, 79% extension close, SMT); then a 5m continuation confluence, which here still includes **order blocks and breaker blocks** as well as EQ and FVG; then drop to the 1m for a confirmation confluence entry, with targets at opposing key levels. He shows two NQ/ES examples, both SMT-driven with EQ continuations: a short "yesterday" and a long "today" off a PPI data low. He finishes with the trading objective ("predict where price wants to go with high probability"), demo practice and journaling.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Objective of trading** — "We're trying to accurately predict where price wants to go with a high probability on a day-to-day basis." Money is the side effect; detach emotionally from P&L [03:22–05:01].
- **Key levels** — 1H and 4H highs and lows, session highs and lows (London, Asia), and **data highs/lows**: the high and low of a news-release candle, used "very rare ... when we have news data" [06:40; 21:42–22:15].
- **Why key levels work** — retail buys above highs and sells below lows, letting institutions fill opposite orders and reverse price. "Institutional money comes into the market every single day at market open", so at 9:30 ET look for manipulation, either at the open or from a previous session followed by a LTF manipulation [07:13–08:19].
- **Daily bias** — established from the key levels: whichever level gets hit and manipulated sets the direction. "In order to understand our daily bias, we need to be able to identify our key levels first" [05:34; 06:07; 08:19].
- **Confirmation confluences (5m)** — BOS, IFVG, 79% extension closure on the Fibonacci extension; SMT is added on the list [09:25; 11:06]. On the 1m: BOS, IFVG, SMT, 79% ext [12:44–13:18].
- **Continuation confluences (5m)** — **order blocks, breaker blocks, equilibrium, fair value gaps** [10:33–11:06].
- **Why continuation** — executing purely off the confirmation can fake you out and gives bad entries, so wait for a retrace that continues the trend [09:59].
- **1m during the 5m retrace** — the 5m retrace leg usually flips the 1m trend against you ("a mini uptrend"). Wait for the 1m to change back [12:11–12:44; 18:20].
- **SMT** — the index that sweeps a key level vs the one that doesn't. "If one of the indexes takes out a sweep draw on liquidity, we're looking for entries in either direction" because an SMT may form. He calls it "one of our strongest confirmation confluences" [22:47–23:20].
- **EQ when no gap or block exists** — take the 5m range from the swept high to the low that formed (or the low to the high) and wait for a 5m retrace candle into 50% [17:48–18:20; 23:53].

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Mark key levels before the NY open; adjust them if pre-market takes one out [14:25–15:32].
- Wait for a key level to be hit (at or after 9:30), then scale to the 5m [08:19–08:52; 16:06].
- Require a 5m confirmation confluence (any one of BOS, IFVG, 79% ext close, SMT) [09:25; 16:06–17:14].
- Then require a 5m continuation confluence (OB, breaker, EQ, FVG) before the entry [10:33–11:06].
- When it is hit, scale to the 1m and enter on a 1m confirmation confluence (BOS, IFVG, SMT, 79% ext) [12:44–13:18].
- Stop: above the liquidity sweep high or "above these highs" (the retrace highs) [19:29].
- Targets: "previous key levels marked in the opposite direction": 1H lows, 4H lows, session lows, data lows, pre-market lows [13:18–13:52; 20:01].
- Management: partials at TP1, the rest stopped at BE [24:59].
- Watch both NQ and ES constantly; a sweep on either index counts [22:47].

## Process / order of operations
1. Mark 1H/4H H/L, session H/L, and data H/L (if there was news) before 9:30.
2. Wait for a level to be interacted with ("manipulation").
3. On the 5m: a confirmation confluence.
4. On the 5m: a continuation confluence (OB/breaker/EQ/FVG).
5. On the 1m: a confirmation confluence, then enter.
6. Exit at the opposite key levels; partials, then BE.

## Risk & psychology rules
- Avoid strategy-hopping and "overeducating"; stick to one strategy, plan, risk management and psychology [01:42–02:48].
- Practice on demo/paper first; a new strategy is a new skill that needs repetitions [26:03–27:41].
- Journal every trade: the pre-trade thought process, why you took it, a post-trade recap, emotions, early entry, plan adherence. "Make mistakes, but never make them again" [27:41–29:55].
- Not a 100% win rate; consistent practice gives probability [25:31].
- Results: $156,943 in August 2025; +$56,375 month-to-date in September [00:00–00:37].
