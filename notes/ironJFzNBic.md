## Summary
TJR shows how he forms daily bias using HTF (4H/1H) trend, draws on liquidity, and continuation/confirmation confluences, via four recent ES examples (CPI day, a Monday, a Friday, and a later day). Bias = direction of the HTF order flow toward the nearest significant draws on liquidity, judged by whether price RESPECTS the HTF continuation confluence (FVG/equilibrium) it is retracing into, or DISRESPECTS it (IFVG/BOS → flip bias). Coming into NY open he expects manipulation (a sweep of LTF/HTF lows/highs) before the move to the draw. He frames market movement as external→internal→external: trends move from swing extreme (liquidity) to continuation confluence (FVG/EQ) and back to the next extreme.

## Concepts & definitions
- **Daily bias** — where the market wants to go that day; required before looking for entries. [00:00]
- **Draws on liquidity as entries and targets** — stacked "low resistance" lows/highs give both targets and entry (sweep) points. [01:09] [01:44]
- **Bias via HTF continuation confluence** — if HTF trend is down and price retraces into an hourly FVG / equilibrium, expect it to be respected and price to draw to the HTF lows; if the FVG is instead closed through (inversed), bias flips. [02:21] [02:56] [08:30]
- **Order-flow logic of sweeps** — price pushes under lows to fill buy orders against resting sell orders, then must travel to where resting buy orders are (highs) to liquidate those positions; hence a sweep of lows targets highs. [05:12] [05:45] [12:57]
- **Lower low that sweeps a low without BOS** — still uptrend; a lower low that sweeps a prior low but doesn't break structure isn't a downtrend. [04:39]
- **Stacked FVGs in LTF uptrend** — closing below one gap doesn't invalidate the uptrend if another gap below holds; prefer the gap under equilibrium (discount) for longs. [14:41] [15:15]
- **External ↔ internal range flow** — uptrend: high (external) → retrace into FVG/equilibrium (internal) → new high → ...; downtrend mirror. "That's the ebbs and flows of the market." [24:51] [25:24] [25:58]
- **Bias checklist** — "where was our most recent liquidity sweep or are we currently sweeping liquidity... what are our high time frame imbalances and equilibrium levels... within this current trend". [26:31]
- **Upside draws named** — Asia session high, previous day high as significant draws. [29:58]

## Rules (model / entry / stop / target / management / filters / risk)
- Determine HTF (4H/1H) trend by structure; identify the HTF continuation confluence price is in (FVG/EQ); bias = trend continuation toward HTF draws if respected; flip if disrespected (candle close through = IFVG, or BOS). [02:21] [08:30] [19:21] [21:31]
- A respected 4H FVG is confirmed by a 1H BOS in the trend direction out of it. [19:52]
- Expect NY-open manipulation; if no manipulation yet, wait for a 5-min or hourly sweep of nearby lows (for bullish bias) before entering. [10:43] [11:49]
- Entry sequence (preview): LTF (5-min) sweep of lows → 5-min BOS up → retrace into 5-min FVG/equilibrium (discount) → long, target HTF highs/draws. Stops under the candle/swing low. Multiple take profits ("first take profit, second take profit"). [07:25] [13:30] [16:22] [16:55]
- Don't set a far HTF high as the one and only take profit; use intermediate lower-timeframe highs as TP points. [20:58]
- Bias can be conditional: if the gap holds → target highs; if inversed → target lows even with bullish HTF bias; reassess at each key level. [20:58] [22:04] [22:38]
- After an hourly BOS up invalidates a bearish setup, ignore the old bearish FVG ("I no longer give a [care] about this fair value gap"). [28:18]
- Be patient; wait for candle closes before concluding. [11:16] [21:31]

## Process / order of operations
1. Identify HTF trend (4H, 1H) by HH/HL or LH/LL and BOS. [00:35] [09:35]
2. Mark HTF draws on liquidity (session highs/lows, previous day high/low, stacked lows/highs). [01:09] [29:58]
3. Identify current HTF continuation confluence (FVG, EQ) or recent liquidity sweep. [26:31]
4. Watch for respect (continuation; LTF BOS in trend direction) or disrespect (IFVG/BOS against) → set bias toward the corresponding draw. [08:30] [19:52]
5. At NY open look for manipulation; then LTF (5-min) confirmation and continuation entry toward the draw. [10:43] [16:22] [30:30]
